解:设概率密度函数为 $f(x) = \begin{cases} \frac{A}{(10 + x)^2}, & x \in (0, 40), \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$，则由 $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x) dx = \int_0^{40} \frac{A}{(10 + x)^2} dx = \left.\frac{-A}{10 + x}\right|_0^{40} = A\left(\frac{1}{10} - \frac{1}{50}\right) = \frac{2}{25} A = 1$ 得 $A = \frac{25}{2}$，即 $f(x) = \begin{cases} \frac{25}{2(10 + x)^2}, & x \in (0, 40) \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$ 因此，$P(X < 5) = \int_0^5 \frac{25}{2(10 + x)^2} dx = -\frac{25}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{10 + x}\bigg|_0^5 = \frac{25}{2} (\frac{1}{10} - \frac{1}{15}) = \frac{5}{12}$.
