解:显然，有 $EX = 0, \quad DX = 3, \quad EY = \frac{3}{2}, \quad DY = \frac{3}{4}$，故由

$D(X - Y) = DX + DY - 2\operatorname{cov}(X,Y) = 3 + \frac{3}{4} - 2\operatorname{cov}(X,Y) = 3$.

得 $\mathbf{cov}(X,Y) = \frac{3}{8}$，于是 $\rho_{X,Y} = \frac{\mathbf{cov}(X,Y)}{\sqrt{DX}\sqrt{DY}} = \frac{\frac{3}{8}}{\sqrt{3} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{3}{4}}} = \frac{1}{4}$. 故选（A）.
