解:由已知 $\mathbf{E}X^{2} = \sigma^{2} + \mu^{2}$。假设统计量 $D$ 是总体方差 $\sigma^{2}$ 的无偏估计量，则 $\mathbf{E}D = k\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\mathbf{E}\left(X_{i+1} - X_i\right)^2 = k\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\mathbf{E}\left(X_{i+1}^2 + X_i^2 - 2X_iX_{i+1}\right)$ $= k\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\left(2\sigma^2 + 2\mu^2 - 2\mu^2\right) = 2k(n-1)\sigma^2 = \sigma^2$。故 $k = \frac{1}{2(n-1)}$。
