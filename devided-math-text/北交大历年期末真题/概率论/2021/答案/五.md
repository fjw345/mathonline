解:设 $X_{k}$ 表示第 $k$ 台分机请求外部通信信道（$k = 1,2,\dots,260$）.
则 $X_{k} \sim B(1,0.04)$，因此，$E(X_{k}) = 0.04$，$D(X_{k}) = 0.0384$，而 $X = \sum_{k=1}^{260} X_{k}$，由独立分布的中心极限定理，随机变量
$\frac{X - 260 \times 0.04}{\sqrt{260} \times \sqrt{0.0384}} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{260} X_{k} - 260 \times 0.04}{\sqrt{260} \times \sqrt{0.0384}}$ --4分
则 $P(X \leq n) = P(\frac{X - 260 \times 0.04}{\sqrt{260} \times \sqrt{0.0384}} \leq \frac{n - 260 \times 0.04}{\sqrt{260} \times \sqrt{0.0384}})$ $= \Phi(\frac{n - 260 \times 0.04}{\sqrt{260} \times \sqrt{0.0384}}) \geq 0.95.$ --8分
即 $\frac{n - 260 \times 0.04}{\sqrt{260} \times \sqrt{0.0384}} \geq 1.645$，解，$n \geq 15.5978$，即 $n \geq 16.$ --10分
