解:构造似然函数 $L(\theta) = \left(\frac{1 - \theta}{2}\right)^3\left(\frac{1 + \theta}{4}\right)^5 = \frac{(1 - \theta)^3(1 + \theta)^5}{2^{13}}$ 则 $\ln L(\theta) = 3\ln (1 - \theta) + 5\ln (1 + \theta) - 13\ln 2$ ，令 $\frac{d\ln L(\theta)}{d\theta} = \frac{3}{\theta - 1} +\frac{5}{\theta + 1} = \frac{8\theta - 2}{(\theta - 1)(\theta + 1)} = 0$ ，得 $\hat{\pmb{\theta}} = \frac{1}{4}$
