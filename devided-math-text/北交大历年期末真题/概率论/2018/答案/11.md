解:（1）由 $\int_{\mu}^{+\infty}\frac{A}{\sigma} e^{\frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}dx = A\sqrt{2}\int_{\mu}^{+\infty}e^{\frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}d\frac{x - \mu}{\sqrt{2}\sigma} = \frac{A\sqrt{2}\pi}{2} = 1$ ，可得 $A = \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}$ （2）设 $x_{1},\dots ,x_{n}$ 为样本值，似然函数为$L(\sigma^2) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} \frac{1}{\sigma^n}\bigg(\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\bigg)^n e^{-\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(x_i - \mu)^2}, & x_1,\dots ,x_n > \mu \\ 0 & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$ 当 $x_{1},\dots ,x_{n} > \mu$ 时， $\ln L(\sigma^2) = \frac{n}{2} (\ln 2 - \ln \pi) - \frac{n}{2}\ln (\sigma^2) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(x_i - \mu)^2$ ，令 $\frac{\mathrm{d}(\ln L(\sigma^2))}{d\sigma} = -\frac{n}{2}\frac{1}{\sigma^2} +\frac{1}{2(\sigma^2)^2}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(x_i - \mu)^2 = 0$ ，可得 $\sigma^2 = \frac{\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(x_i - \mu)^2}{n}$ 故 $\sigma^2$ 的最大似然估计量为 $\hat{\sigma}^2 = \frac{\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(X_i - \mu)^2}{n}$
