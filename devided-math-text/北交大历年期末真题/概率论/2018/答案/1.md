解:1）设 $B_{i} = \{$ 取出的第 $i$ 件为一等品 $\}$ （$i = 1,2$）$A_{i} = \{$ 取出第 $i$ 个箱子 $\}$ （$i = 1,2$）.所求概率为 $P(B_{1})$，由全概率公式，得

$$
\mathrm{P} (\mathrm{B} _ {1}) = P (\mathrm{A} _ {1}) \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{B} _ {1} \mid \mathrm{A} _ {1}) + \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{A} _ {2}) \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{B} _ {1} \mid \mathrm{A} _ {2}) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1 0}{5 0} + \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1 8}{3 0} = \frac {2}{5}.
$$

2）由条件概率公式，得 $P(B_{2}|B_{1})=\frac{P(B_{2}B_{1})}{P(B_{1})}$ . 对 $P(B_{2}B_{1})$ ，应用全概率公式，有

$P(B_{2}B_{1})=P(A_{1})P(B_{2}B_{1}|A_{1})+P(A_{2})P(B_{2}B_{1}|A_{2})=\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{C_{10}^{2}}{C_{50}^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{C_{18}^{2}}{C_{30}^{2}}.$

所以， $P(B_{2}|B_{1})=\frac{\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{C_{10}^{2}}{C_{50}^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{C_{18}^{2}}{C_{30}^{2}}}{\frac{2}{5}}=0.4856.$
