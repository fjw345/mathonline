解: $F_{Y}(\mathrm{y}) = P(\mathrm{Y}\leq \mathrm{y}) = \mathrm{P}(\frac{1}{X}\leq y)$

当 $y < 0, F_{Y}(\mathbf{y}) = 0$；

$$
0 \leq y < 1, F _ {Y} (y) = \mathrm{P} (\frac {1}{X} \leq y) = \mathrm{P} (X \geq \frac {1}{y}) = \int_ {1 / y} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {1}{2 x ^ {2}} d x = \frac {y}{2};
$$

$$
1 \leq y < + \infty , F _ {Y} (y) = \int_ {1 / x} ^ {1} \frac {1}{2 x ^ {2}} d x + \int_ {1} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {1}{2 x ^ {2}} d x = 1 - \frac {1}{2 y}
$$

将 $F_{\gamma}(\mathbf{y})$ 对 $\mathbf{y}$ 求导得其密度函数为

$$
Y \sim f _ {Y} (y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{c c} 0, & y < 0 \\ \frac {1}{2}, & 0 \leq y < 1 \\ \frac {1}{2 y ^ {2}}, & 1 \leq y < + \infty \end{array} \right.
$$
