解:(1) X 服从几何分布， $P(X=k)=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)\times\frac{1}{3}, k=1,2,3,\cdots$

(2) Y 取值是 1, 2, 3.

$$
P (Y = 1) = \frac {1}{3};
$$

$P(Y = 2) = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{3}$，第一次飞向另2扇中的一扇，第二次飞出去.

$$
P (Y = 3) = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times 1 = \frac {1}{3}.
$$

(3) X 和 Y 是独立的随机变量.

$$
\begin{array}{r l} P (X < Y) & = P (X = 1, Y = 2) + P (X = 1, Y = 3) + P (X = 2, Y = 3) \\ & = P (X = 1) P (Y = 2) + P (X = 1) P (Y = 3) + P (X = 2) P (Y = 3) \\ & = \frac {8}{2 7} \end{array}
$$

$$
P (\mathrm{X} = \mathrm{Y}) = \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{X} = 1, \mathrm{Y} = 1) + \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{X} = 2, \mathrm{Y} = 2) + \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{X} = 3, \mathrm{Y} = 3)
$$
