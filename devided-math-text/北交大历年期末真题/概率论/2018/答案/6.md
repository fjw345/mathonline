解:Z 的取值为 0，1，2，...

$$
\begin{array}{r l} P (Z = \mathrm{i}) & = \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{X} + \mathrm{Y} = \mathrm{i}) \\ & = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {i} P (\mathrm{X} = \mathrm{k}, \mathrm{Y} = \mathrm{i} - \mathrm{k}) \\ & = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {i} P (\mathrm{X} = \mathrm{k}) \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{Y} = \mathrm{i} - \mathrm{k}) \\ & = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {i} \frac {\lambda_ {1} ^ {k}}{k !} e ^ {- \lambda_ {1}} \frac {\lambda_ {2} ^ {i - k}}{(\mathrm{i} - \mathrm{k}) !} e ^ {- \lambda_ {2}} \\ & = \frac {e ^ {- (\lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {2})}}{i !} \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {i} \frac {i ! \lambda_ {1} ^ {k}}{k !} \frac {\lambda_ {2} ^ {i - k}}{(\mathrm{i} - \mathrm{k}) !} \\ & = \frac {e ^ {- (\lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {2})}}{i !} (\lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {2}) ^ {i}, i = 1, 2, \dots \end{array}
$$

随机变量 $Z$ 服从参数为 $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2$ 的泊松分布.
