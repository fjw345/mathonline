证明:首先求 $X$ 的边缘概率密度函数 $f_{X}(\mathbf{x})$

①当 $x \leq -1$ 或 $x \geq 1$，$f_{X}(x) = 0$.

② 当 $-1 < x < 1$，$f_{X}(\mathrm{x}) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}) \, \mathrm{d}\mathrm{y} = \int_{-1}^{1} \frac{1}{4} (1 - \mathrm{x}^{3} \mathrm{y} + \mathrm{xy}^{3}) \, \mathrm{d}y = \frac{1}{2}$.

所以， $f_{X}(\mathbf{x}) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{1}{2}, & -1 < x < 1\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$

$$
E (\mathrm{X}) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x f _ {X} (\mathrm{x}) d x = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} \frac {x}{2} d x = 0.
$$

再求 Y 的边缘概率密度函数 $f_{y}(y)$ ,

①当 $y \leq -1$ 或 $y \geq 1$, $f_{Y}(y) = 0$.

② 当 -1 < y < 1， $f_{Y}(y) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x, y) dx = \int_{-1}^{1} \frac{1}{4} (1 - x^{3} y + xy^{3}) dx = \frac{1}{2}$.

所以， $f_{Y}(\mathrm{y}) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{1}{2}, & -1 < y < 1\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$

$$
E (Y) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} y f _ {Y} (\mathrm{y}) d x = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} \frac {y}{2} d y = 0.
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \mathrm{E(XY)} & = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x y f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} \left[ \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} \frac {1}{4} x y (1 - x ^ {3} y + x y ^ {3}) d y \right] d x \\ & = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} \left(\frac {x ^ {2}}{1 0} - \frac {x ^ {4}}{6}\right) d x = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {1} \left(\frac {x ^ {2}}{1 0} - \frac {x ^ {4}}{6}\right) d x = 2 \left[ \frac {x ^ {3}}{3 0} - \frac {x ^ {5}}{3 0} \right] _ {x = 0} ^ {x = 1} = 0 \end{array}
$$

$Cov(X,Y)=E(XY)-E(X)E(Y)=0$ . X 与 Y 不相关;

在区域 $\{(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})\mid -1 < \mathbf{x} < 1, - 1 < \mathbf{y} < 1\}$ 上， $f(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})\neq f_X(\mathbf{x})f_Y(\mathbf{y})$

所以，X与Y不独立.
