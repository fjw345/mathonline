解:总体 $X$ 服从参数为 $\lambda (\lambda >0)$ 的泊松分布，故 $E(X_{i}) = \lambda ,D(X_{i}) = \lambda ,i = 1,2,\dots,n.$

$$
E (T _ {1}) = \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} E (X _ {i}) = \frac {n \lambda}{n} = \lambda , \quad D (T _ {1}) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} D (X _ {i}) = \frac {n \lambda}{n ^ {2}} = \frac {\lambda}{n};
$$

$$
E (T _ {2}) = \frac {1}{n - 1} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n - 1} E (X _ {i}) + \frac {\lambda}{n} = (1 + \frac {1}{n}) \lambda ,
$$

$$
D (T _ {2}) = \frac {1}{(n - 1) ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n - 1} D (X _ {i}) + \frac {\lambda}{n ^ {2}} = (\frac {1}{n - 1} + \frac {1}{n ^ {2}}) \lambda .
$$

所以， $E(T_{1})<E(T_{2}),\quad D(T_{1})<D(T_{2}).$

故（D）正确.
