解: $f(x,y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{cc}\frac{1}{4}, & -1 < x < 1,0 < y < 1,\\ 0, & \text{other} \end{array} \right.$ ，所以

$$
P (X + Y \geq 0) = \iint_ {x + y \geq 0} f (x, y) d x d y = \iint_ {y \geq - x} \frac {1}{4} d x d y = \frac {1}{2};
$$

$$
P (X - Y \geq 0) = \iint_ {x - y \geq 0} f (x, y) d x d y = \iint_ {y \leq x} \frac {1}{4} d x d y = \frac {1}{2};
$$

$$
P (\min \{X, Y \} \geq 0) = P (X \geq 0, Y \geq 0) \iint_ {x \geq 0, y \geq 0} f (x, y) d x d y = \int_ {0} ^ {1} d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} \frac {1}{4} d y = \frac {1}{4};
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} P (\max \{X, Y \} \geq 0) & = 1 - P (\max \{X, Y \} < 0) = 1 - P (X < 0, Y < 0) \\ & = 1 - \iint_ {x < 0, y < 0} f (x, y) d x d y = 1 - \frac {1}{4} = \frac {3}{4}. \end{array}
$$

故选（D）.
