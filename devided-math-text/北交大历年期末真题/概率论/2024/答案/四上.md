解:1） $E(X) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xf(x,\quad y)dxdy = \frac{21}{4}\int_{-1}^{1}dx\int_{x^2}^{1}x^3 ydy = \frac{21}{8}\int_{-1}^{1}x^3 (1 - x^4)dx = 0,$ $E(Y) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}yf(x,\quad y)dxdy = \frac{21}{4}\int_{-1}^{1}dx\int_{x^2}^{1}x^2 y^2 dy = \frac{7}{4}\int_{-1}^{1}x^2 (1 - x^6)dx = \frac{7}{9},$ $E(XY) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xyf(x,\quad y)dxdy = \frac{21}{4}\int_{-1}^{1}dx\int_{x^2}^{1}x^3 y^2 dy = \frac{7}{4}\int_{-1}^{1}x^3 (1 - x^6)dx = 0.$
2）当 $-1\leq x\leq 1$ 时， $f_{X}(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(x,\quad y)dy = \frac{21}{4}\int_{x^2}^{1}x^2 ydy = \frac{21}{8} x^2 (1 - x^4).$
所以，随机变量 $X$ 的边缘密度函数为 $f_{X}(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{cc}\frac{21}{8} x^{2}(1 - x^{4}) & -1\leq x\leq 1\\ 0 & \text{其它} \end{array} \right.$
当 $0\leq y\leq 1$ 时， $f_{Y}(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f(x,\quad y)dx = \frac{21}{4}\int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{\sqrt{y}}x^2 ydx = \frac{7}{2} yx^3\bigg|_0^{\sqrt{y}} = \frac{7}{2} y^{\frac{5}{2}}.$
