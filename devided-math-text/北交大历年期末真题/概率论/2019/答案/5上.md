解:1）由题意， $Y$ 为离散型随机变量，因此，

$$
P (Y = 1) = P (X = - 1) + P (X = 0) = \frac {2}{3}; \quad P (Y = 3) = P (X = 1) = \frac {1}{3},
$$

故 $Y$ 的分布函数

$$
F _ {Y} (y) = P (Y \leq y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 0 & , y < 1 \\ \frac {2}{3} & 1 \leq y < 3 \\ 1 & 3 \leq y \end{array} \right.
$$

-3 分

2) $F_{X}(x) = P(X\leq x),Y = X^{2} + X + 1 = (X + \frac{1}{2})^{2} + \frac{3}{4}\geq \frac{3}{4},$

因此， $F_{Y}(y) = P(Y\leq y) = P((X + \frac{1}{2})^{2} + \frac{3}{4}\leq y),$

当 $y < \frac{3}{4}$ 时，$F_{Y}(y) = 0$；

当 $y \geq \frac{3}{4}$ 时，$F_{Y}(y) = P((X + \frac{1}{2})^{2} \leq y - \frac{3}{4}) = P(|X + \frac{1}{2}| \leq \sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}})$ $= P(-\sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}} \leq X + \frac{1}{2} \leq \sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}})$ $= P(-\sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}} - \frac{1}{2} \leq X \leq \sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}} - \frac{1}{2})$ $= F_{X}(\sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}} - \frac{1}{2}) - F_{X}(-\sqrt{y - \frac{3}{4}} - \frac{1}{2}).$

故 $Y$ 的分布函数

$$
F _ {Y} (y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{c c} 0 & , y < \frac {3}{4} \\ F _ {X} (\sqrt {y - \frac {3}{4}} - \frac {1}{2}) - F _ {X} (- \sqrt {y - \frac {3}{4}} - \frac {1}{2}) & , y \geq \frac {3}{4} \end{array} \right.
$$

--6 分

3）若 $X$ 在(0，1)上服从均匀分布，即 $f_{X}(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & 0 < x < 1 \\ 0, & \text{other}. \end{cases}$

故， $F_{Y}(y)=P(X^{2}+X+1\leq y,\quad0<X<1)$ .

当 y<1 时， $F_{Y}(y)=0$ ;

当 $1 \leq y < 3$ 时，$F_{Y}(y) = P((X + \frac{1}{2})^{2} + \frac{3}{4} \leq y, \quad 0 < X < 1)$
