解:构造似然函数为

$$
L (\theta) = \prod_ {i = 1} ^ {n} f (x _ {i}) = \prod_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \theta x _ {i} ^ {- (\theta + 1)} = \theta^ {n} \left(x _ {\mathrm{r}} x _ {2} \dots x _ {n}\right) ^ {- (\theta + 1)}
$$

取 $\ln L(\theta) = n\ln \theta - (\theta + 1)\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln x_i$.

所以， $\frac{d}{d\theta}\ln L(\theta)=\frac{n}{\theta}-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln x_{i}$

令: $\frac{d}{d\theta}\ln L(\theta)=0$ ，得 $\frac{n}{\theta}-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln x_{i}=0$ ，

得到似然函数的唯一驻点: $\theta = \frac{n}{\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\ln x_i}$

所以，参数 $\theta$ 的最大似然估计量为 $\hat{\theta} = \frac{n}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln X_i}$
