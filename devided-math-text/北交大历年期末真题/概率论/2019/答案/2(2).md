解: $f(x,y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} \frac{1}{4}, & -1 < x < 1, -1 < y < 1, \\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$，所以
$P(X + Y \geq 0) = \iint_{x + y \geq 0} f(x,y) dx dy = \iint_{y \geq -x} \frac{1}{4} dx dy = \frac{1}{2};$
$P(X - Y \geq 0) = \iint_{x - y \geq 0} f(x,y) dx dy = \iint_{y \leq x} \frac{1}{4} dx dy = \frac{1}{2};$
$P(\min\{X,Y\} \geq 0) = P(X \geq 0,Y \geq 0) \iint_{x \geq 0,y \geq 0} f(x,y) dx dy = \int_0^1 dx \int_0^1 \frac{1}{4} dy = \frac{1}{4};$
$P(\max\{X,Y\} \geq 0) = 1 - P(\max\{X,Y\} < 0)$
$= 1 - P(X < 0,Y < 0)$
$= 1 - \iint_{x < 0,y < 0} f(x,y) dx dy = 1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}.$
