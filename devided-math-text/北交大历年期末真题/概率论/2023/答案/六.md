解:似然函数为:

$$
\begin{array}{l} L (p) = \prod_ {i = 1} ^ {n} P (X _ {i} = x _ {i}) = \prod_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \binom {x _ {i} - 1} {r - 1} p ^ {r} (1 - p) ^ {x _ {i} - r} \\ = \prod_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \binom {x _ {i} - 1} {r - 1} \cdot p ^ {n r} (1 - p) ^ {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} x _ {i} - n r}. \end{array}\tag{...4分}
$$

取对数，得

$\ln L(p) = C + nr \cdot \ln p + \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i - nr \right) \cdot \ln (1 - p),$ （$C$ 为常数）.…6分对 $p$ 求导，得似然方程:$\frac{nr}{p} - \frac{1}{1 - p} \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i - nr \right) = 0$.解似然方程，得 $p = \frac{nr}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i} = \frac{r}{\bar{x}}$.…8分因此，$p$ 的极大似然估计量为 $\hat{p} = \frac{r}{\bar{X}}$.…10分
