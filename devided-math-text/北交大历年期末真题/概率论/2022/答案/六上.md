$$

$$

$$
f _ {Y} (y) = n (1 - F (y)) ^ {n - 1} f (y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {n}{\theta} e ^ {- \frac {x y}{\theta}} & y > 0 \\ 0 & y \leq 0 \end{array} . \right.
