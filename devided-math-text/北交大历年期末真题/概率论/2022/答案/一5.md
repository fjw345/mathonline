解: $X$ 与 $\mathbf{Y}$ 的取值仅限于0，1，因此，有

$$
P (X = 1, Y = 1) = P (X = 1) \cdot P (Y = 1 \mid X = 1) = \frac {2}{4} \cdot \frac {3}{5} = \frac {3}{1 0};
$$

$$
P (X = 1, Y = 0) = P (X = 1) \cdot P (Y = 0 \mid X = 1) = \frac {2}{4} \cdot \frac {2}{5} = \frac {2}{1 0};
$$

$$
P (X = 0, Y = 1) = P (X = 0) \cdot P (Y = 1 | X = 0) = \frac {2}{4} \cdot \frac {2}{5} = \frac {2}{1 0};
$$

$$
P (X = 0, Y = 0) = P (X = 0) \cdot P (Y = 0 \mid X = 0) = \frac {2}{4} \cdot \frac {3}{5} = \frac {3}{1 0}.
$$

则 $(X,Y)$ 的联合分布律以及边缘分布律如下所示:

| X\\Y | 0 | 1 | ${p}_{i \cdot }$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.3 | 0.2 | 0.5 |
| 1 | 0.2 | 0.3 | 0.5 |
| ${p}_{\cdot j}$ | 0.5 | 0.5 | |

于是，EX = EY = 0.5，DX = DY = 0.25， $E(XY) = 0.3$ .

因此， $Cov(X,Y) = 0.3 - 0.5\times 0.5 = 0.05,\quad \rho_{XY} = \frac{Cov(X,Y)}{\sqrt{DX}\cdot\sqrt{DY}} = \frac{1}{5}.$ 故选（B）.
