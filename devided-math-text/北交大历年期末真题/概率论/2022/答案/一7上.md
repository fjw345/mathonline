【解法1】 $X$ 的概率密度函数为: $f_{X}(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^{2}}{2}}, -\infty < x < +\infty,$

$Y$ 关于 $X = x$ 的条件密度为: $f_{\gamma ,Y}(y|x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(y - y)^2}{2}}, - \infty < x < + \infty .$

于是 X 与 Y 的联合密度为:

$$
f (x, y) = f (x) f _ {Y | X} (y | x) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} e ^ {- \frac {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 2 x y + x ^ {2}}{2}}, - \infty < x, y < + \infty .
$$

于是 $Y$ 的边缘密度为

$$
f _ {Y} (y) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {\infty} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{2 \pi} e ^ {- \left(x - \frac {y ^ {2}}{2}\right) ^ {2}} e ^ {- \frac {y ^ {2}}{4}} \mathrm{d} x = \frac {1}{2 \sqrt {\pi}} e ^ {- \frac {y ^ {2}}{4}}, - \infty < y < + \infty .
$$

即 Y 服从二维正态 $N(0,2)$ . 则 $f(x,y)$ 分布的概率密度形式为:

$$
f (x, y) = \frac {1}{2 \pi \sigma_ {1} \sigma_ {2} \sqrt {1 - \rho^ {2}}} e ^ {- \frac {1}{2 (1 - \rho^ {2})} \left\{\left(\frac {x - \mu_ {1}}{\sigma_ {1}}\right) ^ {2} - 2 \rho \left(\frac {x - \mu_ {1}}{\sigma_ {1}}\right) \left(\frac {y - \mu_ {2}}{\sigma_ {1}}\right) t \left(\frac {x - \mu_ {2}}{\sigma_ {2}}\right) ^ {2} \right\}}.
$$

对比 $X$ 与 $Y$ 的联合密度可得， $\mu_{1} = \mu_{2} = 0,\sigma_{1} = 1$ ， $\sigma_{2} = \sqrt{2}$

因此， $\sqrt{2}\sqrt{1 - \rho^2} = 1$ ，且 $\rho >0$ ，于是 $\rho = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

【解法2】 $X \sim N(0,1) \Rightarrow EX = 0, DX = 1;$

由 $X$ 与 $Y$ 的联合密度为

$$
\begin{array}{c} f (x, y) = f (x) f _ {Y \mid X} (y | x) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} e ^ {- \frac {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 2 x y + x ^ {2}}{2}}, \quad - \infty < x, y < + \infty . \\ E X Y = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x y \frac {1}{2 \pi} e ^ {- \frac {2 x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 2 x y}{2}} \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x \cdot \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} e ^ {- \frac {x ^ {2}}{2}} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} y \frac {1}{2 \pi} e ^ {- \frac {2 x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 2 x y}{2}} \mathrm{d} y \\ = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} e ^ {- t} \cdot 2 t ^ {2} \cdot \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 t}} \cdot \frac {1}{2} \cdot \sqrt {2} \mathrm{d} t = \frac {1}{\sqrt {\pi}} \Gamma \left(\frac {5}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {\pi}} \Gamma \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) = 1. \end{array}
$$
