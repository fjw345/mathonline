$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {【解】} & \operatorname{cov} (U, V) = \operatorname{cov} (a X + Y, X + b Y) \\ & \qquad = a \operatorname{cov} (X, X) + (a b + 1) \operatorname{cov} (X, Y) + b \operatorname{cov} (Y, Y) \\ & \qquad = a D (X) + (a b + 1) \operatorname{cov} (X, Y) + b D (Y). \end{array}
$$

再由于随机变量 $X \sim N(-1, 2)$，$Y \sim N(1, 2)$，而且 $X$ 与 $Y$ 不相关，所以

$$
D (X) = 2, D (Y) = 2, \operatorname{cov} (X, Y) = 0.
$$

因此， $\mathrm{cov}(U,V)=2(a+b)$ .
