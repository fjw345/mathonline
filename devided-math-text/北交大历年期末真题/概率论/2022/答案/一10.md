【解】首先有 $P\left(\max (X,Y) = 2\right) = P(Y = 2) = b + 0.1$

$$
P \left(\min (X, Y) = 1\right) = P (X = 1, Y = 1) + P (X = 1, Y = 2) = 0. 2,
$$

$$
P \left(\max (X, Y) = 2, \min (X, Y) = 1\right) = P (X = 1, Y = 2),
$$

由独立性条件可得

$$
P \left(\max (X, Y) = 2, \min (X, Y) = 1\right) = P \left(\max (X, Y) = 2\right) P \left(\min (X, Y) = 1\right)
$$

即 $0.2(b+0.1)=0.1 \Rightarrow b=0.4;$

再由分布律的归一性，可得 $0.4+a+b=1\Rightarrow a=0.2$ .

于是 $EX = 0.4 - 0.6 = -0.2$ ， $EY = 0.1 + 1 = 1.2$ ，

$$
E X Y = (- 1) \cdot 0. 1 + (- 2) \cdot 0. 4 + 1 \cdot 0. 1 + 2 \cdot 0. 1 = - 0. 6,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov} (X, Y) = E X Y - E X \cdot E Y = - 0. 3 6.
$$
