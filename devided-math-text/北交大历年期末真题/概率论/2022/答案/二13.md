【解】 $\mu_{k} = EX^{k}$ ， $\mu_{2} = E\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}X_{i}^{2}\right) = EX^{2},$

$$
D \left(\frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \cdot n \cdot \left(E X ^ {4} - \left(E X ^ {2}\right) ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{n} \left(\mu_ {4} - \mu_ {2} ^ {2}\right),
$$

$$
\text {故} P \left\{\left| \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} ^ {2} - \mu_ {2} \right| \geq \varepsilon \right\} \leq \frac {D \left(\frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} ^ {2}\right)}{\varepsilon^ {2}} = \frac {\mu_ {+} - \mu_ {+} ^ {2}}{n \varepsilon^ {2}}.
$$

故选（A）.
