8、设 $(X_{1},Y_{1}),(X_{2},Y_{2}),\ldots,(X_{n},Y_{n})$ 为来自总体 $N(\mu_{1},\mu_{2};\sigma_{1}^{2},\sigma_{2}^{2};\rho)$ 的简单随机样本，令 $\theta=\mu_{1}-\mu_{2},\quad\overline{X}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}X_{k},\quad\overline{Y}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}Y_{k},\quad\hat{\theta}=\overline{X}-\overline{Y}$ ，则【】.
(A) $E(\hat{\theta})=\theta, D(\hat{\theta})=\frac{\sigma_{1}^{2}+\sigma_{2}^{2}}{n}$
(B) $E(\hat{\theta})=\theta, D(\hat{\theta})=\frac{\sigma_{1}^{2}+\sigma_{2}^{2}-2\rho\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}}{n}$
(C) $E(\hat{\theta})\neq\theta,\quad D(\hat{\theta})=\frac{\sigma_{1}^{2}+\sigma_{2}^{2}}{n}$
(D) $E(\hat{\theta})\neq\theta,D(\hat{\theta})=\frac{\sigma_{1}^{2}+\sigma_{2}^{2}-2\rho\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}}{n}.$
