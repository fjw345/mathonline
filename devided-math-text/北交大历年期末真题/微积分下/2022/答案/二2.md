解: $gradf = \nabla f = \left\{1 - \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}, 1 - \frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}\right\}$，因此，$gradf(3,4) = \left\{\frac{2}{5}, \frac{1}{5}\right\}$；因此，$\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial \bar{a}}\right|_{(3,4)} = gradf(3,4) \bullet \bar{a}^0 = \left\{\frac{2}{5}, \frac{1}{5}\right\} \bullet \left\{-\frac{4}{5}, \frac{3}{5}\right\} = -\frac{1}{5}$.
