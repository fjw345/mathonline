解:补充下盖 $\Sigma_{1}:z = 0,x^{2} + y^{2}\leq 1$ ，取下侧.则， $\iint_{\Sigma_1}xdydz + ydzdx + zdxdy = 0.$ 于是，由高斯公式，有

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \Phi & = \iint_ {\Sigma + \Sigma_ {1}} - \iint_ {\Sigma_ {1}} = \iiint_ {\Omega} 3 d x d y d z - 0 \\ & = 3 \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {1} r d r \int_ {0} ^ {1 - r ^ {2}} d z \\ & = 6 \pi \int_ {0} ^ {1} (r - r ^ {3}) d r = \frac {3 \pi}{2}. \end{array}
$$
