2 曲线 $x = e^{2t}, y = \ln t, z = t^2$ 在对应于 $t = 2$ 点处的切线方程是【】.
(A) $\frac{x - e^4}{2e^4} = \frac{y - \ln 2}{1} = \frac{z - 4}{4}$
(B) $\frac{x - e^4}{2e^4} = \frac{y - \ln 2}{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{z - 4}{2}$
(C) $\frac{x + e^4}{2e^4} = \frac{y + \frac{1}{2} - \ln 2}{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{z}{4}$
(D) $\frac{x + e^4}{e^4} = \frac{y + \frac{1}{2} - \ln 2}{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{z}{4}$
