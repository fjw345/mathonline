3. 设 $f(x, y)$ 是连续函数，交换积分 $\int_{0}^{a} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{x} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}y (a > 0)$ 的积分次序后的结果为【】.
(A) $\int_{0}^{a}\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{y}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x$
(B) $\int_{0}^{a}\mathrm{d}y\int_{a}^{y}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x$
(C) $\int_{0}^{a}\mathrm{d}y\int_{y}^{a}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x$
(D) $\int_{0}^{a}\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{a}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x$ .
