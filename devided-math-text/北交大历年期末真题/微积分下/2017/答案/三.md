解法1: $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = yf_1' + yg'(x)f_2'$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial x \partial y} = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} + y \left(f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} x + f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} g (x)\right) + g ^ {\prime} (x) f _ {2} ^ {\prime} + y g ^ {\prime} (x) \left(x f _ {2 1} ^ {\prime \prime} + g (x) f _ {2 2} ^ {\prime \prime}\right).
$$

因为 $g(1) = 1, g'(1) = 0$，于是代入 $x = 1, y = 1$，得

$$
\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x\partial y}\bigg|_{\substack{x = 1\\ y = 1}} = f_1'(1,1) + f_{11}''(1,1) + f_{12}''(1,1).
$$

解法2: $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = yf_1' + yg'(x)f_2'$，因为 $g(1) = 1, g'(1) = 0$，

所以， $\left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{x=1}=yf_{1}^{\prime}(y,y)$ ,

于是， $\left.\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{\substack{x = 1\\ y = 1}} = \left[f_1'(y,y) + y\big(f_{11}''(y,y) + f_{12}''(y,y)\big)\right]_{y = 1}$ $= f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1) + f_{11}^{\prime \prime}(1,1) + f_{12}^{\prime \prime}(1,1).$
