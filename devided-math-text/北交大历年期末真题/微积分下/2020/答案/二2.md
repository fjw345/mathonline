解:令 $F(x,y,z) = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2} -\sqrt{2} xyz - \sqrt{2}$ ，则

$$
F _ {x} (x, y, z) = \frac {x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}}} - \sqrt {2} y z, \Rightarrow F _ {x} (1, 0, - 1) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}};
$$

$$
F _ {y} (x, y, z) = \frac {y}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}}} - \sqrt {2} x z, \Rightarrow F _ {y} (1, 0, - 1) = \sqrt {2};
$$

$$
F _ {z} (x, y, z) = \frac {z}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}}} - \sqrt {2} x y, \Rightarrow F _ {z} (1, 0, - 1) = \frac {- 1}{\sqrt {2}}.
$$

所以， $dz\bigg|_{(1,0,-1)}=-\frac{F_{x}(1,0,-1)}{F_{z}(1,0,-1)}dx-\frac{F_{y}(1,0,-1)}{F_{z}(1,0,-1)}dy=dx+2dy$
