解:由题意， $\frac{\partial(xy^{2})}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial(y\varphi(x))}{\partial x}$ ，即 $\varphi'(x)=2x,\quad\varphi(x)=x^{2}+C$ .
再由 $\varphi(0)=0$ ，得C=0，即 $\varphi(x)=x^{2}$
 $I=\int_{(0,0)}^{(1,1)}xy^{2}dx+yx^{2}dy=\frac{1}{2}\int_{(0,0)}^{(1,1)}d(x^{2}y^{2})=\frac{1}{2}x^{2}y^{2}\bigg|_{(0,0)}^{(1,1)}=\frac{1}{2}.$
