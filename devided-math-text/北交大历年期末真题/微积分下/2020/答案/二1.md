解:令 $F(x,y,z) = z - e^{z} + 2xy - 3$ ，则

$$
F _ {x} (x, y, z) = 2 y \Rightarrow F _ {x} (1, 2, 0) = 4;
$$

$$
\boldsymbol {F} _ {y} (x, y, z) = 2 x \Rightarrow \boldsymbol {F} _ {y} (1, 2, 0) = 2;
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} F _ {z} (x, y, z) = 1 - e ^ {z} & \Rightarrow F _ {z} (1, 2, 0) = 0. \end{array}
$$

故所求切平面方程: $4(x - 1) + 2(y - 2) = 0$ ，或 $2x + y - 4 = 0$
