解:令 $gradz=(z_{x}, z_{y})$ ，则 $(z_{x}, z_{y})\cdot\frac{(1,1)}{\sqrt{2}}=1,\quad(z_{x}, z_{y})\cdot\frac{(1,-1)}{\sqrt{2}}=2$ .
整理，得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}z_{x}+z_{y}&=\sqrt{2}\\ z_{x}-z_{y}&=2\sqrt{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，即 $z_{x}=\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2},\quad z_{y}=\frac{-\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ,
所以，gradz=(zx,zy)= $\left(\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2},\quad\frac{-\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)=\left(\frac{3}{\sqrt{2}},\quad\frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)$ .
