3. 设 $f(x, y)$ 是连续函数，交换二次积分 $\int_{1}^{e} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{\ln x} f(x, y) \mathrm{d}y$ 积分次序的结果为【D】.
(A) $\int_{1}^{e}\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{\ln x}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x;$
(B) $\int_{e^{y}}^{e}\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{1}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x;$
(C) $\int_{0}^{\ln x}\mathrm{d}y\int_{1}^{e}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x;\quad(D)\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}y\int_{e^{y}}^{e}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x.$
