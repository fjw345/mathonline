解:添一平面 $\Sigma_{1}:z = 1(x^{2} + y^{2}\leqslant 1)$ 取下侧，

则 $\Sigma +\Sigma_{1}$ 拘成一封闭曲面（取内侧）.因此

$$
\begin{array}{l} I = \iint_ {\Sigma} x y \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z + x y ^ {2} \mathrm{d} z \mathrm{d} x + z \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} z \\ = \left\{\oint_ {\Sigma^ {+} \Sigma_ {1}} - \iint_ {\Sigma_ {1}} \right\} x y \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z + x y ^ {2} \mathrm{d} z \mathrm{d} x + z \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} z \\ = - \iiint_ {V} (y + 2 x y + 1) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z + \iint_ {D _ {x y}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \end{array}
$$

由对称性: $\iiint_{\Omega} y \, dv = 0$ $\iiint_{\Omega} 2xy \, dv = 0.$

$$
\iiint_ {D _ {x y}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \pi .
$$
