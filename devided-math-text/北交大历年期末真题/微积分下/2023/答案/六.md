解:设 $D: x^{2} + y^{2} \leq a^{2}$，则

$$
\begin{array}{r l} I & = \iint_ {D} \left(\frac {\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac {\partial P}{\partial y}\right) d x d y = \iint_ {D} (3 - 3 x ^ {2} - 3 y ^ {2}) d x d y \\ & = 3 \pi R ^ {2} - 3 \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {R} r ^ {2} \cdot r d r \\ & = 3 \pi R ^ {2} - \frac {3}{2} \pi R ^ {4} \end{array}
$$

则由 $I_{R}^{\prime} = 6\pi R - 6\pi R^{3} = 0$ ，得 $R = 1$ ， $R = 0$ （舍去）.

由实际问题或 $I_{R = 1}^{\prime \prime} = -6\pi < 0$ ，因此

$$
I _ {\mathrm{max}} = 3 \pi - \frac {3}{2} \pi = \frac {3}{2} \pi .
$$
