解:由 $A^T BA = 2A^T B + I$ 知 $B = (AA^T - 2A^T)^{-1}$

$$
\text {又} A A ^ {T} - 2 A ^ {T} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{array} \right) - 2 \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} - 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 2 \\ 0 & - 2 & - 1 \end{array} \right),
$$

所以， $B = (AA^{T} - 2A^{T})^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 2 \\ 0 & -2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 & -2 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}.$
