解:将方程组的增广矩阵化为阶梯形:

$$
\begin{array}{c}\left(\begin{array}{c c c c}a&1&1&b\\1&a&a&2\\1&1&a&1\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c}1&1&a&1\\0&a - 1&0&1\\0&1 - a&1 - a ^ {2}&b - a\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c}1&1&a&1\\0&a - 1&0&1\\0&0&1 - a ^ {2}&b - a + 1\end{array}\right),\end{array}
$$
