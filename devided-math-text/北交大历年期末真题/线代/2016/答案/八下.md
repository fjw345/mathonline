属于1的特征向量为 $\alpha_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, \alpha_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$，单位化得 $\beta_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, \beta_{2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$；
属于9的特征向量为 $\alpha_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$，单位化得 $\alpha_{3} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$.
令 $P = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ 0 & \frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{bmatrix}$，则 $P$ 为正交矩阵，且 $P^{T}(A^{T}A)P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 9 \end{pmatrix}$.
