(2) $\lambda = 0 \Rightarrow r(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}) = r(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{4}) = r(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3},\alpha_{4}) = 3$ ，等价；

(3) $\lambda = -1 \Rightarrow r(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}) = 3, r(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{4}) = 2$ ，不等价；

(4) $\lambda = -2 \Rightarrow r(\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3) = 2, r(\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_4) = 3$，不等价；

（5）其它时， $r(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}) = r(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{4}) = r(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3},\alpha_{4}) = 3$ ，等价.

故 $\left\{\lambda\mid\lambda\in R,\lambda\neq-1,\lambda\neq-2\right\}$ .

故选(C).
