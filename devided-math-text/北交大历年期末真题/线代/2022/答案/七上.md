【解】1）二次型的矩阵 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix},$ -2分由 $|\lambda E - A| = 0$ ，得特征值 $\lambda_{1} = \lambda_{2} = 4, \lambda_{3} = 2.$ -4分对 $\lambda_{1} = \lambda_{2} = 4$ ，解方程 $(4E - A)x = 0$ 可得 $\alpha_{1} = (0,1,0)^{T},\alpha_{1} = (1,0,1)^{T};$ 对 $\lambda_3 = 2$ ，解方程 $(2E - A)x = 0$ 可得 $\alpha_{3} = (1,0, - 1)^{T}.$
