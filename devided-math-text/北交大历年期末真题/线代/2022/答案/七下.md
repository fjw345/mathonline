$$
\text {令} \quad Q = \left(\frac {\boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {1}}{\| \boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {1} \|}, \frac {\boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {2}}{\| \boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {2} \|}, \frac {\boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {3}}{\| \boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {3} \|}\right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} & \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} & - \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \end{array} \right), \quad \boldsymbol {x} = \left( \begin{array}{c} x _ {1} \\ x _ {2} \\ x _ {3} \end{array} \right), \boldsymbol {y} = \left( \begin{array}{c} y _ {1} \\ y _ {2} \\ y _ {3} \end{array} \right),
$$

故在正交变换 x = Qy 下，二次型 $f(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3})$ 化为标准形:

$$
f = 4 y _ {1} ^ {2} + 4 y _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 y _ {3} ^ {2}.
$$

2）当 $x = Qy$ 时， $x^{T} = y^{T}Q^{T}Qy = y^{T}y$ ，于是

$$
\frac {f (\boldsymbol {x})}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {T} \boldsymbol {x}} = \frac {4 y _ {1} ^ {2} + 4 y _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 y _ {3} ^ {2}}{y _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {3} ^ {2}} \geq \frac {2 y _ {1} ^ {2} + 2 y _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 y _ {3} ^ {2}}{y _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {3} ^ {2}} = 2,
$$
