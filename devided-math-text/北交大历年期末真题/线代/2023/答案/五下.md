因此，$\beta$ 不能被 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 线性表示.

2）当 $a = -6$ 或 $b = 4$ 时，$R(\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3) = R(\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \beta) = 3$，因此，$\beta$ 可由 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ 唯一线性表示.

此时，

$$
\left(\boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {1}, \boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {2}, \boldsymbol {\alpha} _ {3}, \boldsymbol {\beta}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c}1&5&1&2\\0&1&1&1\\0&0&- 4&\boldsymbol {b} - 8\\0&0&0&0\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c}1&0&0&5 - \boldsymbol {b}\\0&4&0&\boldsymbol {b} - 4\\0&0&- 4&\boldsymbol {b} - 8\\0&0&0&0\end{array}\right)
$$

因此， $\beta = (5 - b)\alpha_{1} + \frac{b - 4}{4}\alpha_{2} - \frac{b - 8}{4}\alpha_{3}$
