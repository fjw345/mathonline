解: $(\alpha_{1} + 2\alpha_{2}, 2\alpha_{2} + 3\alpha_{3}, 3\alpha_{3} + \alpha_{1})$ $= (\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}) \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 3 \end{pmatrix} = (\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3})K$，而 $|K| = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 12 \neq 0$。故选（C）。
