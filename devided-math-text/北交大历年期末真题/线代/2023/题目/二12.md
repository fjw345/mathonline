12、若

$$
A \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \boldsymbol {a} _ {1 1} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {1 2} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {1 3} \\ \boldsymbol {a} _ {2 1} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {2 2} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {2 3} \\ \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 1} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 2} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 3} \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \boldsymbol {a} _ {1 1} - 3 \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 1} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {1 2} - 3 \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 2} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {1 3} - 3 \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 3} \\ \boldsymbol {a} _ {2 1} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {2 2} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {2 3} \\ \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 1} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 2} & \boldsymbol {a} _ {3 3} \end{array} \right],
$$

(A)

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ - 3 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right]
$$

(B)

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & - 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right]
$$

(C)

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & 0 & - 3 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right]
$$

(D)

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ - 3 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right].
$$
