6、记三元齐次线性方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \lambda x_1 + x_2 + \lambda^2 x_3 = 0 \\ x_1 + \lambda x_2 + x_3 = 0 \\ x_1 + x_2 + \lambda x_3 = 0 \end{array} \right.$ 的系数矩阵为 $A$。若存在三阶矩阵 $B \neq O$，使得 $AB = O$，则【】.

(A) $\lambda = -2$ 且 $|B| = 0$
(B) $\lambda = -2$ 且 $|B| \neq 0$
(C) $\lambda = 1$ 且 $|B| = 0$
(D) $\lambda = 1$ 且 $|B| \neq 0$.
