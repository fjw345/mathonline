\det (A - \lambda E) = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 2 - \lambda & 2 & - 2 \\ 2 & 5 - \lambda & - 4 \\ - 2 & - 4 & 5 - \lambda \end{array} \right| = (1 - \lambda) ^ {2} (1 0 - \lambda),
