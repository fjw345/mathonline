解:设 $\lambda$ 是矩阵 $A$ 的对应于 $\alpha$ 的特征值，则

$$
A \boldsymbol {\alpha} = \left( \begin{array}{c} 3 - 4 - 3 \\ \boldsymbol {k} + 4 + 6 \\ 3 - 1 2 - 3 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c} - 4 \\ \boldsymbol {k} + 1 0 \\ - 1 2 \end{array} \right) = \lambda \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ - 2 \\ 3 \end{array} \right).
$$

所以， $\lambda=-4,\quad k=-2$ .
