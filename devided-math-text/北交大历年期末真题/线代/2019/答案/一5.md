解:由 $A = I - b\alpha\alpha^{T}$ ，知

$AA^{T} = (I - b\alpha \alpha^{T})(I - b\alpha \alpha^{T}) = I - 2b\alpha \alpha^{T} + ab^{2}\alpha \alpha^{T} = I + (ab^{2} - 2b)\alpha \alpha^{T}.$ 因此， $A$ 为正交矩阵 $\Leftrightarrow AA^T = I$

$$
\Leftrightarrow (a b ^ {2} - 2 b) \alpha \alpha^ {T} = 0 \Leftrightarrow a b (a b - 2) = 0.
