解:利用数学归纳法:

$$
D _ {1} = \alpha + \beta = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {1} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {1 - i}; D _ {2} = \left| \begin{array}{c c} \alpha + \beta & \alpha \beta \\ 1 & \alpha + \beta \end{array} \right| = \alpha^ {2} + \alpha \beta + \beta^ {2} = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {2} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {2 - i}.
$$

2）假设 $D_{k} = \sum_{i = 0}^{k}\alpha^{i}\beta^{k - i}, (k < n)$，则

$$
\begin{array}{r l} D _ {k + 1} & = (\alpha + \beta) D _ {k} - \alpha \beta D _ {k - 1} = (\alpha + \beta) \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {k - i} - \alpha \beta \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k - 1} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {k - 1 - i} \\ & = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k} \alpha^ {i + 1} \beta^ {k - i} + \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {k + 1 - i} - \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k - 1} \alpha^ {i + 1} \beta^ {k - i} \\ & = \alpha^ {k + 1} + \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {k + 1 - i} = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {k + 1} \alpha^ {i} \beta^ {k + 1 - i}. \end{array}
$$

综合上述，有 $D_{n} = \sum_{i = 0}^{n + 1}\alpha^{i}\beta^{n + 1 - i} = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}(n + 1)\alpha^{n}, & \alpha = \beta ,\\ \frac{\alpha^{n + 1} - \beta^{n + 1}}{\alpha - \beta}, & \alpha \neq \beta . \end{array} \right.$

注:仅求出当 $\alpha = \beta$ 时， $D_{n} = (n + 1)\alpha^{n}$ 。——给4分.
