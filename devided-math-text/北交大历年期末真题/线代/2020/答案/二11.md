11、设 $\alpha = (x_{1}, x_{2}, \dots, x_{n})^{T} \in R^{n}$，$\alpha^{T} \alpha = 1$，令 $A = I - 2\alpha\alpha^{T}$。则论述:① $A$ 是对称阵；② $A^{2}$ 是单位阵；③ $A$ 是可逆阵；④ $A$ 是正交阵；⑤ $A$ 可相似对角化。中正确的个数【D】。① $A$ 是对称阵；② $A^{2}$ 是单位阵；③ $A$ 逆阵；④ $A$ 是正交阵；⑤ $A$ 可相似对角化
(A) 2
(B) 3
(C) 4
(D) 5。

解: $A^T = (I - 2\alpha \alpha^T)^T = I - 2(\alpha \alpha^T)^T = I - 2\alpha \alpha^T = A$ $A^2 = (I - 2\alpha \alpha^T)(I - 2\alpha \alpha^T) = I - 2\alpha \alpha^T - 2\alpha \alpha^T + 4\alpha \underline{\alpha}^T\alpha \alpha^T = I.$
