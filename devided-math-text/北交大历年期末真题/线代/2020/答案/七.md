\- 将 $\lambda_{1} = 1$ 代入到齐次方程组 $(\lambda I - A)X = 0$ :

$\left(I - A\right) = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 0 & -2 \\ 0 & -2 & 2 \\ -2 & 2 & -4 \end{pmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$，得基础解系:$\alpha_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$

单位化，得 $\gamma_{1}=\frac{\alpha_{1}}{\|\alpha_{1}\|}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}$ ;

\- 将 $\lambda_{2} = 3$ 代入到齐次方程组 $(\lambda I - A)X = 0$:
$(3I - A) = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \\ -2 & 2 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$，得基础解系:$\alpha_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$，单位化，得 $\gamma_{2} = \frac{\alpha_{2}}{\|\alpha_{2}\|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$；

\- 将 $\lambda_{3} = 7$ 代入到齐次方程组 $(\lambda I - A)X = 0$:
