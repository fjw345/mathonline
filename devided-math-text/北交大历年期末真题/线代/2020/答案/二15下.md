解:由题意，点 $M$ 的坐标 $(x,y,z)$ 是如下方程组的解

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y + z - 2 = 0 \\ x + 2 y - z - 1 = 0 \\ x + 2 y + z - 1 = 0 \end{array} , \right.
$$

再由 $(A,b)$

$$
(A, b) = \left(\begin{array}{c c c c}1&1&1&2\\1&2&- 1&1\\1&2&1&1\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c}1&0&1&3\\0&1&0&- 1\\0&0&1&0\end{array}\right),
$$

易求得 x=3, y=-1, z=0 .
