解:原式 $= e^{\lim_{x\to +\infty}[x^2\ln (1 + \frac{1}{x}) - x]} = e^{\lim_{x\to +\infty}(x^2(\frac{1}{x} -\frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{x})^2 +o(\frac{1}{x^2})) - x]} = e^{-\frac{1}{2}}.$ 另解:令 $\frac{1}{x} = t$ ，则原式 $= e^{\lim_{x\to +\infty}[x^2\ln (1 + \frac{1}{x}) - x]} = e^{\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\ln(1 + t) - t}{t^2}} = e^{\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\frac{1}{1 + t} - 1}{2t}} = e^{-\frac{1}{2}}.$
