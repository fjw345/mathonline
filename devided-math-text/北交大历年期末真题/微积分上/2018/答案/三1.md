解:

$$
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\int_ {0} ^ {x} \left(e ^ {\sin t} - \cos t\right) d t}{x \ln (1 + 2 x)} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\int_ {0} ^ {x} \left(e ^ {\sin t} - \cos t\right) d t}{2 x ^ {2}} \\ = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {e ^ {\sin x} - \cos x}{4 x} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {e ^ {\sin x} \cos x + \sin x}{4} = \frac {1}{4}. \end{array}
$$
