解:令 $\sqrt{e^x - 1} = t \Rightarrow e^x - 1 = t^2 \Rightarrow x = \ln(1 + t^2)$，于是 $dx = \frac{2tdt}{1 + t^2}$。原式 $= \int (1 + t^2)\frac{2t}{1 + t^2}\arctan tdt = \int 2t\arctan tdt = \int \arctan tdt^2$ $= t^2\arctan t - \int \frac{t^2}{1 + t^2} dt$ $= t^2\arctan t - t + \arctan t + C = e^x\arctan \sqrt{e^x - 1} - \sqrt{e^x - 1} + C.$
