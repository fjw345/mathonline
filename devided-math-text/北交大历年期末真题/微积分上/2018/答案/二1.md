解: $y' = -\frac{1}{x^2}, y'' = \frac{2}{x^3}$，于是 $R = \frac{(1 + y'^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{|y''|} = \frac{\left(1 + \frac{1}{x^4}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}{\left|\frac{2}{x^3}\right|} = \frac{1}{2}\left(x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} \geq \sqrt{2}$ 且 $x^2 = \frac{1}{x^2}$，即 $x = \pm 1$ 时，$R$ 取得最小值 $\sqrt{2}$。
