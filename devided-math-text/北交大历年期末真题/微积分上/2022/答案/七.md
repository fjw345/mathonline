解:设 $A(x_0,\ln x_0)$ ，切线斜率 $\frac{1}{x_0}$ ，切线方程为

$$
y - \ln x _ {0} = \frac {1}{x _ {0}} \left(x - x _ {0}\right),
$$

代入 $(0,1)$ 点得 $x_{0}=e^{2}$ ， $A(e^{2},2)$ ，AB的方程为

$$
\begin{array}{r l} y & = \frac {2}{e ^ {2} - 1} (x - 1) \\ S & = \int_ {1} ^ {e ^ {2}} \left(\ln x - \frac {2}{e ^ {2} - 1} (x - 1)\right) d x \\ & = x (\ln x - 1) \Big | _ {1} ^ {e ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{e ^ {2} - 1} (x - 1) ^ {2} \Bigg | _ {1} ^ {e ^ {2}} \\ & = e ^ {2} + 1 - (e ^ {2} - 1) = 2 \\ V & = \pi \int_ {1} ^ {e ^ {2}} \ln^ {2} x d x - \frac {1}{3} \pi 2 ^ {2} (e ^ {2} - 1) \\ & = \pi x (\ln^ {2} x - 2 \ln x + 2) \Big | _ {1} ^ {e ^ {2}} - \frac {4}{3} \pi (e ^ {2} - 1) \\ & = 2 \pi (e ^ {2} - 1) - \frac {4}{3} \pi (e ^ {2} - 1) = \frac {2}{3} \pi (e ^ {2} - 1) \end{array}
$$
