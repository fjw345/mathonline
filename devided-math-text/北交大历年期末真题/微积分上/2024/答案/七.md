解:由已知，有 $V = \pi \int_{-1}^{1}y^{2}dx = \pi \int_{-1}^{1}(x^{3} - ax + b)^{2}dx$ $= \pi \int_{-1}^{1}(x^{6} - 2ax^{4} + 2bx^{3} + a^{2}x^{2} - 2abx + b^{2})dx$ $= 2\pi \int_0^1 (x^6 -2ax^4 +a^2 x^2 +b^2)dx$ $= 2\pi \left(\frac{1}{7} -\frac{2a}{5} +\frac{a^2}{3} +b^2\right).$

再由 $V_{a}^{\prime} = 0$ ， $V_{b}^{\prime} = 0$ 时，即 $a = \frac{3}{5}$ ， $b = 0$ 时， $V_{\mathrm{min}} = \frac{8\pi}{175}$
