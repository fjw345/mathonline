解:当 $t = \frac{\pi}{4}$ 时，曲线 $L$ 上的点为 $(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4})$ ，且 $k = \frac{dy}{dx}\bigg|_{t = \frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{-3\cos^2t\cdot\sin t}{3\sin^2t\cdot\cos t}\bigg|_{t = \frac{\pi}{4}} = -1,$ 故所求切线方程为: $y - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} = -(x - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4})$ ，或 $x + y = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
