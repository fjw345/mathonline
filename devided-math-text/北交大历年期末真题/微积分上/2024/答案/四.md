解:方程 $y = f(x + y)$ 两边同时对 $x$ 求导，得

$\frac {d y}{d x} = f ^ {\prime} (x + y) \cdot (1 + \frac {d y}{d x}), \quad$整理，得$\frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {f ^ {\prime}}{1 - f ^ {\prime}}.\tag{*}$

式（\*）两边，再同时对 $x$ 求导，得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} & = \frac {d}{d x} \left(\frac {d y}{d x}\right) = \left(\frac {f ^ {\prime}}{1 - f ^ {\prime}}\right) _ {x} ^ {l} \\ & = \frac {(1 - f ^ {\prime}) (1 + y ^ {\prime}) f ^ {\prime \prime} + (1 + y ^ {\prime}) f ^ {\prime} \cdot f ^ {\prime \prime}}{(1 - f ^ {\prime}) ^ {2}} \\ & = \frac {f ^ {\prime \prime}}{(1 - f ^ {\prime}) ^ {3}}. \end{array}
$$
