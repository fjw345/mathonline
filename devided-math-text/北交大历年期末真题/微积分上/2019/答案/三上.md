$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int \frac {\arctan \frac {1}{x}}{1 + x ^ {2}} d x & = \int \arctan \frac {1}{x} d \arctan x \\ & = \arctan \frac {1}{x} \cdot \arctan x - \int \arctan x d \arctan \frac {1}{x} \\ & = \arctan \frac {1}{x} \cdot \arctan x + \int \arctan x d \arctan x \\ & = \arctan \frac {1}{x} \cdot \arctan x + \frac {1}{2} (\arctan x) ^ {2} + C. \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int \frac {\arctan \frac {1}{x}}{1 + x ^ {2}} d x & = \int \frac {\arctan u}{1 + \frac {1}{u ^ {2}}} \cdot \frac {- 1}{u ^ {2}} d u = - \int \frac {\arctan u}{1 + u ^ {2}} d u \\ & = - \int \arctan u d \arctan u = - \frac {1}{2} (\arctan u) ^ {2} + C \\ & = - \frac {1}{2} (\arctan \frac {1}{x}) ^ {2} + C. \end{array}
$$

另2: $\int \frac{\arctan\frac{1}{x}}{1 + x^2} dx = \int \frac{\frac{\pi}{2} - \arctan x}{1 + x^2} dx = \int (\frac{\pi}{2} -\arctan x)d\arctan x$ $= \frac{\pi}{2}\arctan x - \frac{1}{2} (\arctan x)^{2} + C.$

另3: $\int \frac{\arctan\frac{1}{x}}{1 + x^2} dx = \int \frac{u}{\csc^2 u} (-\csc^2 u)du$ let $x = \cot u$ $= \int -udu = -\frac{u^2}{2} +C = -\frac{1}{2} (\operatorname {arccot}x)^2 +C.$

错解: $\int \frac{\arctan\frac{1}{x}}{1 + x^2} dx = \int \arctan \frac{1}{x} d\arctan x$ $= \arctan \frac{1}{x}\cdot \arctan x - \int \arctan xd\arctan \frac{1}{x}$ $= \arctan \frac{1}{x}\cdot \arctan x - \int \frac{\arctan\frac{1}{u}}{1 + u^2} du\qquad u = \frac{1}{x}$ $= \arctan \frac{1}{x}\cdot \arctan x - \int \frac{\arctan\frac{1}{x}}{1 + x^2} dx (*)$ 所以， $\int \frac{\arctan\frac{1}{x}}{1 + x^2} dx = \frac{1}{2}\arctan \frac{1}{x}\cdot \arctan x + C.$
