解:令 $F(x) = f(x)\cdot g(x)$ ，则

$$
F ^ {\prime} (x) = f ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot g (x) + f (x) \cdot g ^ {\prime} (x),
$$

$$
\boldsymbol {F} ^ {\prime \prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) = \boldsymbol {f} ^ {\prime \prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) \cdot \boldsymbol {g} (\boldsymbol {x}) + \boldsymbol {f} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) \cdot \boldsymbol {g} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) + \boldsymbol {f} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) \cdot \boldsymbol {g} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) + \boldsymbol {f} (\boldsymbol {x}) \cdot \boldsymbol {g} ^ {\prime \prime} (\boldsymbol {x}),
$$

$$
\boldsymbol {F} ^ {\prime} \left(\boldsymbol {x} _ {0}\right) = 0, \quad \boldsymbol {F} ^ {\prime \prime} \left(\boldsymbol {x} _ {0}\right) = 2 \boldsymbol {f} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) \cdot \boldsymbol {g} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {x}) > 0
$$

故选（C）.
