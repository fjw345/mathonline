(5) 利用分部积分, 得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {1} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\ln x}{(1 + x) ^ {2}} \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {1} ^ {+ \infty} \ln x \mathrm{d} \left(- \frac {1}{1 + x}\right) = - \frac {\ln x}{1 + x} \Big | _ {1} ^ {+ \infty} + \int_ {1} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{x (1 + x)} \\ & = \int_ {1} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{x (1 + x)} = \int_ {1} ^ {+ \infty} \left(\frac {1}{x} - \frac {1}{x + 1}\right) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = \ln \frac {x}{x + 1} \Big | _ {1} ^ {+ \infty} = \ln 2. \end{array}
$$
