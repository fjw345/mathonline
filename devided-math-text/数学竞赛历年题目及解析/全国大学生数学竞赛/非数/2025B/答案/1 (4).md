(4) 对方程两边关于 $x$ 求偏导数，得

$$
z + x \frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = 2 f _ {u} ^ {\prime} + y f _ {v} ^ {\prime} \left(z + x \frac {\partial z}{\partial x}\right).
$$

由此解得

$$
\frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac {1}{x} \left(\frac {2 f _ {u} ^ {\prime}}{1 - y f _ {v} ^ {\prime}} - z\right).
$$

注意到 $x = 1, y = 0$ 时，$z = f(2,0) = 3, f_u'(2,0) = 5,$ 所以

$$
\left|\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{\substack{x = 1\\ y = 0}} = 2f_{u}^{\prime}(2,0) - 3 = 7.
$$
