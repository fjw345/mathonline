【解】利用归纳法易知 $a_{n} > 0 (n \geq 1)$. 由于

$\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = (n+1)\left(1 + \frac{1}{a_n}\right) = (n+1) + (n+1)\frac{1}{a_n} = (n+1) + (n+1)\left(n + n\frac{1}{a_{n-1}}\right)$

$= (n+1) + (n+1)n + (n+1)n\frac{1}{a_{n-1}},$ …… 4 分如此递推，得 $\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = (n+1)!\left(\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{1}{k!} + \frac{1}{a_1}\right) = (n+1)!\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{1}{k!},$ …… 3 分
