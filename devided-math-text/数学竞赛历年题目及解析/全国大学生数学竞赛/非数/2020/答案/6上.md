【解】 $\int_0^m\cos \frac{2\pi n[x + 1]}{m}\mathrm{d}x = \sum_{k = 1}^{m}\int_{k - 1}^{k}\cos \frac{2\pi n[x + 1]}{m}\mathrm{d}x = \sum_{k = 1}^{m}\int_{k - 1}^{k}\cos \frac{2\pi nk}{m} dx = \sum_{k = 1}^{m}\cos k\frac{2\pi n}{m}.$ 如果 $m$ 是 $n$ 的因子，那么 $\int_0^m\cos \frac{2\pi n[x + 1]}{m}\mathrm{d}x = m$；否则，根据三角恒等式$\sum_{k = 1}^{m}\cos kt = \cos \frac{m + 1}{2} t\cdot \frac{\sin\frac{mt}{2}}{\sin\frac{t}{2}},$
