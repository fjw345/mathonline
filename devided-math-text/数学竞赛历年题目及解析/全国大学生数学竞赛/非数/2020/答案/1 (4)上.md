【解】令 $u = x + y$ ，得

$$
I = \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\sin (x + y)}{x + y} \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {x} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\sin u}{u} \mathrm{d} u
$$

$$
= \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\sin x}{x} \mathrm{d} x \left(\int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {\sin u}{u} \mathrm{d} u - \int_ {0} ^ {x} \frac {\sin u}{u} \mathrm{d} u\right)
$$
