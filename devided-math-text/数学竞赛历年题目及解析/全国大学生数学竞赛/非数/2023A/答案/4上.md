解法1. 记 $\Sigma = \{(x,y,z) | z = 0, x^2 + y^2 \leqslant R^2\}$, 取下侧. 由Gauss公式知

$$
\iint_ {S + \Sigma} (x ^ {2} - x) \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z + (y ^ {2} - y) \mathrm{d} z \mathrm{d} x + (z ^ {2} - z) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \iiint_ {\Omega} (2 (x + y + z) - 3) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z,
$$

其中，$\Omega = \{(x,y,z) | z \geqslant 0, x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \leqslant R^2\}$.

(4分)

由于，

$$
\iint_ {\Sigma} (x ^ {2} - x) \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z + (y ^ {2} - y) \mathrm{d} z \mathrm{d} x + (z ^ {2} - z) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = 0.
$$

所以，

$$
I = \iiint_ {\Omega} (2 (x + y + z) - 3) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z.
$$

(8 分)

由对称性，

$$
I = \iiint_ {\Omega} (2 z - 3) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z.
$$

用球坐标变换 $x = r\cos \theta \sin \varphi, y = r\sin \theta \sin \varphi, z = r\cos \varphi,$ 得

$I = \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} \mathrm{d} \theta \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d} \varphi \int_ {0} ^ {R} (2 r \cos \varphi - 3) r ^ {2} \sin \varphi \mathrm{d} r = 2 \pi \left(\frac {R ^ {4}}{4} - R ^ {3}\right)\tag{14$分$}$

注:最后一步直接利用直角坐标计算也可以.

解法2. $S$ 的参数方程为

$$
x = R \cos \theta \sin \varphi , y = R \sin \theta \sin \varphi , z = R \cos \varphi , (0 \leqslant \varphi \leqslant \frac {\pi}{2}, 0 \leqslant \theta \leqslant 2 \pi).
$$

曲面 $S$ 上的点 $(x,y,z)$ 处的单位法向为 $\frac{(x,y,z)}{R}$. 故,

$$
\begin{array}{r l} I & = \iint_ {S} (x ^ {2} - x, y ^ {2} - y, z ^ {2} - z) \cdot \frac {(x , y , z)}{R} \mathrm{d} S \\ & = \frac {1}{R} \iint_ {S} \left[ x ^ {3} + y ^ {3} + z ^ {3} - (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}) \right] \mathrm{d} S \\ & = \frac {1}{R} \iint_ {S} \left[ x ^ {3} + y ^ {3} + z ^ {3} - R ^ {2} \right] \mathrm{d} S. \end{array}
$$
