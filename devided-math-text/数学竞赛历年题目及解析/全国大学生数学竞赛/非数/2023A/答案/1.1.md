(1) 使用洛必达法则，得

$$
\lim _ {x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {3} + 9} - 6}{2 - \sqrt {x ^ {3} - 2 3}} = \lim _ {x \to 3} \frac {\frac {3 x ^ {2}}{2 \sqrt {x ^ {3} + 9}}}{- \frac {3 x ^ {2}}{2 \sqrt {x ^ {3} - 2 3}}} = - \lim _ {x \to 3} \frac {\sqrt {x ^ {3} - 2 3}}{\sqrt {x ^ {3} + 9}} = - \frac {1}{3}.
$$
