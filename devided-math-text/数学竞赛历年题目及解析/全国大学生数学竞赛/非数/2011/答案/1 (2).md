解:若 $\theta = 0$ ，则 $\lim_{n\to \infty}a_n = 1.$

若 $\theta \neq 0$ ，则当 n 充分大，使得 $2^{n} > |k|$ 时，

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {a _ {n} = \cos \frac {\theta}{2} \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {2}} \dots \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}} = \cos \frac {\theta}{2} \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {2}} \dots \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}} \cdot \sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}} \cdot \frac {1}{\sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}}}} \\ & {\quad = \cos \frac {\theta}{2} \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {2}} \dots \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n - 1}} \cdot \frac {1}{2} \sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n - 1}} \cdot \frac {1}{\sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}}}.} \\ & {\quad = \cos \frac {\theta}{2} \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {2}} \dots \cdot \cos \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n - 2}} \cdot \frac {1}{2 ^ {2}} \sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n - 2}} \cdot \frac {1}{\sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}}} = \frac {\sin \theta}{2 ^ {n} \sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}}}} \\ & {\text {这时,} \qquad \lim _ {n \to \infty} a _ {n} = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {\sin \theta}{2 ^ {n} \sin \frac {\theta}{2 ^ {n}}} = \frac {\sin \theta}{\theta}.} \end{array}\tag{4分}
$$
