解:因为

$$
\frac {\left(1 + x\right) ^ {\frac {2}{x}} - e ^ {2} \left(1 - \ln (1 + x)\right)}{x} = \frac {e ^ {\frac {2}{x} \ln (1 + x)} - e ^ {2} \left(1 - \ln (1 + x)\right)}{x},
$$

$\lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {e ^ {2} \ln (1 + x)}{x} = e ^ {2},\tag{3$分$}$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {e ^ {\frac {2}{x} \ln (1 + x)} - e ^ {2}}{x} = e ^ {2} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {e ^ {\frac {2}{x} \ln (1 + x) - 2}}{x} = e ^ {2} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\frac {2}{x} \ln (1 + x) - 2}{x} \\ = 2 e ^ {2} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\ln (1 + x) - x}{x ^ {2}} = 2 e ^ {2} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\frac {1}{1 + x} - 1}{2 x} = - e ^ {2}, \end{array}\tag{5分}
$$

所以

$\lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {(1 + x) ^ {\frac {2}{x}} - e ^ {2} (1 - \ln (1 + x))}{x} = 0.\tag{……6$分$}$
