- 解:设 $D_{1} = \{(x,y)\mid 0\leq x\leq \frac{1}{2},0\leq y\leq 2\}$
- $D_{2} = \{(x,y)\mid \frac{1}{2}\leq x\leq 2,0\leq y\leq \frac{1}{x}\}$
- $D_{3} = \{(x,y)\mid \frac{1}{2}\leq x\leq 2,\frac{1}{x}\leq y\leq 2\} .$ ……2分
- $\iint_{D_1\cup D_2}dxdy = 1 + \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{2}\frac{dx}{x} = 1 + 2\ln 2,\iint_{D_3}dxdy = 3 - 2\ln 2.$ ……4分
- $\iint_{D}\mathrm{sgn}(xy - 1)dxdy = \iint_{D_3}dxdy - \iint_{D_2\cup D_3}dxdy = 2 - 4\ln 2.$ ……6分
