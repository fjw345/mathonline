解: $du(x,y) = \frac{ydx - xdy}{3x^2 - 2xy + 3y^2} = \frac{\frac{d(\frac{x}{y})}{3(\frac{x}{y})^2 - \frac{2x}{y} + 3}}{3(\frac{x}{y})^2 - \frac{2x}{y} + 3} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}} d\arctan \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}} (\frac{x}{y} -\frac{1}{3}).$ 所以， $u(x,y) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\arctan \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}} (\frac{x}{y} -\frac{1}{3}) + C.$
