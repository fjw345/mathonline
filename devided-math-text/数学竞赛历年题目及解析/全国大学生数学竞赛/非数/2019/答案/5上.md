证明:由 $f(x)$ 为仅有正实根的多项式，不妨设 $f(x)$ 的全部根为 $0 < a_{1} < a_{2} < \cdots < a_{k}$，这样，

$$
f (x) = A (x - a _ {1}) ^ {r _ {1}} \dots (x - a _ {k}) ^ {r _ {k}},
$$

其中 $r_{i}$ 为对应根 $a_{i}$ 的重数 ( $i=1,\cdots,k,r_{k}\geq1$ ). ----2 分

$$
f ^ {\prime} (x) = A r _ {1} \left(x - a _ {1}\right) ^ {r _ {1} - 1} \dots \left(x - a _ {k}\right) ^ {r _ {k}} + \dots + A r _ {k} \left(x - a _ {1}\right) ^ {r _ {1}} \dots \left(x - a _ {k}\right) ^ {r _ {k} - 1},
$$

所以， $f'(x)=f(x)\left(\frac{r_{1}}{x-a_{1}}+\cdots+\frac{r_{k}}{x-a_{k}}\right)$ ，从而， $-\frac{f'(x)}{f(x)}=\frac{r_{1}}{a_{1}}\cdot\frac{1}{1-\frac{x}{a_{1}}}+\cdots+\frac{r_{k}}{a_{k}}\cdot\frac{1}{1-\frac{x}{a_{k}}}$ .

------6分若 $|x|<a_{1}$ ，则

$$
- \frac {f ^ {\prime} (x)}{f (x)} = \frac {r _ {1}}{a _ {1}} \cdot \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \left(\frac {x}{a _ {1}}\right) ^ {n} + \dots + \frac {r _ {k}}{a _ {k}} \cdot \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \left(\frac {x}{a _ {k ^ {\prime}}}\right) ^ {n} = \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \left(\frac {r _ {1}}{a _ {1} n + 1} + \dots + \frac {r _ {k}}{a _ {k} n + 1}\right) x ^ {n}.
$$

而 $-\frac{f'(x)}{f(x)} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_n x^n$，由幂级数的唯一性知

$c _ {n} = \frac {r _ {1}}{a _ {1} n + 1} + \dots + \frac {r _ {n + 1}}{a _ {k} n + 1} > 0,\tag{----9$分$}$

$$
\frac {c _ {n}}{c _ {n + 1}} = \frac {\frac {r _ {1}}{a _ {1} n + 1} + \cdots + \frac {r _ {k}}{a _ {k} n + 1}}{\frac {r _ {1}}{a _ {1} n + 2} + \cdots + \frac {r _ {k}}{a _ {k} n + 2}} = a _ {1} \cdot \frac {r _ {1} + \cdots + \left(\frac {a _ {1}}{a _ {k}}\right) ^ {n + 1} r _ {k}}{r _ {1} + \cdots + \left(\frac {a _ {1}}{a _ {k}}\right) ^ {n + 2} r _ {k}}.
$$

$\lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {c _ {n}}{c _ {n + 1}} = a _ {1} \cdot \frac {r _ {1} + 0 + \cdots + 0}{r _ {1} + 0 + \cdots + 0} = a _ {1} > 0, \quad \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {c _ {n + 1}}{c _ {n}} = \frac {1}{a _ {1}},\tag{----12$分$}$
