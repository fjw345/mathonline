解:当 $t = 0$ 时，$x = 1, y = 0$，对 $x = t + \cos t$ 两边关于 $t$ 求导:$\frac{dx}{dt} = 1 - \sin t$，$\left.\frac{dx}{dt}\right|_{t=0} = 1$，对 $e^y + ty + \sin t = 1$ 两边关于 $t$ 求导:$e^y \frac{dy}{dt} + y + t \frac{dy}{dt} + \cos t = 0$，$\left.\frac{dy}{dt}\right|_{t=0} = -1$，则 $\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{t=0} = -1$。所以，切线方程为 $y - 0 = -(x - 1)$。
