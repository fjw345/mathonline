解 由于 $\left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^{\alpha} < \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)$, 则 $(n + 1)^{\alpha} - n^{\alpha} = n^{\alpha} \left( \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^{\alpha} - 1 \right) < n^{\alpha} \left( \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right) - 1 \right) = \frac{1}{n^{1 - \alpha}}$, 于是 $0 < (n + 1)^{\alpha} - n^{\alpha} < \frac{1}{n^{1 - \alpha}}$, 应用两边夹法则, $\lim_{n\to +\infty}((n + 1)^{\alpha} - n^{\alpha}) = 0$.
