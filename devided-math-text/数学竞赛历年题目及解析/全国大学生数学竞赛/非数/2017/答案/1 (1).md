解:在方程两边求导得

$$
f ^ {\prime} (x) \cos x + f (x) \sin x = 1, f ^ {\prime} (x) + f (x) \tan x = \sec x.
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\text {从而} f (x) = e ^ {- \int \tan x d x} \left(\int \sec x e ^ {\int \tan x d x} d x + c\right)} \\ & {\qquad = e ^ {- \ln \cos x} \left(\int \frac {1}{\cos x} e ^ {- \ln \cos x} d x + c\right) = \cos x \left(\int \frac {1}{\cos^ {2} x} d x + c\right)} \\ & {\qquad = \cos x (\tan x + c) = \sin x + c \cos x} \end{array}
$$

由于 $f(0)=1$ ，故 $f(x)=\sin x+\cos x$ 。
