## 继续求导得

$$
3 y ^ {\prime \prime} \mathrm{e} ^ {3 y} + 9 (y ^ {\prime}) ^ {2} \mathrm{e} ^ {3 y} + y ^ {\prime \prime} \cos (x + y) ^ {2} - 2 (x + y) (1 + y ^ {\prime}) ^ {2} \sin (x + y) ^ {2} = 0.
$$

将 $x = 0, y = 0$ 分别代入上面两式，得

$$
\left| y ^ {\prime} (x) \right| _ {(0, 0)} = - \frac {1}{4}, \quad \left. y ^ {\prime \prime} (x) \right| _ {(0, 0)} = - \frac {9}{6 4}.
$$
