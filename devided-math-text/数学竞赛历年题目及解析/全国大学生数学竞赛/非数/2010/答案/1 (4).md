解 因为 $\frac{\partial r}{\partial x} = \frac{x}{r}, \frac{\partial r}{\partial y} = \frac{y}{r}$，所以

$\frac{\partial g}{\partial x} = -\frac{x}{r^3} f'(\frac{1}{r})$，$\frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x^2} = \frac{x^2}{r^6} f''(\frac{1}{r}) + \frac{2x^2 - y^2}{r^5} f'(\frac{1}{r}).$

利用对称性，$\frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 g}{\partial y^2} = \frac{1}{r^4} f''(\frac{1}{r}) + \frac{1}{r^3} f'(\frac{1}{r})$
