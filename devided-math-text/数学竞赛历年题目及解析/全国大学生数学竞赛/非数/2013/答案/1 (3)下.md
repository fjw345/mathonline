$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 0 \\ y = - 1 \end{array} \right. \quad \text {和} \quad \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = - 2 \\ y = 1 \end{array} \right..\tag{2分}
$$

又

$$
y ^ {\prime \prime} = \frac {(2y^{2} - x^{2})(2x + 2xy' + 2y) + (x^{2} + 2xy)(4yy' - 2x)}{(2y^{2} - x^{2})^{2}}\Bigg|_{\substack{x = 0\\ y = -1\\ y^{\prime} = 0}} = -1 < 0,
$$

$$
y ^ {\prime \prime} \bigg | _ { \begin{array}{c} x = - 2 \\ y = 1 \\ y ^ {\prime} = 0 \end{array} } = 1 > 0.
$$

故 $y(0)=-1$ 为极大值， $y(-2)=1$ 为极小值.
