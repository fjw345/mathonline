解:(1) 记 $a_{n}=1+\frac{1}{2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}$ ， $u_{n}=\frac{a_{n}}{(n+1)(n+2)}$ ， $n=1,2,3,\cdots$ .

因为 $n$ 充分大时

$0 < a _ {n} = 1 + \frac {1}{2} + \dots + \frac {1}{n} < 1 + \int_ {1} ^ {n} \frac {1}{x} d x = 1 + \ln n < \sqrt {n},\tag{3$分$}$

所以 $u_{n} \leq \frac{\sqrt{n}}{(n + 1)(n + 2)} < \frac{1}{n^{3 / 2}}$ 而 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{3 / 2}}$ 收敛，所以 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} u_n$ 收敛.

(2分)

(2) $a_{k} = 1 + \frac{1}{2} +\dots +\frac{1}{k} (k = 1,2,\dots)$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} S _ {n} & = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \frac {1 + \frac {1}{2} + \cdots + \frac {1}{k}}{(k + 1) (k + 2)} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \frac {a _ {k}}{(k + 1) (k + 2)} = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \left(\frac {a _ {k}}{k + 1} - \frac {a _ {k}}{k + 2}\right) \\ & = \left(\frac {a _ {1}}{2} - \frac {a _ {1}}{3}\right) + \left(\frac {a _ {2}}{3} - \frac {a _ {2}}{4}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {a _ {n - 1}}{n} - \frac {a _ {n - 1}}{n + 1}\right) + \left(\frac {a _ {n}}{n + 1} - \frac {a _ {n}}{n + 2}\right) \end{array}\tag{2分}
$$

$= \frac {1}{2} a _ {1} + \frac {1}{3} (a _ {2} - a _ {1}) + \frac {1}{4} (a _ {3} - a _ {2}) + \dots + \frac {1}{n + 1} (a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1}) - \frac {1}{n + 2} a _ {n}\tag{2$分$}$

$= \left(\frac {1}{1 \cdot 2} + \frac {1}{2 \cdot 3} + \frac {1}{3 \cdot 4} + \dots + \frac {1}{n \cdot (n - 1)}\right) - \frac {1}{n + 2} a _ {n} = 1 - \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 2} a _ {n}.\tag{2$分$}$

因为 $0 < a_{n} < 1 + \ln n$

所以 $0 < \frac{a_n}{n + 2} < \frac{1 + \ln n}{n + 2}$ 且 $\lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{1 + \ln n}{n + 2} = 0$ ，所以 $\lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{a_n}{n + 2} = 0.$

于是 $S = \lim_{n\to \infty}S_n = 1 - 0 - 0 = 1$ ，证毕。

(3 分)
