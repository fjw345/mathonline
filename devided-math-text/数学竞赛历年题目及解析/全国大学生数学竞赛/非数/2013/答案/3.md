证 由于 $f(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续，且 $\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{f(x)}{x} = 0$，

则

$f (0) = \lim _ {x \to 0} f (x) = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f (x)}{x} \cdot x = 0,\tag{2$分$}$

$f ^ {\prime} (0) = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f (x) - f (0)}{x - 0} = 0.\tag{2$分$}$

应用罗比达法则，

$\lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f (x)}{x ^ {2}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f ^ {\prime} (x)}{2 x} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f ^ {\prime} (x) - f ^ {\prime} (0)}{2 (x - 0)} = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime \prime} (0).\tag{3$分$}$

所以

$$
\varliminf_ {n \to 0} \frac {\left| f \left(\frac {1}{n}\right) \right|}{\frac {1}{\overline {{n ^ {2}}}}} = \frac {1}{2} \big | f ^ {\prime \prime} (0) \big |.\tag{2分}
$$

由于级数 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2}$ 收敛，从而 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left| f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) \right|$ 收敛.

(3分)
