证明 由 $f(x) = f(x + 2)$ 知 $f(x)$ 为以 2 为周期的周期函数，其 Fourier 系数分别为:

$$
a _ {n} = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} f (x) \cos n \pi x \mathrm{d} x \quad b _ {n} = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} f (x) \sin n \pi x \mathrm{d} x
$$

由 $f(x) = f(x + \sqrt{3})$ 知

$$
\begin{array}{l} a _ {n} = \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} f (x + \sqrt {3}) \cos n \pi x \mathrm{d} x \\ \quad = \int_ {- 1 + \sqrt {3}} ^ {1 + \sqrt {3}} f (t) \cos n \pi (t - \sqrt {3}) \mathrm{d} t \\ \quad = \int_ {- 1 + \sqrt {3}} ^ {1 + \sqrt {3}} f (t) \big (\cos n \pi t \cos \sqrt {3} n \pi + \sin n \pi t \sin \sqrt {3} n \pi \big) \mathrm{d} t \\ \quad = \cos \sqrt {3} n \pi \int_ {- 1 + \sqrt {3}} ^ {1 + \sqrt {3}} f (t) \cos n \pi t \mathrm{d} t + \sin \sqrt {3} n \pi \int_ {- 1 + \sqrt {3}} ^ {1 + \sqrt {3}} f (t) \sin n \pi t \mathrm{d} t \\ \quad = \cos \sqrt {3} n \pi \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} f (t) \cos n \pi t \mathrm{d} t + \sin \sqrt {3} n \pi \int_ {- 1} ^ {1} f (t) \sin n \pi t \mathrm{d} t \end{array}
$$

所以 $a_{n} = a_{n}\cos \sqrt{3} n\pi +b_{n}\sin \sqrt{3} n\pi$

同理可得 $b_{n} = b_{n}\cos \sqrt{3} n\pi +a_{n}\sin \sqrt{3} n\pi$

联立

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} a _ {n} & = a _ {n} \cos \sqrt {3} n \pi + b _ {n} \sin \sqrt {3} n \pi \\ b _ {n} & = b _ {n} \cos \sqrt {3} n \pi + a _ {n} \sin \sqrt {3} n \pi \end{array} \right.
$$
