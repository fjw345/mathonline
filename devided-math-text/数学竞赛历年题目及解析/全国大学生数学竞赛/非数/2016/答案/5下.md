由介值定理, 存在 $\xi \in (0,1)$ 使得 $F(\xi) = \frac{1}{2}$
在两个子区间 $(0,\xi)$ ， $(\xi,1)$ 分别应用拉格朗日中值定理:

$$
F ^ {\prime} (x _ {1}) = \frac {f (x _ {1})}{I} = \frac {F (\xi) - F (0)}{\xi - 0} = \frac {1 / 2}{\xi}, \quad x _ {1} \in (0, \xi)
$$

$$
F ^ {\prime} (x _ {2}) = \frac {f (x _ {2})}{I} = \frac {F (1) - F (\xi)}{1 - \xi} = \frac {1 / 2}{1 - \xi}, \quad x _ {2} \in (0, \xi)
$$

$$
\frac {I}{f (x _ {1})} + \frac {I}{f (x _ {2})} = \frac {1}{F ^ {\prime} (x _ {1})} + \frac {1}{F ^ {\prime} (x _ {2})} = \frac {\xi}{1 / 2} + \frac {1 - \xi}{1 / 2} = 2
$$
