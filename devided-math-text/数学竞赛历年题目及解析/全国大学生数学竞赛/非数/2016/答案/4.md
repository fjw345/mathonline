解 n 等分区间 $[0,1]$ ，分点为 $0 = x_{0} < x_{1} < \cdots < x_{n-1} < x_{n} = 1$ .

记 $h = \frac{1 - 0}{n} = \frac{1}{n}$ ，则 $x_{k} = 0 + kh = kh$ ，$x_{k} - x_{k-1} = h$ ，$k = 1,2,\cdots,n$ :

$$
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {n \to \infty} n \left(\int_ {0} ^ {1} f (x) \mathrm{d} x - \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} f \left(\frac {k}{n}\right)\right) \\ = \lim _ {n \to \infty} n \left(\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \int_ {x _ {k 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} f (x) \mathrm{d} x - \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} h f (x _ {k})\right) \\ = \lim _ {n \to \infty} n \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \int_ {x _ {k 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} \big (f (x) - f (x _ {k}) \big) \mathrm{d} x \\ = \lim _ {n \to \infty} n \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \int_ {x _ {k 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} \frac {f (x) - f (x _ {k})}{x - x _ {k}} \cdot (x - x _ {k}) \mathrm{d} x \\ = \lim _ {n \to \infty} n \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \frac {f (\xi_ {k}) - f (x _ {k})}{\xi_ {k} - x _ {k}} \cdot \int_ {x _ {k 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} (x - x _ {k}) \mathrm{d} x \quad \xi_ {k} \in (x _ {k - 1}, x _ {k}) \\ = \lim _ {n \to \infty} n \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} f ^ {\prime} (\eta_ {k}) \Big (- \frac {1}{2} (x _ {k} - x _ {k - 1}) ^ {2} \Big) \quad \eta_ {k} \in (\xi_ {k}, x _ {k}) \\ = - \frac {1}{2} \lim _ {n \to \infty} (1 - 0) \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} f ^ {\prime} (\eta_ {k}) h \\ = - \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {1} f ^ {\prime} (x) \mathrm{d} x \\ = \frac {1}{2} (f (1) - f (0)) = - \frac {1}{2} \end{array}
$$
