解:曲面 $z = x^{2} + y^{2} + 1$ 在点 $M(1, - 1,3)$ 的切平面: $2(x - 1) - 2(y + 1) - (z - 3) = 0$ 即 $z = 2x - 2y - 1$ 。联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}z = x^{2} + y^{2}\\ z = 2x - 2y - 1 \end{array} \right.$ 得到所围区域的投影 $D$ 为: $(x - 1)^2 +(y + 1)^2\leq 1$ 所求体积 $V = \iint_{D}[(2x - 2y - 1) - (x^2 +y^2)]dxdy = \iint_{D}[1 - (x - 1)^{2} - (y + 1)^{2}]dxdy$ 令 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x - 1 = r\cos t\\ y + 1 = r\sin t \end{array} \right., V = \int_0^{2\pi}dt\int_0^1 (1 - r^2)rdr = \frac{\pi}{2}$
