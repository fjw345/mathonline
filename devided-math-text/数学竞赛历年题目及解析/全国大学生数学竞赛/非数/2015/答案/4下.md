$$
\sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \frac {n ^ {3} + 2}{(n + 1) !} (x - 1) ^ {n} = \sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{(n - 2) !} (x - 1) ^ {n} + \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{n !} (x - 1) ^ {n} + \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{(n + 1) !} (x - 1) ^ {n}.
$$

7分用 $S_{1}(x)$ ， $S_{2}(x)$ 和 $S_{3}(x)$ 分别表示上式右端三个幂级数的和函数。依据 $e^x$ 的展开式得到

$$
S _ {1} (x) = (x - 1) ^ {2} \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{n !} (x - 1) ^ {n} = (x - 1) ^ {2} e ^ {x - 1}, \quad S _ {2} (x) = e ^ {x - 1}
$$

再由

$(x - 1)S_{3}(x) = \sum_{n = 0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(n + 1)!}(x - 1)^{n + 1} = \sum_{n = 1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n!}(x - 1)^{n} = e^{x - 1} - 1$ 得到，当 $x\neq 1$ 时 $S_{3}(x) = \frac{1}{x - 1} (e^{x - 1} - 1)$ 。10分又 $S_{3}(1) = 1$ 。12分综合以上讨论，最终得到所给幂级数的和函数

$$
S (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} (x ^ {2} - 2 x + 2) e ^ {x - 1} + \frac {1}{x - 1} (e ^ {x - 1} - 1), & x \neq 1 \\ 2 & x = 1 \end{array} \right.\tag{----14 分}
$$
