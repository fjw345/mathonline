证明:在点 $(0,0)$ 展开 $f(x,y)$ 得

$$
f (x, y) = \frac {1}{2} \left(x \frac {\partial}{\partial x} + y \frac {\partial}{\partial y}\right) ^ {2} f (\theta x, \theta y) = \frac {1}{2} \left(x ^ {2} \frac {\partial^ {2}}{\partial x ^ {2}} + 2 x y \frac {\partial^ {2}}{\partial x \partial y} + y ^ {2} \frac {\partial^ {2}}{\partial y ^ {2}}\right) f (\theta x, \theta y),
$$

其中 $\theta \in (0,1)$ 。

------6分记 $(u,v,w) = \left(\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2},\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x\partial y},\frac{\partial^2}{\partial y^2}\right)f(\theta x,\theta y)$ ，则

$f (x, y) = \frac {1}{2} \Big (u x ^ {2} + 2 v x y + w y ^ {2} \Big)$。

由于 $\| (u,\sqrt{2} v,w)\| = \sqrt{u^2 + 2v^2 + w^2}\leq \sqrt{M}$ 以及 $\| (x^2,\sqrt{2} xy,y^2)\| = x^2 +y^2$ ，我们有

$$
\mid (u, \sqrt {2} v, w) \cdot (x ^ {2}, \sqrt {2} x y, y ^ {2}) \mid \leq \sqrt {M} \left(x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}\right),
$$

即

$\mid f (x, y) \mid \leq \frac {1}{2} \sqrt {M} \left(x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}\right)$。

13分从而

$$
\left| \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1} f (x, y) d x d y \right| \leq \frac {\sqrt {M}}{2} \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) d x d y = \frac {\pi \sqrt {M}}{4}.
$$
