[解] 由于 $u^{2}(x) = \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-xt^{2}} dt \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-xs^{2}} ds = \iint_{s \geq 0, t \geq 0} e^{-x(s^{2} + t^{2})} ds dt$，故有

$u^{2}(x) = \int_{0}^{\pi / 2} d\varphi \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-x\rho^{2}} \rho d\rho = \frac{\pi}{4x} \int_{0}^{+\infty} e^{-x\rho^{2}} d_{\rho}(x\rho^{2}) = -\left.\frac{\pi}{4x} e^{-x\rho^{2}}\right|_{\rho=0}^{\rho = +\infty} = \frac{\pi}{4x}$。所以 $u(x) = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2\sqrt{x}}$。
