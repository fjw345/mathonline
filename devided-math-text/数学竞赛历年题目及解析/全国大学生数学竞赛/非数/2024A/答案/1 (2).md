(2) 利用极坐标变换 $x = \rho \cos \theta, y = \rho \sin \theta$, 则 $\iint_{D} (\mathrm{e}^{x^2 + y^2} - 1) \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = 2\pi \int_0^r (\mathrm{e}^{\rho^2} - 1) \rho \, \mathrm{d}\rho = \pi'(\mathrm{e}^{r^2} - 1 - r^2).$

注意, $r \to 0^+$ 时, $\mathrm{e}^{r^2} - 1 - r^2 = \frac{r^4}{2} + o(r^4)$, 故原式 $= \frac{\pi}{2}$.
