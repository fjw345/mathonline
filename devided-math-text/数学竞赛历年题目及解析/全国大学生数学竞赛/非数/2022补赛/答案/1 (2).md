【解】因为 $ne^{x} + i \leq \sqrt{(ne^{x} + i)(ne^{x} + i + 1)} \leq ne^{x} + i + 1$ ，所以

$$
\mathrm{e} ^ {x} + \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \frac {i}{n} \leq \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \sqrt {(n \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + i) (n \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + i + 1)} \leq \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \frac {i}{n} + \frac {1}{n},
$$

根据定积分的定义，得

$$
\lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \frac {i}{n} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x \mathrm{d} x = \frac {1}{2}.
$$

利用夹逼准则，所求极限为

$$
\lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \sqrt {(n \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + i) (n \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + i + 1)} = \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + \frac {1}{2}.
$$
