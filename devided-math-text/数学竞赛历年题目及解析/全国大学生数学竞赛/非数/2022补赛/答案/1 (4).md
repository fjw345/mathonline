【解】由于区域关于 $x$ 轴和 $y$ 轴对称，所以 $\iint_{D} x \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = 0$，$\iint_{D} y \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = 0$，且 $\iint_{D} x^2 \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = 4 \iint_{D_1} x^2 \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y$，其中 $D_1 = \{(x, y) | x^2 + y^2 \leq 1, x \geq 0, y \geq 0\}$，于是

$\iint_{D} (x + y - x^2) \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = -4 \iint_{D_1} x^2 \, \mathrm{d}x \, \mathrm{d}y = -4 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^2 \theta \, \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{1} r^3 \, \mathrm{d}r$

$= -\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1 + \cos 2\theta}{2} \, \mathrm{d}\theta = -\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos 2\theta \, \mathrm{d}\theta = -\frac{\pi}{4}$.
