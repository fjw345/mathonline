【解】根据复合函数求导法则，得 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = f'(u)\frac{x}{u},\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = f'(u)\frac{y}{u},$ $\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x^2} = f''(u)\frac{x^2}{u^2} +f'(u)\frac{y^2}{u^3},\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial y^2} = f''(u)\frac{y^2}{u^2} +f'(u)\frac{x^2}{u^3}.$ 4分代入题设方程并整理，得到不显含未知函数 $f(u)$ 的二阶微分方程$f^{\prime \prime}(u) + \frac{1}{u} f^{\prime}(u) = u^{2}.$
