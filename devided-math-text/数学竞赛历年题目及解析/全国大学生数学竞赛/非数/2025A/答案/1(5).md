(5) 根据对称性，得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int_ {L} (x ^ {2} + 2 x y) \mathrm{d} s = \frac {1}{3} \int_ {L} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} s + \frac {2}{3} \int_ {L} (x y + y z + z x) \mathrm{d} s \\ = \frac {1}{3} \int_ {L} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} s + \frac {1}{3} \int_ {L} ((x + y + z) ^ {2} - (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2})) \mathrm{d} s \\ = \frac {1}{3} \int_ {L} (x + y + z) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} s = 6 \pi . \end{array}
$$
