又由于 $|\sin nx|\leq 1$ ，及 $\sin x\geq \frac{2}{\pi} x$ （ $0\leq x\leq \frac{\pi}{2}$ ），

所以当 $n > 1$ 时，得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} x \left(\frac {\sin n x}{\sin x}\right) ^ {4} \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2 n}} x \left(\frac {\sin n x}{\sin x}\right) ^ {4} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {\frac {\pi}{2 n}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} x \left(\frac {\sin n x}{\sin x}\right) ^ {4} \mathrm{d} x \\ & \leq n ^ {4} \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2 n}} x \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {\frac {\pi}{2 n}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} x \left(\frac {1}{2 x / \pi}\right) ^ {4} \mathrm{d} x = \frac {n ^ {4}}{2} \left(\frac {\pi}{2 n}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {\pi^ {4}}{1 6} \int_ {\frac {\pi}{2 n}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \frac {\mathrm{d} x}{x ^ {3}} \\ & = \frac {n ^ {2} \pi^ {2}}{8} + \frac {\pi^ {4}}{1 6} \cdot \frac {1}{(- 2 x ^ {2})} \Bigg | _ {\frac {\pi}{2 n}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} = \frac {n ^ {2} \pi^ {2}}{8} - \frac {\pi^ {4}}{1 6} \left(\frac {2}{\pi^ {2}} - \frac {2 n ^ {2}}{\pi^ {2}}\right) = \left(\frac {n ^ {2}}{4} - \frac {1}{8}\right) \pi^ {2}. \end{array}
$$

当 $n = 1$ 时， $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}}x\mathrm{d}x = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$ ，等号成立.
