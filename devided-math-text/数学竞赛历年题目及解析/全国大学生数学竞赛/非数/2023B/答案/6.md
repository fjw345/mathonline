解答. 由分部积分法

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {0} ^ {1} f ^ {2} (x) \mathrm{d} x & = (x - 1) f ^ {2} (x) \Big | _ {0} ^ {1} - \int_ {0} ^ {1} (x - 1) 2 f (x) f ^ {\prime} (x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {1} (1 - x) f ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot f (x) \mathrm{d} x. \end{array}
$$

(4分)

由 Cauchy 积分不等式, 有

$$
\int_ {0} ^ {1} (1 - x) f ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot f (x) \mathrm{d} x \leqslant \left(\int_ {0} ^ {1} (1 - x) ^ {2} (f ^ {\prime} (x)) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} \left(\int_ {0} ^ {1} f ^ {2} (x) \mathrm{d} x\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}}.
$$

于是

$$
\int_ {0} ^ {1} f ^ {2} (x) \mathrm{d} x \leqslant 4 \int_ {0} ^ {1} (1 - x) ^ {2} | f ^ {\prime} (x) | ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x.
$$

(10分)

等式成立时应有常数 $c$ 使得 $(1 - x)f'(x) = cf(x)$. 因此当 $x \in (0,1)$ 时, 有

$$
((1 - x) ^ {c} f (x)) ^ {\prime} = (1 - x) ^ {c - 1} \bigl ((1 - x) f ^ {\prime} (x) - c f (x) \bigr) = 0.
$$

因而存在常数 $d$ 使得 $f(x) = d(1 - x)^{-c} (0 < x < 1)$. 当 $x \to 0$ 时，$f(x) \to 0$, 故 $d = 0$. 于是 $f = 0$. 所以使得题中不等式成为等式的函数是 $f(x) = 0$. (14分)
