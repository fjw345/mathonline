【证】记 $x_{k} = a + \frac{k(b - a)}{n}$，$\xi_{k} = a + \frac{(2k - 1)(b - a)}{2n}$，$k = 1, 2, \dots, n$。将 $f(x)$ 在 $[x_{k-1}, x_k]$ 上展开成泰勒公式，得

$$
f (x) = f (\xi_ {k}) + f ^ {\prime} (\xi_ {k}) (x - \xi_ {k}) + \frac {f ^ {\prime \prime} (\eta_ {k})}{2} (x - \xi_ {k}) ^ {2},
$$

其中 $x \in [x_{k-1}, x_k]$，$\eta_k$ 介于0和 $x$ 之间。于是

---- 4 分

$$
\begin{array}{l} B _ {n} = \int_ {a} ^ {b} f (x) \mathrm{d} x - \frac {b - a}{n} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} f \left(a + \frac {2 k - 1}{2 n} (b - a)\right) = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \int_ {x _ {k - 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} \left(f (x) - f (\xi_ {k})\right) \mathrm{d} x \\ = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \int_ {x _ {k - 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} \left[ f ^ {\prime} (\xi_ {k}) (x - \xi_ {k}) + \frac {f ^ {\prime \prime} (\eta_ {k})}{2} (x - \xi_ {k}) ^ {2} \right] \mathrm{d} x = \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \int_ {x _ {k - 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} f ^ {\prime \prime} (\eta_ {k}) (x - \xi_ {k}) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x. \end{array}
$$

设 $f''(x)$ 在 $[x_{k - 1},x_k]$ 上的最大值和最小值分别为 $M_{k}$ ， $m_{k}$ ，因为

$$
\int_ {x _ {k - 1}} ^ {x _ {k}} \left(x - \xi_ {k}\right) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x = \frac {(b - a) ^ {3}}{1 2 n ^ {3}},
$$

所以

$\frac {(b - a) ^ {2}}{2 4} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} m _ {k} \frac {b - a}{n} \leq n ^ {2} B _ {n} \leq \frac {(b - a) ^ {2}}{2 4} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} M _ {k} \frac {b - a}{n}.\tag{---- 6$分$}$

因为 $f''(x)$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续，所以 $f''(x)$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上可积. 根据定积分 $\int_0^1 f''(x)\mathrm{d}x$ 的定义，及牛顿-莱布尼兹公式，得

$$
\lim _ {n \to \infty} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} m _ {k} \frac {b - a}{n} = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} M _ {k} \frac {b - a}{n} = \int_ {a} ^ {b} f ^ {\prime \prime} (x) \mathrm{d} x = f ^ {\prime} (b) - f ^ {\prime} (a).
$$

再根据夹逼准则，得

$\lim _ {n \to \infty} n ^ {2} B _ {n} = \frac {(b - a) ^ {2}}{2 4} \Big [ f ^ {\prime} (b) - f ^ {\prime} (a) \Big ].\tag{4$分$}$
