【解】将方程两边分别关于 $x$ 和 $y$ 求偏导，得

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 \cos (x + 2 y - 3 z) \left(1 - 3 \frac {\partial z}{\partial x}\right) = 1 - 3 \frac {\partial z}{\partial x}, \\ 2 \cos (x + 2 y - 3 z) \left(2 - 3 \frac {\partial z}{\partial y}\right) = 2 - 3 \frac {\partial z}{\partial y}. \end{array} \right.
$$

按 $\cos(x+2y-3z)=\frac{1}{2}$ 和 $\neq\frac{1}{2}$ 两种情形，都可解得: $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}&=\frac{1}{3},\\ \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}&=\frac{2}{3}.\end{aligned}\right.$ 因此 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=1$ .
