【解】因为 $f(x,y,z)$ 为4次齐次函数，所以对 $\forall t\in R$ ，恒有

$$
f (t x, t y, t z) = t ^ {4} f (x, y, z).
$$

对上式两边关于 $t$ 求导，得

$$
x f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (t x, t y, t z) + y f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (t x, t y, t z) + z f _ {3} ^ {\prime} (t x, t y, t z) = 4 t ^ {3} f (x, y, z).
$$

取 $t = 1$ ，得

$x f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) + y f _ {y} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) + z f _ {z} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) = 4 f (x, y, z).\tag{-------- 5$分$}$

设曲面 $\Sigma$ 上点 $(x,y,z)$ 处的外法线方向的方向余弦为 $(\cos \alpha, \cos \beta, \cos \gamma)$，则 $\cos \alpha = x, \cos \beta = y, \cos \gamma = z$，因此 2 分

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\iint_ {\Sigma} f (x, y, z) \mathrm{d} S = \frac {1}{4} \iint_ {\Sigma} \left(x f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) + y f _ {y} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) + z f _ {z} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z)\right) \mathrm{d} S} \\ & {= \frac {1}{4} \iint_ {\Sigma} \left[ \cos \alpha f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) + \cos \beta f _ {y} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) + \cos \gamma f _ {z} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) \right] \mathrm{d} S} \\ & {= \frac {1}{4} \iint_ {\Sigma} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z + f _ {y} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) \mathrm{d} z \mathrm{d} x + f _ {z} ^ {\prime} (x, y, z) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y} \\ & {= \frac {1}{4} \iiint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2} \leq 1} \left[ f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y, z) + f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y, z) + f _ {z z} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y, z) \right] \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z (\text {利用高斯公式})} \\ & {= \frac {3}{2} \iiint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2} \leq 1} \left[ x ^ {2} \left(2 a _ {1} + a _ {4} + a _ {6}\right) + y ^ {2} \left(2 a _ {2} + a _ {4} + a _ {5}\right) + z ^ {2} \left(2 a _ {3} + a _ {5} + a _ {6}\right) \right] \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z} \\ & {\quad (\text {利用轮换对称性}} \\ & {= \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {6} a _ {i} \iiint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2} \leq 1} \left(x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} + z ^ {2}\right) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \mathrm{d} z = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {6} a _ {i} \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} \mathrm{d} \theta \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} \mathrm{d} \varphi \int_ {0} ^ {1} \rho^ {2} \cdot \rho^ {2} \sin \varphi \mathrm{d} \rho} \\ & {= \frac {4 \pi}{5} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {6} a _ {i}.} \end{array}
$$
