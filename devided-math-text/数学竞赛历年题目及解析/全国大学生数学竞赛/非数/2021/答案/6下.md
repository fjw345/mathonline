$$
a _ {1} a _ {2} \dots a _ {n} = \left(1 + \frac {2}{b _ {2}}\right) \left(1 + \frac {2}{b _ {3}}\right) \dots \left(1 + \frac {2}{b _ {n}}\right) b _ {n},
$$

---- 4 分由于 $\{b_{n}\}$ 有界，故存在M>0，使得当 $n\geq1$ 时，恒有 $0<b_{n}\leq M$ 。因此

$$
0 < \frac {b _ {n}}{a _ {1} a _ {2} \cdots a _ {n}} = \left(1 + \frac {2}{b _ {2}}\right) ^ {- 1} \left(1 + \frac {2}{b _ {3}}\right) ^ {- 1} \dots \left(1 + \frac {2}{b _ {n}}\right) ^ {- 1} \leq \left(1 + \frac {2}{M}\right) ^ {- n + 1} \rightarrow 0, \quad n \rightarrow \infty
$$

根据夹逼准则， $\lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{b_n}{a_1a_2\cdots a_n} = 0.$

---- 4 分考虑级数 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n}$ 的部分和 $S_n$，当 $n \geq 2$ 时，有

$$
\begin{array}{r l} S _ {n} & = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \frac {1}{a _ {1} a _ {2} \cdots a _ {k}} = \frac {1}{a _ {1}} + \sum_ {k = 2} ^ {n} \frac {1}{a _ {1} a _ {2} \cdots a _ {k}} \cdot \frac {a _ {k} b _ {k - 1} - b _ {k}}{2} \\ & = 1 + \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {k = 2} ^ {n} \left(\frac {b _ {k - 1}}{a _ {1} a _ {2} \cdots a _ {k - 1}} - \frac {b _ {k}}{a _ {1} a _ {2} \cdots a _ {k}}\right) \\ & = \frac {3}{2} - \frac {b _ {n}}{2 a _ {1} a _ {2} \cdots a _ {n}}, \end{array}
$$

所以 $\lim_{n\to \infty}S_n = \frac{3}{2}$，这就证明了级数 $\sum_{n = 1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{a_1a_2\cdots a_n}$ 收敛，且其和为 $\frac{3}{2}$.
