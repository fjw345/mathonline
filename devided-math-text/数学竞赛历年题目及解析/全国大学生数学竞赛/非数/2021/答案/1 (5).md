【解】根据重积分的对称性，得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {原式} & = \iint_ {D} \sin x ^ {2} \cos y ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \iint_ {D} \sin y ^ {2} \cos x ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \iint_ {D} (\sin x ^ {2} \cos y ^ {2} + \sin y ^ {2} \cos x ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \iint_ {D} \sin (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {\sqrt {\pi}} r \sin r ^ {2} \mathrm{d} r \\ & = \frac {\pi}{2} (- \cos r ^ {2}) \Bigg | _ {0} ^ {\sqrt {\pi}} = \pi . \end{array}
$$
