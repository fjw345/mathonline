【解】利用不等式:当 $x \in (0,1]$ 时，$x - \frac{x^2}{2} \leq \ln(1 + x) \leq x$，$\sin x \leq x$，可得

$f(x) = \int_0^x \frac{\ln(1 + t)}{1 + e^{-t} \sin^3 t} \mathrm{d}t \geq \frac{1}{1 + x} \int_0^x \left(t - \frac{t^2}{2}\right) \mathrm{d}t = \frac{1}{1 + x} \left(\frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{x^3}{6}\right) > \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{x^2}{1 + x},$

且 $f(x) = \int_0^x \frac{\ln(1 + t)}{1 + e^{-t} \sin^3 t} \mathrm{d}t \leq \int_0^x t \mathrm{d}t = \frac{1}{2} x^2,$

所以

$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) > \frac{1}{3} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\frac{1}{n^2}}{1 + \frac{1}{n}} = \frac{1}{3} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{1}{3} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}\right) = \frac{1}{3}.$

$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) \leq \frac{1}{2} \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi^2}{6} < \frac{5}{6}.$

综合上述，级数 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)$ 收敛，且 $\frac{1}{3} < \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f\left(\frac{1}{n}\right) < \frac{5}{6}.$
