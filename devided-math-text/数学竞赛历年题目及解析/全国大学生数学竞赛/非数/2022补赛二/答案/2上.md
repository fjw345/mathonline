【解】令 $F(x) = \int_{0}^{x} \frac{t^{2023}}{1 + t^2} \, \mathrm{d}t$，则 $F'(x) = \frac{x^{2023}}{1 + x^2}$，$F''(x) = \frac{2023x^{2022}(1 + x^2) - 2x^{2024}}{(1 + x^2)^2}$，以 $F(0) = F'(0) = F''(0) = 0$. …… 5 分对 $f(x) = \mathrm{e}^{-x}F(x)$ 利用Leibniz公式，再代入 $x = 0$ 得

$$
f ^ {(n)} (0) = \mathrm{e} ^ {- x} \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {n - k} C _ {n} ^ {k} F ^ {(k)} (x) \Bigg | _ {x = 0} = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {n - k} C _ {n} ^ {k} F ^ {(k)} (0)
$$
