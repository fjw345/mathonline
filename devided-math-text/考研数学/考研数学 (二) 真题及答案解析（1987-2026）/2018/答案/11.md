(11) $\int_{5}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{x^2 - 4x + 3} dx =$
【答案】 $\frac{1}{2}\ln 2$
【解析】 $\int_{5}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{x^2 - 4x + 3} dx = \int_{5}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{(x - 1)(x - 3)} dx = (-\frac{1}{2})\int_{5}^{+\infty}(\frac{1}{x - 1} -\frac{1}{x - 3})dx$ $= (-\frac{1}{2})\cdot [\ln (x - 1) - \ln (x - 3)]\Big|_{5}^{+\infty} = -( \frac{1}{2})(\ln \frac{x - 1}{x - 3})\Big|_{5}^{+\infty}$ $= (-\frac{1}{2})(0 - \ln \frac{4}{2}) = \frac{1}{2}\ln 2$
