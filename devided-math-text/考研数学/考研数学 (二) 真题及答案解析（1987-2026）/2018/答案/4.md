【答案】(D)

【解析】 $f(x) = f(\frac{1}{2}) + f'(\frac{1}{2})(x - \frac{1}{2}) + \frac{f''(\xi)}{2!} (x - \frac{1}{2})^2, \xi$ 介于 $\frac{1}{2}$，$x$ 之间，故 $0 = \int_{0}^{1} f(x) dx = f(\frac{1}{2}) + \int_{0}^{1} f'(\frac{1}{2})(x - \frac{1}{2}) dx + \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f''(\xi)}{2!}(x - \frac{1}{2})^2 dx = f(\frac{1}{2}) + \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f''(\xi)}{2!}(x - \frac{1}{2})^2 dx$ 由于 $f''(x) > 0 \Rightarrow \int_{0}^{1} \frac{f''(\xi)}{2!}(x - \frac{1}{2})^2 dx > 0,$ 所以，$f(\frac{1}{2}) < 0.$ 应选 $D$.
