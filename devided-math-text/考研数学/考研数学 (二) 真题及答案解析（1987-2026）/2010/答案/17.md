(17)【解析】根据题意得

$$
\frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {\frac {d y}{d t}}{\frac {d x}{d t}} = \frac {\psi^ {\prime} (t)}{2 t + 2}, \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} = \frac {\frac {d \left(\frac {\psi^ {\prime} (t)}{2 t + 2}\right)}{d t}}{\frac {d x}{d t}} = \frac {\frac {\psi^ {\prime \prime} (t) (2 t + 2) - 2 \psi^ {\prime} (t)}{(2 t + 2) ^ {2}}}{2 t + 2} = \frac {3}{4 (1 + t)}
$$

即 $\psi''(t)(2t + 2) - 2\psi'(t) = 6(t + 1)^2$ ，整理有 $\psi''(t)(t + 1) - \psi'(t) = 3(t + 1)^2$ ，解

$\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\psi^{\prime \prime}(t) - \frac{\psi^{\prime}(t)}{t + 1} = 3(t + 1)\\ ,\text{令} y = \psi^{\prime}(t),\text{即} y^{\prime} - \frac{1}{1 + t} y = 3(1 + t).\\ \psi (1) = \frac{5}{2},\psi^{\prime}(1) = 6 \end{array} \right.$

所以 $y = e^{\int \frac{1}{1 + t} dt}\left(\int 3(1 + t)e^{-\int \frac{1}{1 + t} dt}dt + C\right) = (1 + t)(3t + C), t > -1$. 因为 $y(1) = \psi'(1) = 6$, 所以 $C = 0$, 故 $y = 3t(t + 1)$, 即 $\psi'(t) = 3t(t + 1)$,

故 $\psi (t) = \int 3t(t + 1)dt = \frac{3}{2} t^2 +t^3 +C_1.$

又由 $\psi (1) = \frac{5}{2}$, 所以 $C_1 = 0$, 故 $\psi (t) = \frac{3}{2} t^2 + t^3, (t > -1)$.
