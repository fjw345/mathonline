(9)【答案】 $y = C_{1}e^{2x} + C_{2}\cos x + C_{3}\sin x$.

【解析】该常系数线性齐次微分方程的特征方程为 $\lambda^3 - 2\lambda^2 + \lambda - 2 = 0$，因式分解得

$$
\lambda^ {2} (\lambda - 2) + (\lambda - 2) = (\lambda - 2) (\lambda^ {2} + 1) = 0,
$$

解得特征根为 $\lambda = 2, \lambda = \pm i$，所以通解为 $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 \cos x + C_3 \sin x$.
