令 $y = b\sin t, y = -b$ 时 $t = -\frac{\pi}{2}; y = \frac{b}{2}$ 时 $t = \frac{\pi}{6}$.
$S = 2ab \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{6}} \cos^2 t dt = 2ab \int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{6}} (\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \cos 2t) dt = (\frac{2}{3} \pi + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}) ab$
所以油的质量 $m = (\frac{2}{3} \pi + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}) abl \rho$.
