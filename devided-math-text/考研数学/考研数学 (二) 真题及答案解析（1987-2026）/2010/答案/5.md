(5) 【答案】 (B).

【解析】 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=-\frac{F_{x}^{\prime}}{F_{z}^{\prime}}=-\frac{F_{1}^{\prime}\left(-\frac{y}{x^{2}}\right)+F_{2}^{\prime}\left(-\frac{z}{x^{2}}\right)}{F_{2}^{\prime}\cdot\frac{1}{x}}=\frac{F_{1}^{\prime}\cdot\frac{y}{x}+F_{2}^{\prime}\cdot\frac{z}{x}}{F_{2}^{\prime}}=\frac{yF_{1}^{\prime}+zF_{2}^{\prime}}{xF_{2}^{\prime}},$

$\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=-\frac{F_{y}^{\prime}}{F_{z}^{\prime}}=-\frac{F_{1}^{\prime}\cdot\frac{1}{x}}{F_{2}^{\prime}\cdot\frac{1}{x}}=-\frac{F_{1}^{\prime}}{F_{2}^{\prime}},$

$x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=\frac{yF_{1}^{\prime}+zF_{2}^{\prime}}{F_{2}^{\prime}}-\frac{yF_{1}^{\prime}}{F_{2}^{\prime}}=\frac{F_{2}^{\prime}\cdot z}{F_{2}^{\prime}}=z.$
