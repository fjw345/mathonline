(7) 【答案】 (A).

【解析】由于向量组I能由向量组II线性表示, 所以 $r(\mathrm{I}) \leq r(\mathrm{II})$, 即

$r(\alpha_{1}, \cdots, \alpha_{r}) \leq r(\beta_{1}, \cdots, \beta_{s}) \leq s$

若向量组I线性无关, 则 $r(\alpha_{1}, \cdots, \alpha_{r}) = r$, 所以 $r = r(\alpha_{1}, \cdots, \alpha_{r}) \leq r(\beta_{1}, \cdots, \beta_{s}) \leq s$, 即 $r \leq s$, 选(A).
