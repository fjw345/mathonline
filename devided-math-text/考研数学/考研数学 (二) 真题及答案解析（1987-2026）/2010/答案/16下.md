则 $\int_0^1 |\ln t|\left[\ln (1 + t)\right]^n dt < \int_0^1 |\ln t|t^n dt (n = 1,2,\dots).$

(II) $\int_0^1 |\ln t|t^n dt = -\int_0^1 \ln t \cdot t^n dt = -\frac{1}{n+1} \int_0^1 \ln td(t^{n+1}) = \frac{1}{(n+1)^2}$, 故由

$0 < u_n < \int_0^1 |\ln t|t^n dt = \frac{1}{(n+1)^2},$

根据夹逼定理得 $0 \leq \lim_{n \to \infty} u_n \leq \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{(n+1)^2} = 0$, 所以 $\lim_{n \to \infty} u_n = 0$.
