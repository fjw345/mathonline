又 $f(x)=f(x_{0})+f'(x_{0})(x-x_{0})+\frac{f''(\gamma)}{2!}(x-x_{0})^{2}=f(x_{0})+\frac{f''(\gamma)}{2!}(x-x_{0})^{2}$ ， $\gamma$ 介于

0与x之间，则 $f(-a)=f(x_{0})+\frac{f''(\gamma_{1})}{2!}(-a-x_{0})^{2}$ ， $-a<\gamma_{1}<0$

 $f(a)=f(x_{0})+\frac{f''(\gamma_{2})}{2!}(a-x_{0})^{2}$ ， $0<\gamma_{2}<a$

从而 $\left|f(a)-f(-a)\right|=\left|\frac{1}{2}(a-x_{0})^{2}f''(\gamma_{2})-\frac{1}{2}(a+x_{0})^{2}f''(\gamma_{1})\right|$

 $\leq\frac{1}{2}\left|(a-x_{0})^{2}f''(\gamma_{2})\right|+\frac{1}{2}\left|(a+x_{0})^{2}f''(\gamma_{1})\right|$

又 $\left|f''(x)\right|$ 连续，设 $M=\max\left\{\left|f''(\gamma_{1})\right|,\left|f''(\gamma_{2})\right|\right\}$ ，则

 $\left|f(a)-f(-a)\right|\leq\frac{1}{2}M(a+x_{0})^{2}+\frac{1}{2}M(a-x_{0})^{2}\leq M(a^{2}+x_{0}^{2})$

又 $x_{0}\in(-a,a)$ ，则 $\left|f(a)-f(-a)\right|\leq M(a^{2}+x_{0}^{2})\leq2Ma^{2}$ ，则 $M\geq\frac{1}{2a^{2}}\left|f(a)-f(-a)\right|$ ，

即存在 $\eta=\gamma_{1}$ 或 $\eta=\gamma_{2}\in(-a,a)$ ，有

 $\left|f''(\eta)\right|\geq\frac{1}{2a^{2}}\left|f(a)-f(-a)\right|.$
