【解析】由题意可知:设存在常数 $k_{1}, k_{2}, t_{1}, t_{2}$ 使得:

$$
\gamma = k _ {1} \alpha_ {1} + k _ {2} \alpha_ {2}; \gamma = t _ {1} \beta_ {1} + t _ {2} \beta_ {2}
$$

即 $k_{1}\alpha_{1}+k_{2}\alpha_{2}=t_{1}\beta_{1}+t_{2}\beta_{2}$ ，可得: $\left\{\begin{aligned}k_{1}+2k_{2}&=2t_{1}+t_{2};\\ 2k_{1}+k_{2}&=5t_{1}+0\cdot t_{2};\\ 3k_{1}+k_{2}&=9t_{1}+t_{2};\end{aligned}\right.$

解得: $k_{1} = -3k_{2}$

即 $\gamma = k_{1}\alpha_{1} + k_{2}\alpha_{2} = -3k_{2}\alpha_{1} + k_{2}\alpha_{2} = k_{2}\begin{bmatrix}-1\\-5\\-8\end{bmatrix} = k\begin{bmatrix}1\\5\\8\end{bmatrix}, k \in R,$ 选（D）.
