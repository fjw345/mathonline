 $\int_{1}^{3}f(x)dx=\int_{1}^{2}f(x)dx+\int_{2}^{3}f(x)dx=\int_{1}^{2}f(x)dx+\int_{0}^{1}f(x+2)dx$
 $=\int_{1}^{2}f(x)dx+\int_{0}^{1}[f(x)+x]dx=\int_{1}^{2}f(x)dx+\int_{0}^{1}f(x)dx+\int_{0}^{1}xdx$
 $=\int_{0}^{2}f(x)dx+\int_{0}^{1}xdx=0+\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{2}.$
