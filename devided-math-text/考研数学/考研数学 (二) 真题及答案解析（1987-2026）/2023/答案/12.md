【答案】 $\frac{4\pi}{3} +\sqrt{3}$

【解析】 $y^{\prime} = \sqrt{3 - x^{2}}, -\sqrt{3}\leq x\leq \sqrt{3}$

$$
S = \int_ {- \sqrt {3}} ^ {\sqrt {3}} \sqrt {1 - (y ^ {\prime}) ^ {2}} d x = \int_ {- \sqrt {3}} ^ {\sqrt {3}} \sqrt {1 + 3 - x ^ {2}} d x = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {\sqrt {3}} \sqrt {4 - x ^ {2}} d x
$$

令 $x = 2\sin u$

$$
S = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{3}} 2 \cos u \cdot d 2 \sin u = 8 \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{3}} \cos^ {2} u d u
$$

$$
= 8 \cdot \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{3}} \frac {1 + \cos 2 u}{2} d u = 8 \left(\frac {1}{2} u + \frac {1}{4} \sin 2 u\right) \bigg | _ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{3}} = 8 \left(\frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {\pi}{3} + \frac {1}{4} \cdot \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}\right) = \frac {4 \pi}{3} + \sqrt {3}.
$$
