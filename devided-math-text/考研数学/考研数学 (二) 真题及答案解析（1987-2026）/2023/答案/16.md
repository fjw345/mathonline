【解析】

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a x _ {1} + x _ {3} = 1, \\ x _ {1} + a x _ {2} + x _ {3} = 0, \\ x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2} + a x _ {3} = 0, \\ a x _ {1} + b x _ {2} = 2 \end{array} \right.
$$

方程组有解

$r (A) = r (A, b) \leq 3 < 4, | A, b | = 0$

$$
\mid A, b \mid = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} a & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & a & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & a & 0 \\ a & b & 0 & 2 \end{array} \right| = 1 \cdot (- 1) ^ {1 + 4} \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & a & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & a \\ a & b & 0 \end{array} \right| + 2 \cdot (- 1) ^ {4 + 4} \left| \begin{array}{c c c} a & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & a & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & a \end{array} \right| = - \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & a & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & a \\ a & b & 0 \end{array} \right| + 2 \times 4 = 0,
$$

$$
\left| \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & a & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & a \\ a & b & 0 \end{array} \right| = 8.
$$
