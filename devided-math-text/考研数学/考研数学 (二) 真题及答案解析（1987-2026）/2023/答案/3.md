【答案】B.

【解析】

在 $\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 中, $\frac{2}{\pi}x<\sin x$

故 $x_{n + 1} = \sin x_n > \frac{2}{\pi} x_n$

$$
y _ {n + 1} = y _ {n} ^ {2} < \frac {1}{2} y _ {n} \Rightarrow \frac {y _ {n + 1}}{x _ {n + 1}} < \frac {\frac {1}{2}}{\underline {{2}} \pi} \cdot \frac {y _ {n}}{x _ {n}} = \frac {\pi}{4} \cdot \frac {y _ {n}}{x _ {n}} < \dots < \left(\frac {\pi}{4}\right) ^ {n} \frac {y _ {1}}{x _ {1}} = \left(\frac {\pi}{4}\right) ^ {n}
$$

所以 $\lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{y_n}{x_n} = 0$ 故 $y_{n}$ 是 $x_{n}$ 的高阶无穷小.选B.
