【解析】

（I）因为 $A\left( \begin{array}{c}x_{1}\\ x_{2}\\ x_{3} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c}x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3}\\ 2x_{1} - x_{2} + x_{3}\\ x_{2} - x_{3} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 1 & 1\\ 2 & -1 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{array} \right)\left( \begin{array}{c}x_{1}\\ x_{2}\\ x_{3} \end{array} \right)$ 对任意的 $x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}$ 均成立，

所以 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$
