【答案】 $A$

【解析】

要使得 $f(\alpha) = \int_{2}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{x(\ln x)^{\alpha + 1}}\mathrm{d}x$ 收敛， $\alpha >0$

$$
f (\alpha) = \int_ {2} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {1}{x (\ln x) ^ {\alpha + 1}} \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {2} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {1}{(\ln x) ^ {\alpha + 1}} \mathrm{d} \ln x = (\ln x) ^ {- \alpha} \Bigg | _ {2} ^ {+ \infty} = 0 - \frac {1}{\alpha} (\ln 2) ^ {- \alpha} = \frac {1}{(\ln 2) ^ {\alpha}} \cdot \frac {1}{\alpha}
$$
