【解析】 $\left\{\begin{aligned}f_{x}^{\prime}&=e^{\cos y}+x=0\\ f_{y}^{\prime}&=xe^{\cos y}(-\sin y)=0\end{aligned}\right.$
得驻点为: $(-e^{-1},(2n-1)\pi),(-e,2n\pi),n=0,1,2,\cdots$
则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}f_{xx}^{\prime\prime}&=1\\ f_{xy}^{\prime\prime}&=e^{\cos y}(-\sin y)\\ f_{yy}^{\prime\prime}&=xe^{\cos y}\sin^{2}y+xe^{\cos y}(-\cos y)\end{aligned}\right.$
