$$
\begin{array}{l} \text {(II)} \left| \lambda E - A \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 1 & - 1 & - 1 \\ - 2 & \lambda + 1 & - 1 \\ 0 & - 1 & \lambda + 1 \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 1 & - 1 & 0 \\ - 2 & \lambda & - 2 - \lambda \\ 0 & - 1 & 2 + \lambda \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 1 & - 1 & 0 \\ - 2 & \lambda & 0 \\ 0 & - 1 & 2 + \lambda \end{array} \right| \\ = (2 + \lambda) (\lambda^ {2} - \lambda - 2) = (\lambda + 2) (\lambda - 2) (\lambda - 1) = 0 \end{array}
$$

所以 $A$ 的特征值为 $\lambda_1 = -2$ ， $\lambda_{2} = 2$ ， $\lambda_3 = -1$ :

$\lambda_{1} = -2$ 时， $\lambda_1E - A = \left( \begin{array}{rrr} - 3 & -1 & -1\\ -2 & -1 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & -1 \end{array} \right)\to \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right),$

得特征向量的基础解系为 $\alpha_{1} = (0, -1, 1)^{T}$;

$\lambda_{2} = 2$ 时， $\lambda_2E - A = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & -1 & -1\\ -2 & 3 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & 3 \end{array} \right)\to \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & -4\\ 0 & 1 & -3\\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right),$

得特征向量的基础解系为 $\alpha_{2} = (4,3,1)^{T}$

$\lambda_{3} = -1$ 时， $\lambda_3E - A = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & -1 & -1\\ -2 & 0 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}\rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}\rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & \frac{1}{2}\\ 0 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix},$

得特征向量的基础解系为 $\alpha_{3}=(1,0,-2)^{T}$ ;

令 $P = (\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}) = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & 4 & 1\\ -1 & 3 & 0\\ 1 & 1 & -2 \end{array} \right)$ ，则 $P^{-1}AP = \left( \begin{array}{ccc} - 2 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 2 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{array} \right)$
