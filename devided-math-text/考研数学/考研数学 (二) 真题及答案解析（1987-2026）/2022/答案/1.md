其中所有的序号是( )
(A) ①②
(B) ①④
(C) ①③④
(D) ②③④

【答案】(C)

【解析】当 $x \to 0$ 时，$\alpha(x) \sim \beta(x)$，则 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x)}{\beta(x)} = 1, \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha^2(x)}{\beta^2(x)} = \lim_{x \to 0} [\frac{\alpha(x)}{\beta(x)}]^2 = 1$ 则 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x) - \beta(x)}{\alpha(x)} = 0$，故 $\alpha(x) - \beta(x) = o(\alpha(x))$，所以，①③正确；

当 $x \to 0$ 时，$\alpha^2(x) \sim \beta^2(x)$，则 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha^2(x)}{\beta^2(x)} = 1$，则 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x)}{\beta(x)} = \pm 1$，

当 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x)}{\beta(x)} = -1$ 时，$\alpha(x)$ 与 $\beta(x)$ 不是等价无穷小，所以②不正确；

当 $\alpha(x) - \beta(x) = o(\alpha(x))$ 时，$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x)}{\beta(x)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x)}{\alpha(x) - o(\alpha(x))} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\alpha(x)}{\alpha(x)} = 1$ 所以④正确。故选(C).
