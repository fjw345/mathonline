【解析】由题意可知 $\lim_{x\to 0}\left[f(e^{x^2}) - 3f(1 + \sin^2 x)\right] = -2f(1) = 0$ ，故 $f(1) = 0$ ，则
$\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{f(e^{x^2}) - 3f(1 + \sin^2x)}{x^2} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{f(e^{x^2}) - f(1) - 3\left[f(1 + \sin^2x) - f(1)\right]}{x^2}\\ = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{f(e^{x^2}) - f(1)}{x^2} -\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{3\left[f(1 + \sin^2x) - f(1)\right]}{x^2}\\ = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{f(e^{x^2}) - f(1)}{e^{x^2} - 1}\cdot \frac{e^{x^2} - 1}{x^2} -\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{3\left[f(1 + \sin^2x) - f(1)\right]}{\sin^2x}\\ = f'(1) - 3f'(1) = 2$ ，故 $f^{\prime}(1) = -1.$
