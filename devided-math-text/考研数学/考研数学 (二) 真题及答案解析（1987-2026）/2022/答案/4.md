【答案】(C)

$$
F (x, y) = \int_ {0} ^ {x - y} (x - y - t) f (t) d t = (x - y) \int_ {0} ^ {x - y} f (t) d t - \int_ {0} ^ {x - y} t f (t) d t
$$

$$
\frac {\partial F}{\partial x} = \int_ {0} ^ {x - y} f (t) d t + (x - y) f (x - y) - (x - y) f (x - y) = \int_ {0} ^ {x - y} f (t) d t,
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial x ^ {2}} = f (x - y)
$$

同理 $\frac{\partial F}{\partial y} = -\int_0^{x - y}f(t)dt - (x - y)f(x - y) + (x - y)f(x - y) = -\int_0^{x - y}f(t)dt,$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial y ^ {2}} = f (x - y)
$$

综上所述， $\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}=-\frac{\partial F}{\partial y},\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial x^{2}}=\frac{\partial^{2}F}{\partial y^{2}}$ ，故选(C).
