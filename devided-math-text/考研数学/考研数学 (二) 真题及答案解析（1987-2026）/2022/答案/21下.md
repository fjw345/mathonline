令 $G(x) = \int_{c}^{x}f(t)dt - (x - c)f\left(\frac{c + x}{2}\right), G(c) = 0,$ $G^{\prime}(x) = f(x) - f(\frac{c + x}{2}) - \frac{1}{2} (x - c)f^{\prime}(\frac{c + x}{2}) = \frac{1}{2} (x - c)\bigg[f^{\prime}(\eta) - f^{\prime}(\frac{c + x}{2})\bigg],$ 其中 $\eta \in (\frac{c + x}{2},x)$ $= \frac{1}{2} (x - c)(\eta -\frac{c + x}{2})f''(\gamma) < 0$ ，其中 $\gamma \in (\frac{c + x}{2},\eta)$ 故 $G(x)$ 单调递减，所以 $G(d) < G(c) = 0\Rightarrow \frac{1}{d - c}\int_{c}^{d}f(t)dt < f(\frac{c + d}{2})$ ，这个与题设条件$f(\frac{a + b}{2})\leq \frac{1}{b - a}\int_{a}^{b}f(x)dx$ 矛盾，故对于 $\forall x$ ，有 $f^{\prime \prime}(x)\geq 0.$ 证毕.
