【解析】: $Q = P\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$，则 $Q^{-1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} P^{-1}$

$$
\text {故} Q ^ {- 1} A Q = \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ - 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) P ^ {- 1} A P \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ - 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 2 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{l l l} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 2 \end{array} \right)
$$

故选（B）
