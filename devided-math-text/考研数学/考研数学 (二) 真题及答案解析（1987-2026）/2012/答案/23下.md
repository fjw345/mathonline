$$
\left| \lambda E - B \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 2 & 0 & - 2 \\ 0 & \lambda - 2 & - 2 \\ - 2 & - 2 & \lambda - 4 \end{array} \right| = \lambda (\lambda - 2) (\lambda - 6) = 0
$$

解得 $B$ 矩阵的特征值为: $\lambda_{1} = 0;\lambda_{2} = 2;\lambda_{3} = 6$

对于 $\lambda_{1} = 0$ ，解 $(\lambda_1E - B)X = 0$ 得对应的特征向量为: $\eta_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$ 对于 $\lambda_{2} = 2$ ，解 $(\lambda_{2}E - B)X = 0$ 得对应的特征向量为: $\eta_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$ 对于 $\lambda_{3} = 6$ ，解 $(\lambda_{3}E - B)X = 0$ 得对应的特征向量为: $\eta_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$ 将 $\eta_{1},\eta_{2},\eta_{3}$ 单位化可得:

$$
\alpha_ {1} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {3}} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 1 \\ - 1 \end{array} \right), \quad \alpha_ {2} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ - 1 \\ 0 \end{array} \right), \quad \alpha_ {3} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {6}} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{array} \right)
$$

$$
Q = \left(\alpha_ {1}, \alpha_ {2}, \alpha_ {3}\right)
