【答案】 $\frac{8}{\pi}$

【解析】由旋转体的体积公式，得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mathrm{V} _ {1} = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \pi f ^ {2} (x) d x = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \pi (A \sin x) ^ {2} d x = \pi A ^ {2} \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \frac {1 - \cos 2 x}{2} d x = \frac {\pi^ {2} A ^ {2}}{4} \\ \mathrm{V} _ {2} = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} 2 \pi x f (x) d x = - 2 \pi A \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} x d \cos x = 2 \pi A \\ \text {由题} \mathrm{V} _ {1} = \mathrm{V} _ {2}, \text {求得} \mathrm{A} = \frac {8}{\pi}. \end{array}
$$
