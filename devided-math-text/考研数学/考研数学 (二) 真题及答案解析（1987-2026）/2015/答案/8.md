【解析】由 $x = Py$ ，故 $f = x^{T}Ax = y^{T}(P^{T}AP)y = 2y_{1}^{2} + y_{2}^{2} - y_{3}^{2}$ .且

$$
P ^ {T} A P = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & - 1 \end{array} \right)
$$

$$
Q = P \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & - 1 & 0 \end{array} \right) = P C
$$

$$
Q ^ {T} A Q = C ^ {T} (P ^ {T} A P) C = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & - 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right)
$$

所以 $f = x^{T}Ax = y^{T}(Q^{T}AQ)y = 2y_{1}^{2} - y_{2}^{2} + y_{3}^{2}$ 。选（A）
