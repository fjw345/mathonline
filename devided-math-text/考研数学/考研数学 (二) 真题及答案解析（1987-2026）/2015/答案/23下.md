【解析】(I) $A \sim B \Rightarrow tr(A) = tr(B) \Rightarrow 3 + a = 1 + b + 1$

$$
\left| A \right| = \left| B \right| \Rightarrow \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & 2 & - 3 \\ - 1 & 3 & - 3 \\ 1 & - 2 & a \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & - 2 & 0 \\ 0 & b & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 1 \end{array} \right|
$$

$$
\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a - b = - 1 \\ 2 a - b = 3 \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 4 \\ b = 5 \end{array} \right.
$$

(II)

$$
A = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & 2 & - 3 \\ - 1 & 3 & - 3 \\ 1 & - 2 & 3 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}{c c c} - 1 & 2 & - 3 \\ - 1 & 2 & - 3 \\ 1 & - 2 & 3 \end{array} \right) = E + C
$$

$$
C = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} - 1 & 2 & - 3 \\ - 1 & 2 & - 3 \\ 1 & - 2 & 3 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c} - 1 \\ - 1 \\ 1 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & - 2 & 3 \end{array} \right)
$$

$C$ 的特征值 $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = 0, \lambda_3 = 4$

$\lambda = 0$ 时 $(0E - C)x = 0$ 的基础解系为 $\xi_{1} = (2,1,0)^{T};\xi_{2} = (-3,0,1)^{T}$

$\lambda = 5$ 时 $(4E - C)x = 0$ 的基础解系为 $\xi_{3} = (-1, -1, 1)^{T}$

A 的特征值 $\lambda_{A} = 1 + \lambda_{C}:1,1,5$

令 $P = (\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3) = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$,

$$
\therefore P ^ {- 1} A P = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 5 \end{array} \right)
$$
