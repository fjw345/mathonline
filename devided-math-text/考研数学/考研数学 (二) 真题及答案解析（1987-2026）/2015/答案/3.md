【解析】 $x < 0$ 时，$f'(x) = 0 f_{-}'(0) = 0$

$$
f _ {+} ^ {\prime} (0) = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} \frac {x ^ {\alpha} \cos \frac {1}{x ^ {\beta}} - 0}{x} = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} x ^ {\alpha - 1} \cos \frac {1}{x ^ {\beta}}
$$

$x > 0$ 时， $f^{\prime}(x) = \alpha x^{\alpha -1}\cos \frac{1}{x^{\beta}} +(-1)x^{\alpha}\sin \frac{1}{x^{\beta}} (-\beta)\frac{1}{x^{\beta + 1}}$

$$
= \alpha x ^ {\alpha - 1} \cos \frac {1}{x ^ {\beta}} + \beta x ^ {\alpha - \beta - 1} \sin \frac {1}{x ^ {\beta}}
$$

$f^{\prime}(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续则: $f_{-}^{\prime}(0) = f_{+}^{\prime}(0) = \lim_{x\to 0^{+}}x^{\alpha -1}\cos \frac{1}{x^{\beta}} = 0$ 得 $\alpha -1 > 0$

$$
f ^ {\prime} (0) = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} f ^ {\prime} (x) = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} \left(\alpha x ^ {\alpha - 1} \cos \frac {1}{x ^ {\beta}} + \beta x ^ {\alpha - \beta - 1} \sin \frac {1}{x ^ {\beta}}\right) = 0
$$

得: $\alpha -\beta -1 > 0$ ，答案选择A
