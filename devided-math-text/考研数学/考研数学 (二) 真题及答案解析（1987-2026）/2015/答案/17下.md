求得 $\varphi (x) = e^{x}(x + 1)$

故 $f_{x}^{\prime}(x,y) = (y^{2} + 2y)e^{x} + e^{x}(1 + x)$，两边关于 $\mathbf{x}$ 积分，得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} f (x, y) & = (y ^ {2} + 2 y) e ^ {x} + \int e ^ {x} (1 + x) d x \\ & = (y ^ {2} + 2 y) e ^ {x} + \int (1 + x) d e ^ {x} \\ & = (y ^ {2} + 2 y) e ^ {x} + (1 + x) e ^ {x} - \int e ^ {x} d x \\ & = (y ^ {2} + 2 y) e ^ {x} + (1 + x) e ^ {x} - e ^ {x} + C \\ & = (y ^ {2} + 2 y) e ^ {x} + x e ^ {x} + C \end{array}
$$

由 $f(0,y) = y^{2} + 2y + C = y^{2} + 2y$ ，求得 $C = 0$

所以 $f(x,y) = (y^{2} + 2y)e^{x} + xe^{x}$

令 $\left\{\begin{aligned}f_{x}^{\prime}&=(y^{2}+2y)e^{x}+e^{x}+xe^{x}=0\\ f_{y}^{\prime}&=(2y+2)e^{x}=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，求得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=0\\ y&=-1\end{aligned}\right.$ .

又 $f_{xx}^{\prime \prime} = (y^{2} + 2y)e^{x} + 2e^{x} + xe^{x}$

$$
f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} = 2 (y + 1) e ^ {x}, \quad f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} = 2 e ^ {x},
$$

当 $x = 0, y = -1$ 时，$A = f_{xx}''(0, -1) = 1, B = f_{xy}''(0, -1) = 0, C = f_{yy}''(0, -1) = 2,$ $AC - B^2 > 0, f(0, -1) = -1$ 为极小值.
