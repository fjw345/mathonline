【答案】 $a = -1, k = -\frac{1}{3}, b = -\frac{1}{2}$

【解析】

方法一:

因为 $\ln (1 + x) = x - \frac{x^2}{2} +\frac{x^3}{3} +o(x^3),$ ， $\sin x = x - \frac{x^3}{3!} +o(x^3)$ 那么，

$$
1 = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f (x)}{g (x)} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {x + a \ln (1 + x) + b x \sin x}{k x ^ {3}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {(1 + a) x + (b - \frac {a}{2}) x ^ {2} + \frac {a}{3} x ^ {3} + o (x ^ {3})}{k x ^ {3}},
$$
