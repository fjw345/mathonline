又因为:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {1 - \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x \cdot \cos 3 x} \\ & {= 1 - \cos x + \cos x - \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x + \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x - \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x \cdot \cos 3 x} \\ & {= 1 - \cos x + \cos x (1 - \cos 2 x) + \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x (1 - \cos 3 x)} \\ & {\text {即} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {1 - \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x \cdot \cos 3 x}{a x ^ {n}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {1 - \cos x + \cos x (1 - \cos 2 x) + \cos x \cdot \cos 2 x (1 - \cos 3 x)}{a x ^ {n}}} \\ & {= \lim _ {x \to 0} (\frac {1 - \cos x}{a x ^ {n}} + \frac {\cos x (1 - \cos 2 x)}{a x ^ {n}} + \frac {\cos x \cdot \cos 2 x (1 - \cos 3 x)}{a x ^ {n}})} \\ & {= \lim _ {x \to 0} (\frac {\frac {1}{2} x ^ {2} + o (x ^ {2})}{a x ^ {n}} + \frac {\frac {1}{2} (2 x) ^ {2} + o (x ^ {2})}{a x ^ {n}} + \frac {\frac {1}{2} (3 x) ^ {2} + o (x ^ {2})}{a x ^ {n}})} \end{array}
$$

所以 $n = 2$ 且 $\frac{1}{2a} +\frac{4}{2a} +\frac{9}{2a} = 1\rightarrow a = 7$
