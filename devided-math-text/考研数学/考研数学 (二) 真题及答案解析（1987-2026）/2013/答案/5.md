(5) 设 $z = \frac{y}{x} f(xy)$，其中函数 $f$ 可微，则 $\frac{x}{y}\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = (\quad)$

(A) $2yf'(xy)$
(B) $-2yf'(xy)$
(C) $\frac{2}{x}f(xy)$
(D) $-\frac{2}{x}f(xy)$

【答案】(A)

【解析】已知 $z = \frac{y}{x}f(xy)$，所以 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{y}{x^2}f(xy) + \frac{y^2}{x}f'(xy)$,

所以 $\frac{x}{y}\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = [-\frac{1}{x}f(xy) + yf'(xy)] + (\frac{1}{x}f(xy) + yf'(xy)) = 2yf'(xy)$。
