【解析】（I）设 $f(x) = \ln (1 + x), x \in \left[0, \frac{1}{n}\right]$

显然 $f(x)$ 在 $\left[0,\frac{1}{n}\right]$ 上满足拉格朗日的条件，

$$
f \left(\frac {1}{n}\right) - f (0) = \ln \left(1 + \frac {1}{n}\right) - \ln 1 = \ln \left(1 + \frac {1}{n}\right) = \frac {1}{1 + \xi} \cdot \frac {1}{n}, \xi \in \left(0, \frac {1}{n}\right)
$$

所以 $\xi \in \left(0, \frac{1}{n}\right)$ 时，

$\frac{1}{1 + \frac{1}{n}}\cdot \frac{1}{n} < \frac{1}{1 + \xi}\cdot \frac{1}{n} < \frac{1}{1 + 0}\cdot \frac{1}{n},$ 即: $\frac{1}{n + 1} < \frac{1}{1 + \xi}\cdot \frac{1}{n} < \frac{1}{n},$

亦即: $\frac{1}{n + 1} < \ln \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right) < \frac{1}{n}$

结论得证.
