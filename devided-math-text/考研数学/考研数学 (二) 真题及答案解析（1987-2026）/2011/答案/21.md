【解析】因为 $f(x,1) = 0$ ， $f(1,y) = 0$ ，所以 $f_x'(x,1) = 0$ :

$$
\begin{array}{l} I = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} y f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) d y = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} y d f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) \\ \quad = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \left[ y f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) \big | _ {0} ^ {1} - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d y \right] = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \left(f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, 1) - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d y\right) \\ \quad = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d y = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d y \int_ {0} ^ {1} x f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d x = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d y \left[ x f (x, y) \big | _ {0} ^ {1} - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f (x, y) d x \right] \\ \quad = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d y \left[ f (1, y) - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f (x, y) d x \right] = \iint_ {D} f (x, y) d x d y = a. \end{array}
$$
