【解析】 $f(x) = \ln |x - 1| + \ln |x - 2| + \ln |x - 3|$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} f ^ {\prime} (x) & = \frac {1}{x - 1} + \frac {1}{x - 2} + \frac {1}{x - 3} \\ & = \frac {3 x ^ {2} - 1 2 x + 1 1}{(x - 1) (x - 2) (x - 3)} \end{array}
$$

令 $f'(x) = 0$ ，得 $x_{1,2} = \frac{6\pm\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，故 $f(x)$ 有两个不同的驻点.
