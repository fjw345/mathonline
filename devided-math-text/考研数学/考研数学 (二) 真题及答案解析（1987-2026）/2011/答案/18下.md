令 $y' = p$，$y'' = p'$，则 $\frac{p'}{1 + p^2} = p$，$\int \frac{dp}{p^3 + p} = \int dx$，即

$\int \frac{dp}{p} - \int \frac{p}{p^2 + 1} dp = \int dx, \ln |p| - \frac{1}{2} \ln (p^2 + 1) = x + c_1$，即 $p^2 = \frac{1}{ce^{-2x} - 1}$.

当 $x = 0$，$p = 1$，代入得 $c = 2$，所以 $y' = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2e^{-2x} - 1}}$,

则 $y(x) - y(0) = \int_0^x \frac{dt}{\sqrt{2e^{-2t} - 1}} = \int_0^x \frac{e^t dt}{\sqrt{2 - e^{2t}}}$

$= \int_0^x \frac{d\left(\frac{e^t}{\sqrt{2}}\right)}{\sqrt{1 - (\frac{e^t}{\sqrt{2}})^2}} = \arcsin \frac{e^t}{\sqrt{2}} \bigg|_0^x = \arcsin \frac{e^x}{\sqrt{2}} - \frac{\pi}{4} \, .$

又因为 $y(0) = 0$，所以 $y(x) = \arcsin \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} e^x - \frac{\pi}{4} \, .$
