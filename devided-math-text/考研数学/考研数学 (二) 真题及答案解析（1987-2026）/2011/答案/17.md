【解析】 $z = f\left[xy,yg(x)\right]$

$$
\frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} \big [ x y, y g (x) \big ] \cdot y + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} \big [ x y, y g (x) \big ] \cdot y g ^ {\prime} (x)
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial x \partial y} = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} [ x y, y g (x) ] + y [ f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} (x y, y g (x)) x + f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} (x y, y g (x)) g (x) ]
$$

$$
\left. + g ^ {\prime} (x) \cdot f _ {2} ^ {\prime} [ x y, y g (x) ] + y g ^ {\prime} (x) \left\{f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} [ x y, y g (x) ] \cdot x + f _ {2 2} ^ {\prime \prime} [ x y, y g (x) ] g (x) \right\}. \right.
$$

因为 $g(x)$ 在 $x = 1$ 可导，且为极值,所以 $g'(1) = 0$ ，则

$$
\frac{d^2z}{dxdy}\big|_{\substack{x = 1\\ y = 1}} = f_1'(1,1) + f_{11}''(1,1) + f_{12}''(1,1).
$$
