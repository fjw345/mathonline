(13)【答案】 $\frac{7}{12}$
【解析】原式 $= \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}d\theta \int_0^{2\sin \theta}r\cos \theta \cdot r\sin \theta rdr = \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}r\cos \theta \cdot \sin \theta d\theta \int_0^{2\sin \theta}r^3 dr$
$= \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin \theta \cdot \cos \theta \cdot \frac{1}{4}\cdot 16\sin^4\theta d\theta = \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}4\cos \theta \cdot \sin^5\theta d\theta = 4\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^5\theta d\sin \theta$
$= \frac{4}{6}\sin^6\theta \bigg|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{6}} = \frac{7}{12}.$
