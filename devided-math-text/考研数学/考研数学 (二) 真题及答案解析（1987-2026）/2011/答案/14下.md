【解析】方法1: $f$ 的正惯性指数为所对应矩阵的特征值中正的个数.

二次型 $f$ 对应矩阵为 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$.

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \left| \lambda E - A \right| & = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 1 & - 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & \lambda - 3 & - 1 \\ - 1 & - 1 & \lambda - 1 \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda & 0 & - \lambda \\ - 1 & \lambda - 3 & - 1 \\ - 1 & - 1 & \lambda - 1 \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda & 0 & 0 \\ - 1 & \lambda - 3 & - 2 \\ - 1 & - 1 & \lambda - 2 \end{array} \right| \\ & = \lambda \left| \begin{array}{c c} \lambda - 3 & - 2 \\ - 1 & \lambda - 2 \end{array} \right| = \lambda (\lambda - 1) (\lambda - 4), \end{array}
$$

故 $\lambda_1 = 0, \lambda_2 = 1, \lambda_3 = 4$。因此 $f$ 的正惯性指数为 2。

方法 2: f 的正惯性指数为标准形中正的平方项个数.

$$
\begin{array}{r l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) & = x _ {1} ^ {2} + 3 x _ {2} ^ {2} + x _ {3} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {1} x _ {2} + 2 x _ {1} x _ {3} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} \\ & = \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2} + x _ {3}\right) ^ {2} - x _ {2} ^ {2} - 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} - x _ {3} ^ {2} + 3 x _ {2} ^ {2} + x _ {3} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} \\ & = \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2} + x _ {3}\right) ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} ^ {2}, \end{array}
$$

令 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y_{1}&=x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3},\\ y_{2}&=x_{2},\\ y_{3}&=x_{3},\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 $f=y_{1}^{2}+2y_{2}^{2}$ ，故f的正惯性指数为2.
