(1)【答案】(C).

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\text {【解析】因为} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {3 \sin x - \sin 3 x}{c x ^ {k}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {3 \sin x - \sin x \cos 2 x - \cos x \sin 2 x}{c x ^ {k}}} \\ & {\quad = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\sin x (3 - \cos 2 x - 2 \cos^ {2} x)}{c x ^ {k}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {3 - \cos 2 x - 2 \cos^ {2} x}{c x ^ {k - 1}}} \\ & {\quad = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {3 - (2 \cos^ {2} x - 1) - 2 \cos^ {2} x}{c x ^ {k - 1}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {4 - 4 \cos^ {2} x}{c x ^ {k - 1}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {4 \sin^ {2} x}{c x ^ {k - 1}}} \\ & {\quad = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {4}{c x ^ {k - 3}} = 1.} \end{array}
$$

所以 $c = 4, k = 3$ ，故答案选(C).
