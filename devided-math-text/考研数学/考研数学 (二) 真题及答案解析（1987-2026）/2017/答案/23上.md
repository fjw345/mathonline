【答案】 $a = 2; Q = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\\ -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & 0 & \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}}\\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \end{array} \right), f\underline{x=Qy}-3y_{1}^{2}+6y_{2}^{2}$

【解析】

$f(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}) = X^{T}AX$ ，其中 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -4\\ 1 & -1 & 1\\ -4 & 1 & a \end{pmatrix}$

由于 $f(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}) = X^{T}AX$ 经正交变换后，得到的标准形为 $\lambda_1y_1^2 +\lambda_2y_2^2$

故 $r(A)=2\Rightarrow|A|=0\Rightarrow\begin{vmatrix}2&1&-4 1&-1&1 -4&1&a\end{vmatrix}=0\Rightarrow a=2$ ,

将 $a = 2$ 代入，满足 $r(A) = 2$ ，因此 $a = 2$ 符合题意，此时 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 & -4 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \\ -4 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$，则

$$
| \lambda E - A | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 2 & - 1 & 4 \\ - 1 & \lambda + 1 & - 1 \\ 4 & - 1 & \lambda - 2 \end{array} \right| = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda_ {1} = - 3, \lambda_ {2} = 0, \lambda_ {3} = 6
$$

由 $(-3E-A)x=0$ ，可得A的属于特征值-3的特征向量为 $\alpha_{1}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix}$ ;

由 $(6E - A)x = 0$ ，可得A的属于特征值6的特征向量为 $\alpha_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} -1\\ 0\\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$

由 $(0E - A)x = 0$ ，可得A的属于特征值0的特征向量为 $\alpha_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$

令 $P = (\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3})$ ，则 $P^{-1}AP = \begin{pmatrix} -3 & & \\ & 6 & \\ & & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ ，由于 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 彼此正交，故只需单位化即可:$\beta_{1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} (1, - 1,1)^{T},\beta_{2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (-1,0,1)^{T},\beta_{3} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} (1,2,1)^{T},,$
