【答案】 $-\frac{1}{8}$

【解析】

$$
\begin{array}{l} \frac {d y}{d t} = \cos t, \frac {d x}{d t} = 1 + e ^ {t} \Rightarrow \frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {\cos t}{1 + e ^ {t}} \\ \Rightarrow \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} = \frac {\left(\frac {\cos t}{1 + e ^ {t}}\right) ^ {\prime}}{\frac {d x}{d t}} = \frac {- \sin t (1 + e ^ {t}) - \cos t e ^ {t}}{\left(1 + e ^ {t}\right) ^ {2}} \Rightarrow \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} \big | _ {t = 0} = - \frac {1}{8} \end{array}
$$
