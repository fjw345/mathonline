19. 解:

$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = -2xe^{-y}\Rightarrow f(x,y) = -x^{2}e^{-y} + \varphi (y)$ .则 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = x^2 e^{-y} + \varphi '(y) = e^{-y}x^2 -(y + 1)e^{-y}$

则 $\varphi^{\prime}(y) = -(y + 1)e^{-y}$

$$
\Rightarrow \varphi (y) = (y + 2) e ^ {- y} + C
$$

则

$$
f (x, y) = - x ^ {2} e ^ {- y} + (y + 2) e ^ {- y} + C
$$

又$f (0, 0) = 2, \Rightarrow \mathrm{C} = 0$

则 $f(x,y) = -x^{2}e^{-y} + (y + 2)e^{-y}$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \frac {\partial f}{\partial x} = - 2 x e ^ {- y} = 0 \\ \frac {\partial f}{\partial y} = e ^ {- y} (x ^ {2} - y - 1) = 0 \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 0 \\ y = - 1 \end{array} \right. \end{array}
$$

则驻点（0，-1）

$$
f _ {x x} = - 2 e ^ {- y}, f _ {x y} = 2 x e ^ {- y}, f _ {y y} = e ^ {- y} (x ^ {2} - y - 1) - e ^ {- y} = e ^ {- y} (x ^ {2} - y)
$$

在点（0，-1）处 $A = -2e, B = 0, C = -e$

则 $AC - B^{2} > 0, A < 0$, 从而 $f(x, y)$ 在 $(0, -1)$ 处有极大值，且极大值为 $f(0, -1) = e$
