$D=\left\{(x,y)\mid4-x^{2}\leq y\leq4,-2\leq x\leq2\right\}$ ，对应图像为上图所示。

记 $\mathrm{D}_1 = \left\{(x,y)\mid 4 - x^2\leq y\leq 4, - 2\leq x\leq 0\right\}$ ， $\mathrm{D}_2 = \left\{(x,y)\mid 4 - x^2\leq y\leq 4,0\leq x\leq 2\right\}$ ，且

$\mathrm{I} = \int_{-2}^{2}\mathrm{d}x\int_{4 - x^2}^4 f(x,y)\mathrm{d}y$ ，则 $\mathrm{I} = \iint_{D_1}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}\sigma +\iint_{D_2}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}\sigma$ ，交换积分次序得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mathrm{I} = \int_ {0} ^ {4} \mathrm{d} y \int_ {- 2} ^ {- \sqrt {4 - y}} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {4} \mathrm{d} y \int_ {\sqrt {4 - y}} ^ {2} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \\ = \int_ {0} ^ {4} \left[ \int_ {- 2} ^ {- \sqrt {4 - y}} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {\sqrt {4 - y}} ^ {2} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \right] \mathrm{d} y \\ \end{array}
$$

故A正确。
