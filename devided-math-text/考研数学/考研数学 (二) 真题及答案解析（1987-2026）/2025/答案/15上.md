【答案】 $3x^{2} - 4xy + 5y^{2} = 4$

【解析】

解:

即

$$
\begin{array}{l} (2 y - 3 x) \mathrm{d} x + (2 x - 5 y) \mathrm{d} y = 0 \\ \Rightarrow 2 y \mathrm{d} x + 2 x \mathrm{d} y - 3 x \mathrm{d} x - 5 y \mathrm{d} y = 0 \\ \Rightarrow \mathrm{d} (2 x y) - \mathrm{d} (\frac {3}{2} x ^ {2}) - \mathrm{d} (\frac {5}{2} y ^ {2}) = 0 \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mathrm{d} (2 x y - \frac {3}{2} x ^ {2} - \frac {5}{2} y ^ {2}) = 0 \\ \Rightarrow 2 x y - \frac {3}{2} x ^ {2} - \frac {5}{2} y ^ {2} = c \end{array}
$$
