【解析】

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \ln (1 + 2 t) ①, \\ 2 t - \int_ {1} ^ {y + t ^ {2}} e ^ {- u ^ {2}} d u = 0 ②, \end{array} \right.
$$

由②两边关于 $t$ 求导，则 $2 - e^{-(y + t^2)^2}\cdot \left(\frac{dy}{dt} +2t\right) = 0.$

当 $t = 0$ 时, $y = 1$, $2 - e^{-1} \cdot \frac{dy}{dt} = 0 \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dt} = 2e$.

则 $\left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{t=0}=\left.\frac{\frac{dy}{dt}}{\frac{dx}{dt}}\right|_{t=0}=\frac{2e}{2}=e.$
