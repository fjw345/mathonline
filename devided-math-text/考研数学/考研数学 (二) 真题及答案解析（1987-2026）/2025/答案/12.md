【解析】 $y = x - 1$

可得无水平渐近线、铅直渐近线，故求斜渐近线即可.

$$
k = \lim _ {x \to \infty} \frac {\sqrt [ 3 ]{x ^ {3} - 3 x ^ {2} + 1}}{x} = \lim _ {x \to \infty} \sqrt [ 3 ]{\frac {x ^ {2} - 3 x ^ {2} + 1}{x ^ {3}}} = 1
$$

$$
\begin{array}{l l} b = \lim _ {x \to \infty} \sqrt [ 3 ]{x ^ {3} - 3 x ^ {2} + 1} - x & = \lim _ {x \to \infty} x \cdot \left(\left(1 + \frac {1 - 3 x ^ {2}}{x ^ {3}}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{3}} - 1\right) \\ & = \lim _ {x \to \infty} x \cdot \frac {1}{3} \cdot \frac {1 - 3 x ^ {2}}{x ^ {3}} = - 1 \end{array}
$$

故y = x - 1.
