$$

$$

$$
A ^ {T} A = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 3 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 2 \end{array} \right)

$$

$$

$$
