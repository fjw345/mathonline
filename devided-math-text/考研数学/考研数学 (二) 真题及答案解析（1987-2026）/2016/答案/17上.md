解:由 $(x^{2} + y^{2})z + \ln z + 2(x + y + 1) = 0$ ，两边分别同时对 $x,y$ 求偏导数得:$\left\{ \begin{array}{l}2xz + (x^2 +y^2)\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} +\frac{1}{z}\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} +2 = 0(1)\\ 2yz + (x^2 +y^2)\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} +\frac{1}{z}\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} +2 = 0(2) \end{array} \right.$
