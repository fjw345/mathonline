【解析】把 $y_{2} = \mu (x)e^{x}$ 代入原方程得，

$$
(2 x - 1) e ^ {x} [ \mu^ {\prime \prime} (x) + 2 \mu^ {\prime} (x) + \mu (x) ] - (2 x + 1) e ^ {x} (\mu^ {\prime} (x) + \mu (x)) + 2 \mu (x) e ^ {x} = 0.
$$

即，$(2x - 1)\mu''(x) + (2x - 3)\mu'(x) = 0$，变量分离得

$\frac{d\mu'(x)}{\mu'(x)} = -\frac{2x - 3}{2x - 1} dx$ ，两边积分，

$\int \frac{d\mu'(x)}{\mu'(x)} = -\int \frac{2x - 3}{2x - 1} dx$ ，即 $\ln |\mu '(x)| = -\int (1 - \frac{2}{2x - 1})dx = -x + \ln |2x - 1| + \ln C_1,$

所以 $\mu'(x) = C_1(2x - 1)e^{-x}$，两边积分

$\int \mu^{\prime}(x)dx = C_{1}\int (2x - 1)e^{-x}dx = C_{1}[-(2x - 1) - 2]e^{-x} + C_{2} = C_{1}(-2x - 1)e^{-x} + C_{2},$

由已知 $\mu (-1) = C_1e + C_2 = e,\mu (0) = -C_1 + C_2 = -1$ ，解得

$C_1 = 1, C_2 = 0$ ，所以 $\mu(x) = -(2x + 1)e^{-x}$

根据二阶齐次线性方程解得结构得原方程通解为

$$
y (x) = D _ {1} y _ {1} + D _ {2} y _ {2} = D _ {1} e ^ {x} + D _ {2} \mu (x) e ^ {x}
$$

$= D_{1}e^{x} - D_{2}(2x + 1)$ ，其中 $D_{1}, D_{2}$ 为任意常数.
