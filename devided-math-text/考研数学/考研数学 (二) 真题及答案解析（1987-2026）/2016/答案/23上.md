【解析】(I) $\left|\lambda E-A\right|=\begin{vmatrix}-2&\lambda+3&0\\0&0&\lambda\end{vmatrix}=\lambda(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2).$

所以得 $A$ 的特征值为

$$
\lambda_ {1} = - 1, \lambda_ {2} = - 2, \lambda_ {3} = 0
$$

其对应的特征向量分别为

$$
\xi_ {1} = (1, 1, 0) ^ {T}, \xi_ {2} = (1, 2, 0) ^ {T} \xi_ {1} = (3, 2, 2) ^ {T}
$$

$$
P = \left(\xi_ {1}, \xi_ {2}, \xi_ {3}\right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 1 & 3 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{array} \right), \Lambda = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} - 1 & & \\ & - 2 & \\ & & 0 \end{array} \right)
$$

有 $P^{-1}AP=\Lambda$ , 易知 $P^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&-2\\-1&1&\frac{1}{2}\\0&0&\frac{1}{2}\end{pmatrix}$

所以 $A = P\Lambda P^{-1}$
