【解析】① $\int_{-\infty}^{0}\frac{1}{x^2} e^{\frac{1}{x}}dx = -\int_{-\infty}^{0}e^{\frac{1}{x}}d\frac{1}{x} = -e^{\frac{1}{x}}\Big|_{-\infty}^{0} = -(\lim_{x\to 0^{-}}e^{\frac{1}{x}} - \lim_{x\to -\infty}e^{\frac{1}{x}}) = 1$ ，收敛. $② \int_0^{+\infty}\frac{1}{x^2} e^{\frac{1}{x}}dx = -\int_0^{+\infty}e^{\frac{1}{x}}d\frac{1}{x} = -e^{\frac{1}{x}}\Big|_{0}^{+\infty} = -(\lim_{x\to +\infty}e^{\frac{1}{x}} - \lim_{x\to 0^{+}}e^{\frac{1}{x}}) = -1 + \lim_{x\to 0^{+}}e^{\frac{1}{x}} = +\infty$ ，发散.故选B.
