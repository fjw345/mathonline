答案: $a^4 - 4a^2$

【解析】

$$
\text {解析} \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} a & 0 & - 1 & 1 \\ 0 & a & 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & 1 & a & 0 \\ 1 & - 1 & 0 & a \end{array} \right| \stackrel {\text {第4行加}} {=} \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} a & 0 & - 1 & 1 \\ 0 & a & 1 & - 1 \\ 0 & 0 & a & a \\ 1 & - 1 & 0 & a \end{array} \right| \stackrel {\text {第1列}} {=} a \cdot \left| \begin{array}{c c c} a & 1 & - 1 \\ 0 & a & a \\ - 1 & 0 & a \end{array} \right| + (- 1) ^ {1 + 4} \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & - 1 & 1 \\ a & 1 & - 1 \\ 0 & a & a \end{array} \right|
$$

继续将第1列展开，

原式=

$$
a \cdot \left(a ^ {3} + (- 1) (- 1) ^ {1 + 3} \left| \begin{array}{c c} 1 & - 1 \\ a & a \end{array} \right|\right) + (- 1) (- 1) ^ {1 + 2} \cdot a \cdot \left| \begin{array}{c c} - 1 & 1 \\ a & a \end{array} \right| = a ^ {4} + 2 a \cdot (- 2 a) = a ^ {4} - 4 a ^ {2}
$$
