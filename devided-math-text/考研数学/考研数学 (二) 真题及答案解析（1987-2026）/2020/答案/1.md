解析:本题选D.考查了无穷小量的阶的比较，同时考查了变上限积分的函数的求导方法、洛必达法则等。用求导定阶法来判断。在 $x \to 0^{+}$时，

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left(\int_ {0} ^ {x} (e ^ {t ^ {2}} - 1) d t\right) ^ {\prime} = e ^ {x ^ {2}} - 1 \square x ^ {2}; \\ \left(\int_ {0} ^ {x} \ln (1 + \sqrt {t ^ {3}}) d t\right) ^ {\prime} = \ln (1 + \sqrt {x ^ {3}}) \square x ^ {\frac {3}{2}}; \\ \left(\int_ {0} ^ {\sin x} \sin t ^ {2} d t\right) ^ {\prime} = \sin (\sin x) ^ {2} \cos x \square x ^ {2}; \\ \left(\int_ {0} ^ {1 - \cos x} \sqrt {\sin^ {3} t} d t\right) ^ {\prime} = \sqrt {\sin^ {3} (1 - \cos x)} \sin x \square x \sqrt {\left(\frac {x ^ {2}}{2}\right) ^ {3}} \square \frac {\sqrt {2}}{4} x ^ {3} | x | 。 \end{array}
$$
