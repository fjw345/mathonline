(1) $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\bigg|_{(0,0)}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(x,0)-f(0,0)}{x}=\lim_{x\to0}\frac{x-0}{x}=1$
(2)因为 $f(x,y)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&xy,xy\neq0,\\ &x,y=0,\quad\text{，当}xy\neq0\text{时},\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}=y,\\ &y,x=0,\end{aligned}\right.$
此时 $\left.\frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{(0,0)}=\lim_{y\to0}\frac{f_{x}(0,y)-f_{x}(0,0)}{y}=\lim_{y\to0}\frac{y-1}{y}=\infty$
故 $\left.\frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{(0,0)}$ 不存在.
