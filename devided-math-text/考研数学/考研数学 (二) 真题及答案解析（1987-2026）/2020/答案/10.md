答案: $\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{9} -\frac{2}{9}$

【解析】:交换积分次序，

$\therefore$ 原式 $= \int_{0}^{1}dx\int_{0}^{x^{2}}\sqrt{x^{3} + 1} dy = \int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{x^3 + 1}\cdot x^2 dx = \frac{1}{3}\int_0^1\sqrt{x^3 + 1} d(x^3 +1) = \frac{1}{3} (x^3 +1)^{\frac{3}{2}}\cdot \frac{2}{3}\Bigg|_0^1$ 原式 $= \frac{2}{9} (2^{\frac{3}{2}} - 1) = \frac{2}{9} (2\sqrt{2} -1) = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{9} -\frac{2}{9}$
