【解析】（1）:由 $2f(x) + x^2 f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \frac{x^2 + 2x}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}} \dots \dots$ ①
得 $2f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + \frac{1}{x^2} f(x) = \frac{\frac{1}{x^2} + \frac{2}{x}}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{x^2}}} = \frac{2x + 1}{x\sqrt{1 + x^2}}$
则 $2x^{2}f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + f(x) = \frac{2x^{2} + x}{\sqrt{1 + x^{2}}} \dots \dots$ ②
$① \times 2 - ②$ 得:$3f(x) = \frac{3x}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}}$
