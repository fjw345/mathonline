【解析】 $y'' + 2y' + y = 0$, 所以特解方程: $\lambda^2 + 2\lambda + 1 = 0, (\lambda + 1)^2 = 0 \Rightarrow \lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = -1$;

$\therefore y _ {$通$} = \left(C _ {1} + C _ {2} x\right) e ^ {- x}; \quad y _ {$通$} ^ {\prime} = e ^ {- x} \left(C _ {2} - C _ {1} - C _ {2} x\right)$; 又$\because y (0) = 0, y ^ {\prime} (0) = 1$;

$$
\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} C _ {1} = 0 \\ C _ {2} - C _ {1} = 1 \end{array} \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} C _ {1} = 0 \\ C _ {2} = 1 \end{array} , \therefore y _ {\text {通}} = x e ^ {- x} \right. \right.
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\therefore \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} y (x) d x = \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} x e ^ {- x} d x \stackrel {\text {分部积分}} {=} - e ^ {- x} (x + 1) \Big | _ {0} ^ {+ \infty} = \lim _ {x \to + \infty} \Big [ - e ^ {- x} (x + 1) \Big ] - \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} \Big [ - e ^ {- x} (x + 1) \Big ] = 1} \\ & {\quad \Big | a, \quad 0, \quad - 1, \quad 1 \Big |} \end{array}
$$
