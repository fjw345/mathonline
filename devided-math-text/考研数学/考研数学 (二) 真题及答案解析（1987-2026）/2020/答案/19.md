【解析】: $\iint_{D} \frac{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}{x} \mathrm{d}\sigma = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{\frac{1}{\cos\theta}}^{\frac{2}{\cos\theta}} \frac{r}{r \cos\theta} r \mathrm{d}r = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{1}{\cos\theta} \cdot \left[\frac{1}{2} r^2\right]^{\frac{2}{\cos\theta}} \frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\cos\theta}$

$= \frac{3}{2} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{1}{\cos^3 \theta} \mathrm{d}\theta$

$= \frac{3}{2} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec^3 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta$

其中 $\int \sec^3 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta = \int \sec \theta \mathrm{d}\tan \theta = \sec \theta \tan \theta - \int \tan \theta \mathrm{d}\sec \theta$

$= \sec \theta \tan \theta - \int \sec \theta \tan^2 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta$

$= \sec \theta \tan \theta - \int \sec \theta (\sec^2 \theta - 1) \mathrm{d}\theta$

$= \sec \theta \tan \theta - \int \sec^3 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta + \int \sec \theta \mathrm{d}\theta$

$= \sec \theta \tan \theta + \ln |\sec \theta + \tan \theta| - \int \sec^3 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta$

所以 $\int \sec^3 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{1}{2} (\sec \theta \tan \theta + \ln |\sec \theta + \tan \theta|) + C$

则 $\iint_{D} \frac{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}{x} \mathrm{d}\sigma = \frac{3}{2} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec^3 \theta \mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} (\sec \theta \tan \theta + \ln |\sec \theta + \tan \theta|)^{\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{3}{4} [\sqrt{2} + \ln (1 + \sqrt{2})]$
