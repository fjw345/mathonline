答案: $-\sqrt{2}$

【解析】

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {d x}{d t} = \frac {t}{\sqrt {t ^ {2} + 1}}, \quad \frac {d y}{d t} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {t ^ {2} + 1}}, \quad \therefore \frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {1}{t}, \\ \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} = \frac {d y ^ {\prime}}{d x} = \frac {d (\frac {d y}{d x}) / d t}{d x / d t} = \frac {- \frac {1}{t ^ {2}}}{\frac {t}{\sqrt {t ^ {2} + 1}}} = - \frac {\sqrt {t ^ {2} + 1}}{t ^ {3}}, \quad \therefore \left. \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} \right| _ {t = 1} = - \sqrt {2}. \end{array} \right.
$$
