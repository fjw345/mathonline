【解析】 $(x^{2} + y^{2})^{3} = y^{4}$ 的极坐标方程 $r = \sin^2\theta$ ，由对称性可得: $\iint_{D}\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} dxdy = 0$ 所以 $\iint_{D}\frac{x + y}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} dxdy = \iint_{D}\frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} dxdy = 2\iint_{D_1}\frac{r\sin\theta}{r} rdrd\theta = 2\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}d\theta \int_0^{\sin^2\theta}r\sin \theta dr$ $= \int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^5\theta d\theta = -\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(1 - \cos^2\theta)^2 d\cos \theta = -\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(1 - 2\cos^2\theta +\cos^4\theta)d\cos \theta$ $= -( \cos \theta -\frac{2}{3}\cos^3\theta +\frac{1}{5}\cos^5\theta)\left|\frac{\pi}{2}\right|_{\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{43}{120}\sqrt{2}$
