【答案】 $yf(\frac{y^2}{x})$

【解析】 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=yf'(\frac{y^2}{x})(-\frac{y^2}{x^2})=-\frac{y^3}{x^2}f'(\frac{y^2}{x}),\quad\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=f(\frac{y^2}{x})+yf'(\frac{y^2}{x})\cdot\frac{2y}{x}=f(\frac{y^2}{x})+\frac{2y^2}{x}f'(\frac{y^2}{x})$

则 $2x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=2x\cdot\left[-\frac{y^3}{x^2}f'(\frac{y^2}{x})\right]+y\left[f(\frac{y^2}{x})+\frac{2y^2}{x}f'(\frac{y^2}{x})\right]=yf(\frac{y^2}{x})$
