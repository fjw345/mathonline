【答案】 $\frac{\cos 1 - 1}{4}$ 【解析】设 $F(x) = \int_{1}^{x}\frac{\sin t^2}{t} dt$ ，故有 $\int_0^1 f(x)dx = \int_0^1 xF(x)dx = \frac{1}{2}\int_0^1 F(x)dx^2 = \frac{1}{2} x^2 F(x)\Big|_0^1 -\frac{1}{2}\int_0^1 x^2 F'(x)dx$ $= -\frac{1}{2}\int_0^1 x^2\frac{\sin x^2}{x} dx = -\frac{1}{2}\int_0^1 x\sin x^2 dx = \frac{1}{4}\cos x^2\Big|_0^1 = \frac{\cos 1 - 1}{4}$
