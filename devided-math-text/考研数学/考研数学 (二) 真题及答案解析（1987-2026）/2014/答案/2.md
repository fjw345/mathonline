$$
\left| y = x + \sin \frac {1}{x} \right|
$$

$$
k = \lim _ {x \to \infty} \frac {y}{x} = \lim _ {x \to \infty} \frac {x + \sin \frac {1}{x}}{x} = 1
$$

$$
\left| \lim _ {x \to \infty} (y - x) = \lim _ {x \to \infty} \sin \frac {1}{x} = 0 \right|
$$

$\therefore y = x + \sin \frac{1}{x}$ 存在斜渐近线 $y = x$
