解:

设

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c c} 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots \\ 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \end{array} \right] \quad B = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c c c} 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & 2 \\ \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots \dots \\ 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & n \end{array} \right]
$$

$$
\left| \lambda E - A \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} \lambda - 1 & - 1 & \dots & - 1 \\ - 1 & \lambda - 1 & \dots & - 1 \\ \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots \\ - 1 & - 1 & \dots & \lambda - 1 \end{array} \right| = (\lambda - n) \lambda^ {n - 1}
$$

所以 A 的 n 个特征值为 $\lambda_{1}=n,\lambda_{2}=\cdots=\lambda_{n}=0$

又因为 $A$ 是一个实对称矩阵，所以 $A$ 可以相似对角化，且

$$
A \square \left[ \begin{array}{c c c c} n & & & \\ & 0 & & \\ & & \ddots & \\ & & & 0 \end{array} \right], \left| \lambda E - B \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} \lambda & 0 & \dots & 0 & - 1 \\ 0 & \lambda & \dots & 0 & - 2 \\ \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots \\ 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & \lambda - N \end{array} \right| = (\lambda - n) \lambda^ {n - 1}
$$

所以 B 的 n 个特征值为 $\lambda_{1}^{'}=n,\lambda_{2}^{'}=\cdots=\lambda_{n}^{'}=0$

$$
\text {又} \left| 0 E - B \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 - 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 - 2 \\ \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots \dots \\ 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 - n \end{array} \right|
$$

所以 $\mathrm{r(0E-B)} = 1$

故 $B$ 的 $\mathrm{n - 1}$ 重特征值0有 $\mathrm{n - 1}$ 个线性无关的特征向量
