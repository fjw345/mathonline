$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = r \cos \theta = \theta \cos \theta \\ y = r \sin \theta = \theta \sin \theta \end{array} \right.
$$

则$\frac {d y}{d x} = \frac {\frac {d y}{d \theta}}{\frac {d x}{d \theta}} = \frac {\sin \theta + \theta \cos \theta}{\cos \theta - \theta \sin \theta}$

$$
\left| \frac {d y}{d x} \right| _ {\theta = \frac {\pi}{2}} = \frac {1 + \frac {\pi}{2} \cdot 0}{0 - \frac {\pi}{2} \cdot 1} = - \frac {2}{\pi}
$$

当 $\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$ 时，$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \theta \cos \theta = 0 \\ y = \theta \sin \theta = \frac{\pi}{2} \end{array} \right.$

则切线方程为

$$
\left| (y - \frac {\pi}{2}) = - \frac {2}{\pi} (x - 0) \right|
$$

化简为

$$
y = - \frac {2}{\pi} x + \frac {\pi}{2}
$$
