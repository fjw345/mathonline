5、$\frac{f(x)}{x} = \frac{\arctan x}{x} = \frac{1}{1 + \xi^2}$. 故 $\xi^2 = \frac{x - \arctan x}{\arctan x}$.

$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\xi^2}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \arctan x}{x^2 \square a \arctan x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \arctan x}{x^3}$

$= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \frac{1}{1 + x^2}}{3x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^2}{3x^2 (1 + x^2)} = \frac{1}{3}$.
