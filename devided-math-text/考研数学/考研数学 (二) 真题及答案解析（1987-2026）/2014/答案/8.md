8、A

解析:

已知 $\alpha_{1}$ ， $\alpha_{2}$ ， $\alpha_{3}$ 无关设 $\lambda_1(\alpha_1 + \mathrm{k}\alpha_3) + \lambda_2(\alpha_2 + l\alpha_3) = 0$

即 $\lambda_1\alpha_1 + \lambda_2\alpha_2 + (\mathrm{k}\lambda_1 + l\lambda_2)\alpha_3 = 0$

$$
\Rightarrow \lambda_ {1} = \lambda_ {2} = k \lambda_ {1} + l \lambda_ {2} = 0
$$

从而 $\alpha_{1} + \mathrm{k}\alpha_{3},\alpha_{2} + l\alpha_{3}$ 无关反之，若 $\alpha_{1} + \mathrm{k}\alpha_{3}$ ， $\alpha_{2} + l\alpha_{3}$ 无关，不一定有 $\alpha_{1}$ ， $\alpha_{2}$ ， $\alpha_{3}$ 无关例如， $\alpha_{1} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\quad \alpha_{2} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix},\quad \alpha_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$
