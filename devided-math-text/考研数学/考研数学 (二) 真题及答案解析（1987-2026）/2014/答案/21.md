21.
解：
因 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}=2(y+1)$ 则

$f(x,y)=y^{2}+2y+\varphi(x)$

$\left\{\begin{aligned}f(y,y)=(y+1)^{2}-(2-y)\\ f(y,y)=y^{2}+2y+\varphi(y)\end{aligned}\right.$

则 $\varphi(y)=y-1$

故 $f(x,y)=y^{2}+2y+x-1$

$f(x,y)=0\Rightarrow x=-y^{2}-2y+1$

$V=\int_{0}^{2}\pi\left(f(x)+1\right)^{2}dx=\int_{0}^{2}\pi\left[f^{2}(x)+2f(x)+1\right]dx=\int_{0}^{2}\pi(2-x)dx=\pi\left(2x-\frac{x^{2}}{2}\right)\bigg|_{0}^{2}=2\pi$
