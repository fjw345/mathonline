解:积分区域 $D$ 关于 $y = x$ 对称，利用轮对称行，

$$
\begin{array}{l} \iint_ {D} \frac {x \sin (\pi \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}})}{x + y} d x d y = \iint_ {D} \frac {\mathrm{y} \sin (\pi \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}})}{x + y} d x d y \\ = \frac {1}{2} \iint_ {D} \frac {x \sin (\pi \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}})}{x + y} + \frac {\mathrm{y} \sin (\pi \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}})}{x + y} d x d y \\ = \frac {1}{2} \iint_ {D} \sin (\pi \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}) d x d y \\ = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} d \theta \int_ {1} ^ {2} \sin (\pi r) r \mathrm{d} r = - \frac {1}{4} \int_ {1} ^ {2} r d \cos (\pi r) \\ = - \frac {1}{4} r \cos (\pi r) | _ {1} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \int_ {1} ^ {2} \cos (\pi r) \mathrm{d} r \\ = - \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{4} = - \frac {3}{4} \end{array}
$$
