11、解:方程两边对 $\mathsf{x}$ 求偏导:

$$
\mathrm{e} ^ {2 y z} (2 \mathrm{y} \cdot \frac {\partial z}{\partial x}) + 2 \mathrm{x} + \frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = 0
$$

代入 $x = \frac{1}{2}, y = \frac{1}{2}$ 解得:

$$
\frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac {1}{\mathrm{e} ^ {z (\frac {1}{2} , \frac {1}{2})} + 1}
$$

两边对y求偏导

$$
\mathrm{e} ^ {2 y z} (2 z + 2 y \frac {\partial z}{\partial y}) + 2 y + \frac {\partial z}{\partial y} = 0
$$

代入 $x = \frac{1}{2}, y = \frac{1}{2}$ 解得:

$$
\frac {\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac {1 - z (\frac {1}{2} , \frac {1}{2}) \mathrm{e} ^ {z (\frac {1}{2} , \frac {1}{2})}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {z (\frac {1}{2} , \frac {1}{2})} + 1}
$$
