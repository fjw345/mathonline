【答案】C

【解析】 $f_{1}^{\prime}(x+1,e^{x})+e^{x}f_{2}^{\prime}(x+1,e^{x})=(x+1)^{2}+2x(x+1)$ ①

$f_{1}^{\prime}(x,x^{2})+2xf_{2}^{\prime}(x,x^{2})=4x\ln x+2x$ ②

分别将 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=0\\ y&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ， $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1\\ y&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 代入①②式有

$f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1)+f_{2}^{\prime}(1,1)=1,\quad f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1)+2f_{2}^{\prime}(1,1)=2$

联立可得 $f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1)=0,\quad f_{2}^{\prime}(1,1)=1,\quad df(1,1)=f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1)dx+f_{2}^{\prime}(1,1)dy=dy$ ，故选C.
