【解析】令 $Z(a,b) = \int_{-\pi}^{\pi}(x - a\cos x - b\sin x)^2 dx$

$$
\int Z _ {a} ^ {\prime} = 2 \int_ {- \pi} ^ {\pi} (x - a \cos x - b \sin x) (- \cos x) d x = 0\tag{1}
$$

$$
Z _ {b} ^ {\prime} = 2 \int_ {- \pi} ^ {\pi} (x - a \cos x - b \sin x) (- \sin x) d x = 0\tag{2}
$$

由（1）得

$$
2 a \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} \cos^ {2} x d x = 0
$$

故$a = 0, a _ {1} = 0$

由（2）得

【答案】A

$$
b = \frac {\int_ {0} ^ {\pi} x \sin x d x}{\int_ {0} ^ {\pi} \sin^ {2} x d x} = 2 \quad b _ {1} = 2
$$
