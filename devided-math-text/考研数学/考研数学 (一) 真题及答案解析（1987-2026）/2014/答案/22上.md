(I) $F_{Y}(y) = P\{Y\leq y\} = P\{X = 1\} P\{Y\leq y\mid X = 1\} +P\{X = 2\} P\{Y\leq y\mid X = 2\}$

$$
= \frac {1}{2} P \{Y \leq y \mid X = 1 \} + \frac {1}{2} P \{Y \leq y \mid X = 2 \},
$$

$y < 0$ 时， $F_{Y}(y) = 0;$

当 $0 \leq y < 1$ 时，$F_{Y}(y) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{y}{1} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{y}{2} = \frac{3y}{4}$;

当 $1 \leq y < 2$ 时，

$$
F _ {Y} (y) = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {y}{2} = \frac {y}{4} + \frac {1}{2};
$$
