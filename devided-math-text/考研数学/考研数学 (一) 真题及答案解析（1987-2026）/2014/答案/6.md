【解析】由 $(\alpha_{1} + k\alpha_{3},\alpha_{2} + l\alpha_{3}) = (\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3})\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 0\\ 0 & 1\\ k & l \end{array} \right)$ 知，
当 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 线性无关时，因为 $\left| \begin{array}{cc}1 & 0\\ 0 & 1 \end{array} \right|\neq 0$
所以 $\alpha_{1} + k\alpha_{3},\alpha_{2} + l\alpha_{3}$ 线性无关
反之不成立 如当 $\alpha_{3} = 0$ ， $\alpha_{1}$ 与 $\alpha_{2}$ 线性无关时， $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 线性相关
【答案】A
