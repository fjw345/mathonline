【解】由 $y^{3}+xy^{2}+x^{2}y+6=0$ 得

$3y^{2}\frac{dy}{dx}+y^{2}+2xy\frac{dy}{dx}+2xy+x^{2}\frac{dy}{dx}=0$，解得

$\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{2xy+y^{2}}{x^{2}+2xy+3y^{2}}$，由 $\frac{dy}{dx}=0$ 得 $y=-2x$，代入原式得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1\\ y&=-2\end{aligned}\right.$ 。

$\frac{d^{2}y}{dx^{2}}=-\frac{(2y+2x\frac{dy}{dx}+2y\frac{dy}{dx})(x^{2}+2xy+3y^{2})-(2xy+y^{2})(2x+2y+2x\frac{dy}{dx}+6y\frac{dy}{dx})}{(x^{2}+2xy+3y^{2})^{2}}$

将 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1\\ y&=-2\end{aligned}\right.$ 代入得 $\frac{d^{2}y}{dx^{2}}=\frac{4}{9}>0$，故 x=1 为最小点，最小值为 y=-2。
