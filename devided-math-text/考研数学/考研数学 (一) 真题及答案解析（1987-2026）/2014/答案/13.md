【解析】 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & a \\ 0 & -1 & 2 \\ a & 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$

因为 $\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}+\lambda_{3}=0,\lambda_{1}+\lambda_{2}+\lambda_{3}\models A$ I，负惯性指数为 1

∴设 $\lambda_{1}<0$ ，从而 $\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\geq0$

$$
\therefore \mid A \mid \leq 0
$$

① 若 $|A| < 0$，则 $\lambda_1 < 0, \lambda_2 > 0, \lambda_3 > 0$. 此时符合题意而 $|A| = a^2 - 4$. $\therefore a^2 - 4 < 0$. 即 $-2 < a < 2$.

② 若 $|A| = 0$ ，则 $\lambda_1 < 0, \lambda_2 > 0, \lambda_3 = 0$ ，此时 $a = \pm 2$

当 $a = 2$ 时

$$
A = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & - 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 0 \end{array} \right) \quad \left| \lambda E - A \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & \lambda + 1 & - 2 \\ - 2 & - 2 & \lambda \end{array} \right| = \lambda (\lambda + 3) (\lambda - 3)
$$

$$
\left| \lambda_ {1} = - 3, \lambda_ {2} = 3, \lambda_ {3} = 0 \right|
$$

$\therefore a = 2$ 符合题意当 $a = -2$ 时

$$
A = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & - 2 \\ 0 & - 1 & 2 \\ - 2 & 2 & 0 \end{array} \right) \quad \left| \lambda \mathrm{E} - A \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & \lambda + 1 & - 2 \\ 2 & - 2 & \lambda \end{array} \right| = \lambda (\lambda + 3) (\lambda - 3)
$$

$\lambda_{1}=-3,\lambda_{2}=3,\lambda_{3}=0$ 符合题意综上， $a$ 的取值范围是 $-2\leq a\leq 2$
