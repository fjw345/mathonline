【解】令 $\Sigma_0: z = 1$ ($x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$)，取下侧，其中 $\Sigma$ 与 $\Sigma_0$ 围成的几何体为 $\Omega$，

由高斯公式得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \iint_ {\Sigma + \Sigma_ {0}} (x - 1) ^ {3} d y d z + (y - 1) ^ {3} d z d x + (z - 1) d x d y = - \iiint_ {\Omega} [ 3 (x - 1) ^ {2} + 3 (y - 1) ^ {2} + 1 ] d v \\ = - \iiint_ {\Omega} [ 3 (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) - 6 x - 6 y + 7 ] d v = - \iiint_ {\Omega} [ 3 (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) + 7 ] d v \\ = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d z \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq z} [ 3 (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) + 7 ] d v = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d z \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {\sqrt {z}} (3 r ^ {3} + 7 r) d r \end{array}
$$
