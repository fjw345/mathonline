【解】

$$
\frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = e ^ {x} \cos y \cdot f ^ {\prime}, \quad \frac {\partial z}{\partial y} = - e ^ {x} \sin y \cdot f ^ {\prime},
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial x ^ {2}} = e ^ {x} \cos y \cdot f ^ {\prime} + e ^ {2 x} \cos^ {2} y \cdot f ^ {\prime \prime}, \frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial y ^ {2}} = - e ^ {x} \cos y \cdot f ^ {\prime} + e ^ {2 x} \sin^ {2} y \cdot f ^ {\prime \prime},
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial x ^ {2}} + \frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial y ^ {2}} = e ^ {2 x} f ^ {r},
$$

令 $u = e^{x}\cos y$ ，由 $\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial x^2} +\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial y^2} = (4z + e^x\cos y)e^{2x}$ 得

$f^{\prime \prime}(u) = 4f(u) + u$ ，或 $f''(u) - 4f(u) = u$

解得 $f(u) = C_1e^{-2u} + C_2e^{2u} - \frac{1}{4} u,$

由 $f(0) = 0, f'(0) = 0$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} C_1 + C_2 = 0 \\ -2C_1 + 2C_2 - \frac{1}{4} = 0 \end{array} \right.$，解得 $C_1 = -\frac{1}{16}, C_2 = \frac{1}{16}$，

故 $f(u) = -\frac{1}{16} (e^{-2u} - e^{2u}) - \frac{1}{4} u$。
