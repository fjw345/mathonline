【解析】在点（1，0，1）处， $z_{x}\left|\begin{matrix} & (1,0,1) = [2x(1 - \sin y) - y^{2}\cos x] \\ & (1,0,1) = 2\end{matrix}\right|$

$z_{y}\left|\begin{matrix} & (1,0,1) = [-x^{2}\cos y + 2y(1 - \sin x)] \\ & (1,0,1) = -1\end{matrix}\right|$

切平面方程为 $z_{x}(x - 1) + z_{y}(y - 0) + (-1)(z - 1) = 0$

即 $2x - y - z - 1 = 0$
