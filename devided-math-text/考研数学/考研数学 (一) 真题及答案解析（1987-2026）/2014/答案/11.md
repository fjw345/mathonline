【解析】 $xy' + y(\ln x - \ln y) = 0$ 即 $xy' + y\ln \frac{x}{y} = 0$ 两边同除 $x$ 得

$y' + \frac{y}{x}\ln \frac{x}{y} = 0$

令 $u = \frac{y}{x}$，则 $y = xu$，$\frac{dy}{dx} = u + x\frac{du}{dx}$ 代入上式得

$u + x\frac{du}{dx} + u\ln \frac{1}{u} = 0$ 整理得

$\frac{du}{u(\ln u - 1)} = \frac{1}{x} dx$ 两端积分得

$\int \frac{du}{u(\ln u - 1)} = \int \frac{1}{x} dx + \ln C$

$\int \frac{d(\ln u - 1)}{\ln u - 1} = \int \frac{1}{x} dx + \ln C$

$\ln u - 1 = cx$

$u = e^{cx + 1}$

$y = xe^{cx + 1}$

将 $y(1) = e^3$ 代入上式得 $C = 2$

$\therefore y = xe^{2x + 1}$
