(3) $F_{Z}(z)=P\{U+X\leq z\}=P\{U+X\leq z|U=0\}P\{U=0\}+P\{U+X\leq z|U=1\}P\{U=1\}$
$=\frac{P\{U+X\leq z,U=0\}}{P\{U=0\}}P\{U=0\}+\frac{P\{U+X\leq z,U=1\}}{P\{U=1\}}P\{U=1\}$
$=P\{X\leq z,X>Y\}+P\{1+X\leq z,X\leq Y\}$

又
$P\{X\leq z,X>Y\}=\begin{cases}0,z<0\\ \frac{3}{2}z^{2}-z^{3},0\leq z<1\\ \frac{1}{2},z\geq 1\end{cases}$

$P\{X+1\leq z,X\leq Y\}=\begin{cases}0,z<1\\ 2(z-1)^{\frac{3}{2}}-\frac{3}{2}(z-1)^{2},1\leq z<2\\ \frac{1}{2},z\geq 2\end{cases}$

所以 $F_{Z}(z)=\begin{cases}0,z<0\\ \frac{3}{2}z^{2}-z^{3},0\leq z<1\\ \frac{1}{2}+2(z-1)^{\frac{3}{2}}-\frac{3}{2}(z-1)^{2},1\leq z<2\\ 1,z\geq 2\end{cases}$
