【答案】

(I) $T$ 的概率密度

$$
F _ {z} (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {9 x ^ {3}}{\theta^ {2}}, & 0 < x < \theta \\ 0, & \text {其他} \end{array} \right.
$$

(Ⅱ) $a = \frac{10}{9}$

【解析】（1）根据题意， $X_{1}, X_{2}, X_{3}$ 独立同分布，T 的分布函数为

$$
\begin{array}{r l} F _ {T} (t) & = P \{\max (X _ {1}, X _ {2}, X _ {3}) \leq t \} = P \{X _ {1} \leq t, X _ {2} \leq t, X _ {3} \leq t \} \\ & = P \{X _ {1} \leq t \} P \{X _ {2} \leq t \} P \{X _ {3} \leq t \} = \left(P \{X _ {1} \leq t \}\right) ^ {3} \end{array}
$$

当 $t < 0$ 时，$F_{T}(t) = 0$；

当 $0 < t < \theta$ 时，$F_{T}(t) = \left(\int_{0}^{t}\frac{3x^{2}}{\theta^{3}} d\theta\right)^{3} = \frac{t^{\theta}}{\theta^{\theta}};$

当 $t \geq 0$ 时，$F_{T}(t) = 1$

所以 $f_{T}(t) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{9t^{8}}{\theta^{9}}, & 0 < t < \theta \\ 0, & \text{others} \end{array} \right.$

(2) $E(aT) = aET = a\int_{0}^{\theta}t\frac{9t^{3}}{\theta^{2}} dt = \frac{9}{10}\alpha \theta ,$

根据题意，aT 为 $\theta$ 的无偏估计，

则 $E(aT) = \frac{9}{10} a\theta = \theta$ ，即 $a = \frac{10}{9}$
