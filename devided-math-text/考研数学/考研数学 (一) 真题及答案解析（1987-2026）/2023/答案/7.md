【答案】（D）.

【解析】

解析:由题意可知:设存在常数 $k_{1}, k_{2}, t_{1}, t_{2}$ 使得:

$$
\gamma = k _ {1} \alpha_ {1} + k _ {2} \alpha_ {2}; \gamma = t _ {1} \beta_ {1} + t _ {2} \beta_ {2}
$$

即 $k_{1}\alpha_{1} + k_{2}\alpha_{2} = t_{1}\beta_{1} + t_{2}\beta_{2}$，可得:$\begin{cases} k_1 + 2k_2 = 2t_1 + t_2; \\ 2k_1 + k_2 = 5t_1 + 0 \cdot t_2; \\ 3k_1 + k_2 = 9t_1 + t_2; \end{cases}$

解得: $k_{1} = -3k_{2}$

即 $\gamma = k_{1}\alpha_{1} + k_{2}\alpha_{2} = -3\dot{k}_{2}\alpha_{1} + k_{2}\alpha_{2} = k_{2}\left[ \begin{array}{c} - 1\\ -5\\ -8 \end{array} \right] = k\left[ \begin{array}{l}1\\ 5\\ 8 \end{array} \right],k\in R$ ，选（D）.
