【解析】由参数方程可得

$$
f (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {x}{3} \sin \frac {x}{3}, & x \geq 0 \\ - x \sin x, & x < 0 \end{array} \right.
$$

$\lim_{x\to 0^{+}}f(x) = \lim_{x\to 0^{-}}f(x) = 0 = f(0),f(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续.

$$
f ^ {\prime \prime} (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {1}{3} \sin \frac {x}{3} + \frac {x}{9} \cos \frac {x}{3}, x \geq 0 \\ - \sin x - x \cos x, x < 0 \end{array} , \right.
$$

$\lim_{x\to 0^{+}}f^{\prime}(x) = \lim_{x\to 0^{-}}f^{\prime}(x) = 0 = f^{\prime}(0),f^{\prime}(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续.

$$
f _ {+} ^ {\prime \prime} (0) = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} \frac {f ^ {\prime} (x) - f ^ {\prime} (0)}{x} = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} \frac {\frac {1}{3} \sin \frac {x}{3} + \frac {x}{9} \cos \frac {x}{3}}{x} = \frac {2}{9};
$$

$$
f _ {-} ^ {\prime \prime} (0) = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {-}} \frac {f ^ {\prime} (x) - f ^ {\prime} (0)}{x} = \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {-}} \frac {- \sin x - x \cos x}{x} = - 2
$$

$\lim_{x\to 0}f^{\prime \prime}(x)\neq \lim_{x\to 0}f^{\prime \prime}(x),f^{\prime \prime}(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处不可导，选C.
