【答案】 $ab = -2$

【解析】

$$
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {a x + b x ^ {2} + \ln (1 + x)}{e ^ {x ^ {2}} - \cos x} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {a x + b x ^ {2} + x - \frac {1}{2} x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{3} x ^ {3} - \frac {1}{4} x ^ {4} + o (x ^ {4})}{1 + x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} (x ^ {4}) - \left(1 - \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} + \frac {1}{4 !} x ^ {4}\right) + o (x ^ {4})} \\ = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {(a + 1) x + \left(b - \frac {1}{2}\right) x ^ {2} + o (x ^ {2})}{\frac {3}{2} x ^ {2} + o (x ^ {2})} \\ a + 1 = 0 \quad a = - 1, b - \frac {1}{2} = \frac {3}{2} \quad b = 2, \\ a b = - 2. \end{array}
$$
