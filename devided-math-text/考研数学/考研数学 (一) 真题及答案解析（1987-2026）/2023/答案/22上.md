【解析】

(1) $Cov(X,Y) = E(XY) - E(XY)E(XY)$

$$
E (X Y) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x y f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \sqsupseteq 1} x y \frac {2}{\pi} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = 0
$$

$$
E (X) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1} x \frac {2}{\pi} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = 0
$$

所以 $Cov(X,Y) = 0$

(2) 当 -1 < x < 1 时

$$
\begin{array}{r l} f _ {X} (x) & = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {- \sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} ^ {\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} \frac {2}{\pi} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} y = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} \frac {2}{\pi} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} y \\ & = \frac {4}{\pi} [ x ^ {2} \sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{3} (1 - x ^ {2}) ^ {\frac {3}{2}} ] \end{array}
$$

$$
\therefore f _ {X} (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {4}{\pi} [ x ^ {2} \sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{3} (1 - x ^ {2}) ^ {\frac {3}{2}} ], & - 1 < x < 1 \\ 0, & \text {其他} \end{array} \right.
$$

同理可得$\therefore f_{Y}(\mathbf{y})=\begin{cases}\frac{4}{\pi}[y^{2}\sqrt{1-y^{2}}+\frac{1}{3}(1-y^{2})^{\frac{3}{2}}],&-1<y<1\\0,&\text{其他}\end{cases}$

$f(x,y)\neq f_{X}(x)f_{Y}(y),X$ 与 $Y$ 不相互独立；
