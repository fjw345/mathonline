9. 设 $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n}$ 为来自总体 $N(\mu, \sigma^{2})$ 的简单随机样本，$Y_{1}, Y_{2}, \cdots, Y_{m}$ 为来自总体 $N(\mu, 2\sigma^{2})$ 的简单随机样本，其两样本之间相互独立，记

$\overline{X} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_{i}, \overline{Y} = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{n} Y_{i}, S_{1}^{2} = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_{i} - \overline{X})^{2} S_{2}^{2} = \frac{1}{m-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (Y_{i} - \overline{Y})^{2}$ 则（）

(A) $\frac{S_{1}^{2}}{S_{2}^{2}} \sim F(n, m)$
(B) $\frac{S_{1}^{2}}{S_{2}^{2}} \sim F(n-1, m-1)$
(C) $\frac{2S_{1}^{2}}{S_{2}^{2}} \sim F(n, m)$
(D) $\frac{2S_{1}^{2}}{S_{2}^{2}} \sim F(n-1, m-1)$
