【解析】: $Q = P\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 0 & 0\\ 1 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right)$，则 $Q^{-1} = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0\\ -1 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right)P^{-1}$，

$$
\text {故} Q ^ {- 1} A Q = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ - 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) P ^ {- 1} A P \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ - 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 2 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 2 \end{array} \right)
$$

故选（B）。
