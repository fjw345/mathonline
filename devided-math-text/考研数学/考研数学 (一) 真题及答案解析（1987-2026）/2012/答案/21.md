【解析】:1）由 $r(A^T A) = r(A) = 2$ 可得，

$$
\left| \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ - 1 & 0 & a \end{array} \right| = a + 1 = 0 \Rightarrow a = - 1
$$

$$
f = x ^ {T} A ^ {T} A x = \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 4 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c} x _ {1} \\ x _ {2} \\ x _ {3} \end{array} \right) \tag {2}
$$

则矩阵 $B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 2 \\ 2 & 2 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$

$$
\left| \lambda E - B \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 2 & 0 & - 2 \\ 0 & \lambda - 2 & - 2 \\ - 2 & - 2 & \lambda - 4 \end{array} \right| = \lambda (\lambda - 2) (\lambda - 6) = 0
$$

解得 $B$ 矩阵的特征值为: $\lambda_1 = 0;\lambda_2 = 2;\lambda_3 = 6$

对于 $\lambda_{1}=0$ , 解 $(\lambda_{1}E-B)X=0$ 得对应的特征向量为: $\eta_{1}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\ 1\\ -1\end{pmatrix}$

对于 $\lambda_{2}=2$ , 解 $(\lambda_{2}E-B)X=0$ 得对应的特征向量为: $\eta_{2}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix}$

对于 $\lambda_{3} = 6$ 解 $(\lambda_3E - B)X = 0$ 得对应的特征向量为: $\eta_{3} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$

将 $\eta_{1},\eta_{2},\eta_{3}$ 单位化可得:

$$
\alpha_ {1} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {3}} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 1 \\ - 1 \end{array} \right), \alpha_ {2} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ - 1 \\ 0 \end{array} \right), \alpha_ {3} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {6}} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{array} \right)
$$

$$
Q = \left(\alpha_ {1}, \alpha_ {2}, \alpha_ {3}\right)
$$
