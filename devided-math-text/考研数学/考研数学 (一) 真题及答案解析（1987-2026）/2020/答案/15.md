$$
f _ {x} ^ {\prime} = 3 x ^ {2} - y = 0 \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x = 0 \\ y = 0 \end{array} \right. \text {或} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x = \frac {1}{6} \\ y = \frac {1}{1 2} \end{array} \right.
$$

【详解】

$$
\text {又} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} f _ {x x} ^ {"} = 6 x \\ f _ {x y} ^ {"} = - 1 \\ f _ {y y} ^ {"} = 4 8 y \end{array} \right. \quad \text {当} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x = 0 \\ y = 0 \end{array} \right. \quad \text {时} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} A = 0 \\ B = - 1 \\ C = 0 \end{array} \right.
$$

$AC-B^{2}=-1<0$ ，不为极值点

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} {x = \frac {1}{6}} \\ {y = \frac {1}{1 2}} \end{array} \right. \text {时} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} {A = 1} \\ {B = - 1} \\ {C = 4} \end{array} \right. \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} {A C - B ^ {2} = 3 > 0} \\ {A = 1 > 0} \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} {x = \frac {1}{6}} \\ {y = \frac {1}{1 2}} \end{array} \right.,} \\ & {\text {极小值为} f (\frac {1}{6}, \frac {1}{1 2}) = - \frac {1}{2 1 6}} \end{array}
$$

为极小值点
