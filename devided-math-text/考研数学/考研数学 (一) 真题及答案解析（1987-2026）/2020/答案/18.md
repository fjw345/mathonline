【详解】将曲面 $Z = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ 向 $xoy$ 面投影得 $D_{xy}$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {D _ {x y} \text {为} 1 \leq x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 4, \text {又} Z _ {x} ^ {\prime} = \frac {x}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}}, Z _ {y} ^ {\prime} = \frac {y}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}}} \\ & {\quad I = - \iint_ {D _ {x y}} \{[ x f (x y) + 2 x - y ] (- Z _ {x} ^ {\prime}) + [ y f (x y) + 2 y + x ] (- Z _ {y} ^ {\prime}) + [ \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} f (x y) + \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} ] \} d x d y} \\ & {= - \iint_ {D _ {x y}} \{\frac {1}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} [ - x f (x y) - 2 x ^ {2} + x y - y ^ {2} f (x y) - 2 y ^ {2} - x y ] + \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} [ f (x y) + 1 ] \} d x d y} \\ & {= - \iint_ {D _ {x y}} - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} f (x y) - 2 \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} + \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} f (x y) + \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} d x d y} \\ & {= \iint_ {D _ {x y}} \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} d x d y} \\ & {\qquad = \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} d \theta \int_ {1} ^ {2} r \cdot r d r} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {1 4}{3} \pi} \end{array}
$$
