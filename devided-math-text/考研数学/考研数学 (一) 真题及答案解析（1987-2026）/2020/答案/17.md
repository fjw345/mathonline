【详解】(I)

$$
\rho = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \left| \frac {a _ {n + 1}}{a _ {n}} \right| = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {n + \frac {1}{2}}{n + 1} = 1
$$

则 $R = \frac{1}{\rho} = 1$ ，所以当 $\left|x\right| < 1$ 时，幂级数 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ 收敛.

$S(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n x^n$, (II) 令

$$
\begin{array}{r l} S ^ {\prime} (x) & = \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} n a _ {n} x ^ {n - 1} = \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} (n + 1) a _ {n + 1} x ^ {n} = \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} (n + 1) a _ {n + 1} x ^ {n} + a _ {1} \\ & = \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} (n + \frac {1}{2}) a _ {n} x ^ {n} + 1 = \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} n a _ {n} x ^ {n} + \frac {1}{2} \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} a _ {n} x ^ {n} + 1 \\ & = x S ^ {\prime} (x) + \frac {1}{2} S (x) + 1 \\ (1 - x) S ^ {\prime} (x) & = \frac {1}{2} [ 2 + S (x) ] \\ \int \frac {d S (x)}{2 + S (x)} & = \int \frac {d x}{2 (1 - x)} \\ \ln | 2 + S (x) | & = - \frac {1}{2} \ln | 1 - x | + \ln C _ {1} \\ 2 + S (x) & = \frac {C}{\sqrt {1 - x}} \end{array}
$$

因为 $S(0) = 0$ ，所以和函数: $S(x) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{1 - x}} -2,\quad (-1 < x < 1)$
