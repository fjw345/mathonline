【解析】 $f_{1}^{\prime}(x + 1,e^{x}) + e^{x}f_{2}^{\prime}(x + 1,e^{x}) = (x + 1)^{2} + 2x(x + 1)$

①

$$
f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (x, x ^ {2}) + 2 x f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (x, x ^ {2}) = 4 x \ln x + 2 x\tag{②}
$$

分别将 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = 0\\ y = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = 1\\ y = 1 \end{array} \right.$ 代入 $①②$ 式有

$$
f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) = 1, \quad f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) + 2 f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) = 2
$$

联立可得 $f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1) = 0$ ， $f_{2}^{\prime}(1,1) = 1$ ， $df(1,1) = f_1'(1,1)dx + f_z'(1,1)dy = dy$ ，故选C.
