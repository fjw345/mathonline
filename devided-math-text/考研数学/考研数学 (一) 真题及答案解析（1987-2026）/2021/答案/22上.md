【答案】(1) $X \sim f(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & 0 < x < 1 \\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{cases}$; (2) $f_{Z}(z) = (F_{Z}(z))' = \begin{cases} \frac{2}{(z + 1)^{2}}, & z \geq 1 \\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{cases}$; (3) $-1 + 2\ln 2$. 【解析】(1)由题知: $X \sim f(x) = \begin{cases} 1, & 0 < x < 1 \\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{cases}$. (2) 由 $Y = 2 - X$, 即 $Z = \frac{2 - X}{X}$, 先求 $Z$ 的分布函数. $F_{Z}(z) = P\{Z \leq z\} = P\left\{\frac{2 - X}{X} \leq z\right\} = P\left\{\frac{2}{X} - 1 \leq z\right\}$ 当 $z < 1$ 时, $F_{Z}(z) = 0$.
