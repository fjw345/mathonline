$$
\left[ L _ {x} ^ {\prime} = 2 x \lambda + 4 \mu = 0 \right.
$$

$$
L _ {y} ^ {\prime} = 4 y \lambda + 2 \mu = 0
$$

$L_{z}^{\prime}=2z-\lambda+\mu=0$ 解得驻点: $(4,1,12),(-8,-2,66)$

$$
L _ {\lambda} ^ {\prime} = x ^ {2} + 2 y ^ {2} - z = 6
$$

$$
\left\lfloor L _ {\mu} ^ {\prime} = 4 x + 2 y + z = 3 0 \right.
$$

故 C 上的点 $(-8, -2, 66)$ 到 xOy 坐标面距离最大为 66.
