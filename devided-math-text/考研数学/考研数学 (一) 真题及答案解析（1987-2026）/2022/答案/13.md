【答案】 $\left[4e^{-2},+\infty\right)$

【解析】原式等价为 $(x^{2}+y^{2})e^{(x+y)}\leq k$

令 $f(x,y)=\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)e^{-(x+y)}$

$f_{x}^{\prime}=2xe^{(x-y)}-\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)e^{(x+y)}=\left(2x-x^{2}-y^{2}\right)e^{(x+y)}=0$

$f_{y}^{\prime}=2ye^{-(x-v)}-\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)e^{-(x+y)}=\left(2y-x^{2}-y^{2}\right)e^{-(x+v)}=0$

$\therefore\begin{cases}2x-x^{2}-y^{2}=0\\2y-x^{2}-y^{2}=0\end{cases}$

得 x=y=1 或 x=y=0

$f(0,0)=0,f(1,1)=2e^{-2}$

$\therefore k\geq2e^{-2}.$ 再考虑边界点 $(2,0),(0,2)\Rightarrow f_{max}=4e^{-2}=k_{min}$
