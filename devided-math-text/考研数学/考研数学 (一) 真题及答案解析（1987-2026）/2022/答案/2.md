【答案】(B)
【解析】 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = yf\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) + xyf'\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\left(\frac{-y}{x^2}\right) = yf\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) - \frac{y^2}{x} f'\left(\frac{y}{x}\right),$
$\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = xf\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) + xyf'\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\frac{1}{x} = xf\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) + yf'\left(\frac{y}{x}\right),$
则 $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 2xyf\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)$，故 $2xyf\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) = y^2 (\ln y - \ln x)$，
则 $f\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\frac{y}{x}\ln \frac{y}{x}$，令 $\frac{y}{x} = u$，所以 $f(u) = \frac{1}{2} u\ln u$，
故 $f(1) = 0, f'(1) = \frac{1}{2}$.

故选(B).
