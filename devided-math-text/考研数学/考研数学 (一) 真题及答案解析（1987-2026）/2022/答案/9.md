【答案】(A)

【解析】 $E\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}X_{i}^{2}\right) = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}E\left(X_{i}^{2}\right) = E\left(X_{1}^{2}\right) = \mu_{2},$

$$
D \left(\frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} D \left(X _ {i} ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(E \left(X _ {i} ^ {4}\right) - \left[ E \left(X _ {i} ^ {2}\right) \right] ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{n} \left(\mu_ {4} - \mu_ {2} ^ {2}\right),
$$

根据切比雪夫不等式， $P\left\{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}X_{i}^{2} - \mu_{2}\right|\geq \varepsilon \Bigg\} \leq \frac{D\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}X_{i}^{2}\right)}{\varepsilon^{2}} = \frac{\mu_{4} - \mu_{2}^{2}}{n\varepsilon^{2}}$ ，故选(A).
