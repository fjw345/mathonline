【解析】 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial\tau}\big|_{(0,1)} = \left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x},\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)\big|_{(0,1)}\cdot (\cos \alpha ,\sin \alpha) = (2x,4y)\big|_{(0,1)}\cdot (\cos \alpha ,\sin \alpha)$ $= 4\sin \alpha \leq 4$ ，其中方向 $\tau = (\cos \alpha ,\sin \alpha)$ ，故最大值为4.
