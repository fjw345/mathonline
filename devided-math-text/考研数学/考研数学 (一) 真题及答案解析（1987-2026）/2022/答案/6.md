【解析】令 $y=\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}$ ，则 $\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\O&B\end{pmatrix}y=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\O&B\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}=0,\quad\begin{pmatrix}B&A\\O&A\end{pmatrix}y=\begin{pmatrix}B&A\\O&A\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}=0,$
所以 $\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\O&B\end{pmatrix}y=0$ 与 $\begin{pmatrix}B&A\\O&A\end{pmatrix}y=0$ 同解等价于 $\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\O&B\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}=0$ 与 $\begin{pmatrix}B&A\\O&A\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}=0$ 同解.
即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}(A&B)\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}&=Au+Bx=0\\ Bx=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 与 $\left\{\begin{aligned}(B&A)\begin{pmatrix}u\\x\end{pmatrix}&=Bu+Ax=0\\ Ax=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 同解.
所以等价于 $\left\{\begin{aligned}Au&=0\\ Bx&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 与 $\left\{\begin{aligned}Bu&=0\\ Ax&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 同解.
因为Ax=0与Bx=0同解，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}Au&=0\\ Bx&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 与 $\left\{\begin{aligned}Bu&=0\\ Ax&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 同解.所以C正确.
