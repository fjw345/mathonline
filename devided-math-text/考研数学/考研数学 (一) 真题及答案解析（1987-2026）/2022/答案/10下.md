由二维正态分布的概率密度 $f(x,y)=\frac{1}{2\pi\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}\sqrt{1-\rho^{2}}}e^{-\frac{1}{2(1-\rho^{2})}\left[\frac{(x-\mu_{1})^{2}}{\sigma_{1}^{2}}-\frac{2\rho(x-\mu_{1})(y-\mu_{2})}{\sigma_{1}\sigma_{2}}+\frac{(y-\mu_{2})^{2}}{\sigma_{2}^{2}}\right]}$ 可知，
 $(X,Y)\sim N(0,0;1,2;\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2})$ ，故选(D).

法二: $EY = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}yf(x,y)dxdy = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f_X(x)dx\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}yf_{Y|X}(y|x)dx$ $= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}E(Y|X = x)dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{x}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}dx = 0$
$EXY = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xyf(x,y)dxdy = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xf_X(x)dx\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}yf_{Y|X}(y|x)dx$
$= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} xe^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}E(Y|X = x)dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{x^2}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}dx = EX^2 = 1$
$EY^{2} = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}y^{2}f(x,y)dxdy = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}f_X(x)dx\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}y^2 f_{Y|X}(y|x)dx$
$= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}E(Y^2|X = x)dx = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{x^2 + 1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}dx = E(X^2 +1) = 2$

故 $Cov(X,Y)=1, D(Y)=2$

所以 $\rho = \frac{Cov(X,Y)}{\sqrt{DX}\sqrt{DY}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ ，故选(D).
