$$
\begin{array}{l} \text {【答案】} a = - 1 \\ \text {【解析】} \lim _ {n \to \infty} \left| \frac {\frac {(n + 1) !}{(n + 1) ^ {n + 1}} e ^ {- (n + 1) x}}{\frac {n !}{n ^ {n}} e ^ {- n x}} \right| = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {(n + 1) e ^ {- x}}{n + 1} \left(\frac {n}{n + 1}\right) ^ {n} = e ^ {- x} \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{\left(1 + \frac {1}{n}\right) ^ {n}} = e ^ {- x} \frac {1}{e} = e ^ {- x - 1} < 1 \end{array}
$$

故 $x > -1$ ， $a = -1$
