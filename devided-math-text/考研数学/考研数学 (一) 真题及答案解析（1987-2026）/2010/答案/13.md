$$
(\alpha_ {1}, \alpha_ {2}, \alpha_ {3}) = \left(\begin{array}{c c c}1&1&2\\2&1&1\\- 1&0&1\\0&2&a\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c}1&1&2\\0&- 1&- 3\\0&1&3\\0&2&a\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c}1&1&2\\0&1&3\\0&0&0\\0&0&a - 6\end{array}\right),
$$

因为由 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 组成的向量组的秩为2，所以 $a = 6$。
