[解答] $\bar{z} = \frac{\iiint\limits_{\Omega} z dv}{\iiint\limits_{\Omega} dv},$

而 $\iiint_{\Omega} z \, d\nu = \int_0^1 z \, dz \iint_{x^2 + y^2 \leq z} dx \, dy = \pi \int_0^1 z^2 \, dz = \frac{\pi}{3}$,

$\iiint_{\Omega} dv = \int_{0}^{1} dz \iint_{x^2 + y^2 - zz} dx dy = \pi \int_{0}^{1} z dz = \frac{\pi}{2}$，所以 $\bar{z} = \frac{2}{3}$。
