[解答] $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{n}{(n+i)(n^2+j^2)} = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n+i}\cdot\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{n}{n^2+j^2},$

因为 $\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{n+i} = \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{1+\frac{i}{n}} = \int_0^1\frac{1}{1+x}dx,$

$\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{n}{n^2+j^2} = \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{1}{1+(\frac{j}{n})^2} = \int_0^1\frac{1}{1+x^2}dx,$

所以 $\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{n}{(n+i)(n^2+j^2)} = \int_0^1\frac{1}{1+x}dx\cdot\int_0^1\frac{1}{1+x^2}dx = \int_0^1dx\int_0^1\frac{1}{(1+x)(1+y^2)}dy,$

选(D)。
