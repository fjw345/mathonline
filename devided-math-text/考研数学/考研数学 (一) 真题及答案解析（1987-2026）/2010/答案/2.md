[解答] $F(\frac{y}{x},\frac{z}{x}) = 0$ 两边对 $x$ 求偏导，得 $-\frac{y}{x^2}F_1' + \frac{x\overline{\partial x} - z}{x^2}F_2' = 0$，解得

$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{1}{F_2'}(yF_1' + zF_2')$;

$F(\frac{y}{x},\frac{z}{x}) = 0$ 两边对 $y$ 求偏导，得 $\frac{1}{x}F_1' + \frac{1}{x}F_2'\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0$，解得

$\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -\frac{F_1'}{F_2'}$，于是 $x\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + y\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{1}{F_2'}(yF_1' + zF_2') - \frac{yF_1'}{F_2'} = z$，选 (B)。
