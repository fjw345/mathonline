[解答]

$N_{1}$ 的可能取值为 $0,1,2,\dots ,n$ ，且 $P(N_{1} = i) = C_{n}^{i}\theta^{n - i}(1 - \theta)^{i}$ （ $i = 0,1,2,\dots ,n)$

$$
E N _ {1} = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {n} i \times C _ {n} ^ {i} \theta^ {n - i} (1 - \theta) ^ {i} = C _ {n} ^ {1} (1 - \theta) \theta^ {n - 1} + 2 C _ {n} ^ {1} (1 - \theta) ^ {2} \theta^ {n - 2} + \dots + n C _ {n} ^ {n} (1 - \theta) ^ {n}
$$

$$
= (1 - \theta) [ C _ {n} ^ {1} \theta^ {n - 1} + 2 C _ {n} ^ {1} (1 - \theta) \theta^ {n - 2} + \dots + n C _ {n} ^ {n} (1 - \theta) ^ {n - 1} ],
$$

因为 $n(a + b)^{n - 1} = na^{n - 1} + C_n^1 a^{n - 2}b + 2C_n^2 a^{n - 3}b^2 +\dots +nC_{n - 1}^{-1}b^{n - 1},$

所以 $EN_{1} = \sum_{i=0}^{n} i \times C_{n}^{i} \theta^{n-i} (1 - \theta)^{i} = (1 - \theta) \times n (1 - \theta + \theta)^{n-1} = n (1 - \theta)$,

$N_{2}$ 的可能取值为 $0,1,2,\dots ,n$ ，且 $P(N_{2} = i) = C_{n}^{i}(\theta -\theta^{2})^{n - i}(1 - \theta +\theta^{2})^{i}(i = 0,1,2,\dots ,n),$

$$
E N _ {2} = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {n} i \times C _ {n} ^ {i} \theta^ {n - i} (1 - \theta) ^ {i} = n (\theta - \theta^ {2}),
$$

同理 $EN_{3} = \sum_{i=0}^{n} i \times C_{n}^{i}(1 - \theta^{2})^{n-i} (\theta^{2})^{i} = n\theta^{2},$

因为 $T = \sum_{i=1}^{3} a_i N_i$ 为 $\theta$ 为无偏估计量，所以 $ET = \theta$ ，即

$$
E T = a _ {1} E N _ {1} + a _ {2} E N _ {2} + a _ {3} E N _ {3} = a _ {1} n (1 - \theta) + a _ {2} n (\theta - \theta^ {2}) + a _ {3} n \theta^ {2} = \theta ,
$$

或 $na_1 + (na_2 - na_1)\theta +(na_3 - na_2)\theta^2 = \theta$ ，解得

$a_1 = 0, a_2 = \frac{1}{n}, a_3 = \frac{1}{n}$.
