【解析】原式 $= \int \arctan \sqrt{e^x - 1} d\left(\frac{1}{2} e^{2x}\right) = \frac{1}{2} e^{2x}\arctan \sqrt{e^x - 1} -\frac{1}{4}\int \frac{e^{2x}}{\sqrt{e^x - 1}} dx$ 再用整体代换去根号:

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int \frac {e ^ {2 x}}{\sqrt {e ^ {x} - 1}} d x t = \sqrt {e ^ {x} - 1} \int \frac {\left(t ^ {2} + 1\right) ^ {2}}{t} \frac {2 t}{\left(t ^ {2} + 1\right)} d t \\ = \frac {2}{3} t ^ {3} + 2 t + C = \frac {2}{3} \left(e ^ {x} - 1\right) ^ {\frac {3}{2}} + 2 \sqrt {e ^ {x} - 1} + C \end{array}
$$
