【解析】（I） $y=e^{-x}\left(\int xe^{x}dx+C\right)=Ce^{-x}+x-1$
微分方程解函数为 $y(x)=e^{-x}\left(\int_{0}^{x}f(t)e^{t}dt+C\right)$
则 $y(x+T)=e^{-x-T}\left(\int_{0}^{x+T}f(t)e^{t}dt+C\right)^{t-T=u}=e^{-x-T}\left(\int_{-T}^{x}f(u+T)e^{u+T}du+C\right)$
$=e^{-x}\left(\int_{-T}^{x}f(u)e^{u}du+Ce^{-T}\right)=e^{-x}\left(\int_{-T}^{0}f(u)e^{u}du+\int_{0}^{x}f(u)e^{u}du+Ce^{-T}\right)$
(Ⅱ)
若 $e^{-x}\left(\int_{-T}^{0}f(u)e^{u}du+\int_{0}^{x}f(u)e^{u}du+Ce^{-T}\right)=e^{-x}\left(\int_{0}^{x}f(t)e^{t}dt+C\right)$
即 $C=\frac{1}{1-e^{-T}}\int_{-T}^{0}f(u)e^{u}du$ 时 $y(x+T)=y(x)$
由于 $C=\frac{1}{1-e^{-T}}\int_{-T}^{0}f(u)e^{u}du$ 为确定常数，故符合条件的周期解 $y(x)$ 唯一.
