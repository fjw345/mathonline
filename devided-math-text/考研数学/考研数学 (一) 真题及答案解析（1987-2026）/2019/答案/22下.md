Z的取值为 $0,\pm1,\pm2,\cdots,$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & P \left\{Z = 0 \right\} = P \left\{X = - 1, Y = 0 \right\} + P \left\{X = 1, Y = 0 \right\} = \frac {1}{2} P \left\{Y = 0 \right\} + \frac {1}{2} P \left\{Y = 0 \right\} = e ^ {- \lambda} \\ & P \left\{Z = k \right\} = P \left\{X = 1, Y = k \right\} = \frac {1}{2} P \left\{Y = k \right\} = \frac {1}{2} \frac {\lambda^ {k} e ^ {- \lambda}}{k !} \\ & P \left\{Z = - k \right\} = P \left\{X = - 1, Y = k \right\} = \frac {1}{2} P \left\{Y = k \right\} = \frac {1}{2} \frac {\lambda^ {k} e ^ {- \lambda}}{k !}, \text {其中} k = 1, 2, \dots . \end{array}\tag{II}
$$
