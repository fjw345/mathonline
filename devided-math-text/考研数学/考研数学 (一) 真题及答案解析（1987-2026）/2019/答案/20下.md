方法二:（配方法）由于

$f(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}) = 2(x_{1}^{2} - x_{1}x_{2} + 3x_{1}x_{3})^{2} + 2x_{2}^{2} + 6x_{3}^{2} = 2(x_{1} - \frac{1}{2} x_{2} + \frac{3}{2} x_{3})^{2} + \frac{3}{2}(x_{2} + x_{3})^{2}.$ 令 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}z_{1} = \sqrt{2}(x_{1} - \frac{1}{2} x_{2} + \frac{3}{2} x_{3})\\ z_{2} = \sqrt{\frac{3}{2}} (x_{2} + x_{3})\\ z_{3} = x_{3} \end{array} \right.$ ，得规范形为 $f(z_{1},z_{2},z_{3}) = z_{1}^{2} + z_{2}^{2}.$
