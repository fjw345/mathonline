【答案】 $\frac{1}{4}$ 【解析】 $P\{AC|AB\cup C\} = \frac{P\{AC(AB\cup C)\}}{P(AB\cup C)} = \frac{P(AC)}{P(AB) + P(C) - P(ABC)} = \frac{1}{4}$ $\frac{P(A)P(C)}{P(A)P(B) + P(C) - P(ABC)} = \frac{1}{4}\Rightarrow \frac{\frac{1}{2}P(C)}{\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2} + P(C) - 0} = \frac{1}{4}\Rightarrow P(C) = \frac{1}{4}.$
