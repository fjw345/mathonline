【解析】选项(D)←
 $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\left[f_{1}(x)F_{2}(x)+f_{2}(x)F_{1}(x)\right]dx=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\left[F_{2}(x)dF_{1}(x)+F_{1}(x)dF_{2}(x)\right]$
 $=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}d\left[F_{1}(x)F_{2}(x)\right]=F_{1}(x)F_{2}(x)|_{-\infty}^{+\infty}=1.$
所以 $f_{1}F_{2}(x)+f_{2}F_{1}(x)$ 为概率密度. ←
