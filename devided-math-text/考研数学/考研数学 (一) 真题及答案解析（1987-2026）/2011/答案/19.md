$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {解析】} I & = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} y f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) d y = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} y d f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) \\ & = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \left[ y f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) | _ {0} ^ {1} - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d y \right] \\ & = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \left(f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, 1) - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d y\right). \end{array}
$$

因为 $f(x,1)=0$ ，所以 $f_{x}^{\prime}(x,1)=0$ 。

$$
\begin{array}{r l} I & = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d x \int_ {0} ^ {1} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d y = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d y \int_ {0} ^ {1} x f _ {x} ^ {\prime} (x, y) d x \\ & = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d y \left[ x f (x, y) \big | _ {0} ^ {1} - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f (x, y) d x \right] = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} d y \left[ f (1, y) - \int_ {0} ^ {1} f (x, y) d x \right] \\ & = \iint_ {D} f d x d y = a. \end{array} \tag {$\leftrightarrow$}
$$
