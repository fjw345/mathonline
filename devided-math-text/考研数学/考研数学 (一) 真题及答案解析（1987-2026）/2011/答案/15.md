【解析】 $\lim_{x\to 0}\left[\frac{\ln(1 + x)}{x}\right]^{\frac{1}{e^x - 1}} = e^{\lim_{x\to 0}\left[\frac{\ln(1 + x)}{x} - 1\right]\frac{1}{e^x - 1}\Leftarrow$ $= e^{\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\ln(1 + x) - x}{x^2}} = e^{\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x - \frac{1}{2}x^2 + o(x^2) - x}{x^2}}\Leftarrow$ $= e^{\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-\frac{1}{2}x^2 + o(x^2)}{x^2}} = e^{-\frac{1}{2}}.\Leftarrow$
