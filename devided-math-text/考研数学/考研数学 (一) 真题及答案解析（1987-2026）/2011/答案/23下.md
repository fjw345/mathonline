(II) 方法1: ←

$X_{i}\sim N(\mu_{0},\sigma^{2})$ ，令 $Y_{i} = X_{i} - \mu_{0}\sim N(0,\sigma^{2})$ ，则 $\hat{\sigma^2} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}Y_i^2.$

$$
E \left(\stackrel {\wedge} {\sigma^ {2}}\right) = E \left(\frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} Y _ {i} ^ {2}\right) = E \left(Y _ {i} ^ {2}\right) = D \left(Y _ {i}\right) + \left[ E \left(Y _ {i}\right) \right] ^ {2} = \sigma^ {2}. \quad \leftrightarrow
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c} D (\stackrel {\wedge} {\sigma^ {2}}) = D (\frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} Y _ {i} ^ {2}) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} D (Y _ {1} ^ {2} + Y _ {2} ^ {2} + \dots + Y _ {n} ^ {2}) = \frac {1}{n} D (Y _ {l} ^ {2}) \\ = \frac {1}{n} \{E (Y _ {i} ^ {4}) - [ E (Y _ {i} ^ {2}) ] ^ {2} \} = \frac {1}{n} (3 \sigma^ {4} - \sigma^ {4}) = \frac {2 \sigma^ {4}}{n}. \end{array} \leftarrow
$$

方法2:

$X_{i}\sim N(\mu_{0},\sigma^{2})$ ，则 $\frac{X_i - \mu_0}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1)$ ，得到 $Y = \sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(\frac{X_i - \mu_0}{\sigma}\right)^2\sim \chi^2 (n)$ ，即$\sigma^2 Y = \sum_{i = 1}^{n}\bigl (X_i - \mu_0\bigr)^2.$ $\leftrightarrow$ $E\left(\stackrel {\wedge}{\sigma^2}\right) = \frac{1}{n} E\left[\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(X_i - \mu_0)^2\right] = \frac{1}{n} E\left(\sigma^2 Y\right) = \frac{1}{n}\sigma^2 E(Y) = \frac{1}{n}\sigma^2\cdot n = \sigma^2.$ $\leftrightarrow$ $D\left(\stackrel {\wedge}{\sigma^2}\right) = \frac{1}{n^2} D\left[\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(X_i - \mu_0)^2\right] = \frac{1}{n^2} D\left(\sigma^2 Y\right) = \frac{1}{n^2}\sigma^4 D(Y) = \frac{1}{n^2}\sigma^4\cdot 2n = \frac{2}{n}\sigma^4.$
