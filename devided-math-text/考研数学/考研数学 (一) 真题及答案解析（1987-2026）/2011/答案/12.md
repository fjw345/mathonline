(12)【答案】 $\pi$ .

【解析】取 $S: x + y - z = 0, x^2 + y^2 \leq 1$，取上侧，则由斯托克斯公式得，$\Leftrightarrow$

$$
\text {原式} = \iint_ {S} \left| \begin{array}{c c c} d y d z & d z d x & d x d y \\ \frac {\partial}{\partial x} & \frac {\partial}{\partial y} & \frac {\partial}{\partial z} \\ x z & x & \frac {y ^ {2}}{2} \end{array} \right| = \iint_ {S} y d y d z + x d z d x + d x d y.\quad \leftrightarrow
$$

因 $z = x + y, z_{x}^{i} = 1, z_{y}^{i} = 1.$ 由转换投影法得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \iint_ {S} y d y d z + x d z d x + d x d y & = \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1} [ y \cdot (- 1) + x (- 1) + 1 ] d x d y \\ & = \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1} (- x - y + 1) d x d y = \pi \leftarrow \\ & = \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1} d x d y = \pi . \end{array}
$$
