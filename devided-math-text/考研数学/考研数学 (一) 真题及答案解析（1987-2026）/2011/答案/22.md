【解析】(I)因为 $P\{X^2 = Y^2\} = 1$ ，所以 $P\{X^2\neq Y^2\} = 1 - P\{X^2 = Y^2\} = 0.$

即 $P\{X = 0,Y = -1\} = P\{X = 0,Y = 1\} = P\{X = 1,Y = 0\} = 0.$

利用边缘概率和联合概率的关系得到。

$$
P \{X = 0, Y = 0 \} = P \{X = 0 \} - P \{X = 0, Y = - 1 \} - P \{X = 0, Y = 1 \} = \frac {1}{3}; \quad \leftarrow
$$

$$
P \{X = 1, Y = - 1 \} = P \{Y = - 1 \} - P \{X = 0, Y = - 1 \} = \frac {1}{3};
$$

$$
P \{X = 1, Y = 1 \} = P \{Y = 1 \} - P \{X = 0, Y = 1 \} = \frac {1}{3}. \leftarrow
$$

即 $(X,Y)$ 的概率分布为 $\Leftrightarrow$

| $\leftarrow$ | $Y$X \leftarrow$ | -1$\leftarrow$ | 0$\leftarrow$ | 1$\leftarrow$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\leftarrow$ | 0$\leftarrow$ | 0$\leftarrow$ | 1/3$\leftarrow$ | 0$\leftarrow$ |
| $\leftarrow$ | 1$\leftarrow$ | 1/3$\leftarrow$ | 0$\leftarrow$ | 1/3$\leftarrow$ |
