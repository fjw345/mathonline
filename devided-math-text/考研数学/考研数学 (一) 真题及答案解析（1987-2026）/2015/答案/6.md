【解析】由 $x = Py$ ，故 $f = x^{T}Ax = y^{T}(P^{T}AP)y = 2y_{1}^{2} + y_{2}^{2} - y_{3}^{2}.$ 且 $P^T AP = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}.$

由已知可得: $Q = P \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = PC$

故有 $Q^T AQ = C^T (P^T AP)C = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & -1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\leftrightarrow$

所以 $f = x^{T}Ax = y^{T}(Q^{T}AQ)y = 2y_{1}^{2} - y_{2}^{2} + y_{3}^{2}$ 选（A）
