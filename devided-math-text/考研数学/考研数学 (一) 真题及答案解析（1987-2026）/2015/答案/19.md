【答案】 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\pi$
【解析】由题意假设参数方程 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = \cos \theta \\ y = \sqrt{2}\sin \theta ,\theta :\frac{\pi}{2}\rightarrow -\frac{\pi}{2}\\ z = \cos \theta \end{array} \right.$
$\int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{-\frac{\pi}{2}}[-(\sqrt{2}\sin \theta +\cos \theta)\sin \theta +2\sin \theta \cos \theta +(1 + \sin^2\theta)\sin \theta ]\mathrm{d}\theta .$
$= \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{-\frac{\pi}{2}} - \sqrt{2}\sin^2\theta +\sin \theta \cos \theta +(1 + \sin^2\theta)\sin \theta \mathrm{d}\theta .$
$= 2\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{2}\theta \mathrm{d}\theta = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\pi .$
