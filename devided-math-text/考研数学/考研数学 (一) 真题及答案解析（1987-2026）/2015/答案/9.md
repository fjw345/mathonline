$$
- \frac {1}{2} \leftarrow
$$

【分析】此题考查 $\frac{0}{0}$ 型未定式极限，可直接用洛必达法则，也可以用等价无穷小替换.
【解析】方法一: $\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\ln(\cos x)}{x^2} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\frac{-\sin x}{\cos x}}{2x} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-\tan x}{2x} = -\frac{1}{2}.$
方法二: $\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\ln(\cos x)}{x^2} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\ln(1 + \cos x - 1)}{x^2} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\cos x - 1}{x^2} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-\frac{1}{2}x^2}{x^2} = -\frac{1}{2}.$
