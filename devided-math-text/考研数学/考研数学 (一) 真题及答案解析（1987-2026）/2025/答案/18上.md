令$u = \frac {x}{y}$, 则$\frac {\partial g}{\partial x} = f ^ {\prime} (u) \frac {1}{y}, \frac {\partial g}{\partial y} = f ^ {\prime} (u) \left(- \frac {x}{y ^ {2}}\right)$

又$g (x, x) = f \left(\frac {x}{x}\right) = f (1) = 1, \left. \frac {\partial g}{\partial x} \right| _ {(x, x)} = f ^ {\prime} (1) \frac {1}{x} = \frac {2}{x}$, 故$f ^ {\prime} (1) = 2$
