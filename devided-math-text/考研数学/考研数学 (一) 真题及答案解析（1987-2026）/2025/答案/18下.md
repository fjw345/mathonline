$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial x ^ {2}} = \left(f ^ {\prime \prime} (u) \frac {1}{y}\right) \frac {1}{y} = f ^ {\prime \prime} (u) \frac {1}{y ^ {2}} \dots .. (1)
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial x \partial y} = f ^ {\prime \prime} (u) \left(- \frac {x}{y ^ {2}}\right) \frac {1}{y} + f ^ {\prime} (u) \left(- \frac {1}{y ^ {2}}\right) = - \frac {x}{y ^ {3}} f ^ {\prime \prime} (u) - \frac {1}{y ^ {2}} f ^ {\prime} (u).. \tag {2}
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial y ^ {2}} = f ^ {\prime \prime} (u) \left(- \frac {x}{y ^ {2}}\right) \frac {x}{y} + f ^ {\prime} (u) \left(\frac {2 x}{y ^ {2}}\right) = \frac {x ^ {2}}{y ^ {4}} f ^ {\prime \prime} (u) + \frac {2 x}{y ^ {3}} f ^ {\prime} (u) \dots (3)
$$

将（1）（2）（3）代入 $x^{2}\frac{\partial^{2}g}{\partial x^{2}} + xy\frac{\partial^{2}g}{\partial x\partial y} + y^{2}\frac{\partial^{2}g}{\partial y^{2}} = 1$ 化简得:
$u^{2}f^{\prime \prime}(u) + uf^{\prime}(u) = 1$ ，即 $f^{\prime \prime}(u) + \frac{1}{u^2} f^{\prime}(u) = \frac{1}{u^2}$ 。
令 $p' = f'(u)$ 则 $p'' + \frac{1}{u} p' = \frac{1}{u^2}$
$p = e^{-\int_{u}^{1 - du}\left[\int \frac{1}{u^2} e^{\int_{u}^{1 - du}du + C}\right]} = \frac{1}{u}\left[\int \frac{1}{u} du + C\right] = \frac{\ln u}{u} +\frac{C}{u}$
又 $p'\big|_{u = 1} = C = 2$ ，故 $p = \frac{\ln u}{u} +\frac{2}{u}$
因此， $\frac{\partial f}{\partial u} = \frac{\ln u}{u} +\frac{2}{u}$ ，积分得 $f(u) = \int \left(\frac{\ln u}{u} +\frac{2}{u}\right)du = \frac{1}{2}\ln^2 u + 2\ln u + C$
又 $f(1) = C = 1$ ，故 $f(u) = \frac{1}{2}\ln^2 u + 2\ln u + 1$ 。
