已知函数 $f(u)$ 在区间 $(0, +\infty)$ 内具有2阶导数，记 $g(x,y) = f\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)$，若 $g(x,y)$ 满足$x^{2}\frac{\partial^{2}g}{\partial x^{2}} + xy\frac{\partial^{2}g}{\partial x\partial y} + y^{2}\frac{\partial^{2}g}{\partial y^{2}} = 1,$ 且 $g(x,y) = 1,\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}\big|_{(x,x)} = \frac{2}{x}$，求 $f(u)$ 解:令 $u = \frac{x}{y}$，则 $\frac{\partial g}{\partial x} = f'(u)\frac{1}{y},\frac{\partial g}{\partial y} = f'(u)\left(-\frac{x}{y^2}\right)$ 又 $g(x,x) = f\left(\frac{x}{x}\right) = f(1) = 1,\left.\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}\right|_{(x,x)} = f'(1)\frac{1}{x} = \frac{2}{x}$，故 $f'(1) = 2$
