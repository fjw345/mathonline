(A) $\left\{(X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n}) \mid \overline{X} > 1 + \frac{2}{n} Z_{\alpha}\right\}$ .
(B) $\left\{(X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n}) \mid \overline{X} > 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{n} Z_{\alpha}\right\}$ .
(C) $\left\{(X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n}) \mid \overline{X} > 1 + \frac{2}{\sqrt{n}} Z_{\alpha}\right\}$ .
(D) $\left\{(X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n}) \mid \overline{X} > 1 + \sqrt{\frac{2}{n}} Z_{\alpha}\right\}$ .
