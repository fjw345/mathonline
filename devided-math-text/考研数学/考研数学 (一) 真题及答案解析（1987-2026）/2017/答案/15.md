【答案】 $\frac{dy}{dx}\bigg|_{x = 0} = f_1'(1,1),\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\bigg|_{x = 0} = f_{11}''(1,1),$

【解析】

$$
\begin{array}{l} y = f (e ^ {x}, \cos x) \stackrel {x = 0} {\Rightarrow} y (0) = f (1, 1) \\ \Rightarrow \frac {d y}{d x} \bigg | _ {x = 0} = \left(f _ {1} ^ {\prime} e ^ {x} + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (- \sin x)\right) \bigg | _ {x = 0} = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) \cdot 1 + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) \cdot 0 = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) \\ \Rightarrow \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} = f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} e ^ {2 x} + f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} e ^ {x} (- \sin x) + f _ {2 1} ^ {\prime \prime} e ^ {x} (- \sin x) + f _ {2 2} ^ {\prime} \sin^ {2} x + f _ {1} ^ {\prime} e ^ {x} - f _ {2} ^ {\prime} \cos x \\ \Rightarrow \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} \bigg | _ {x = 0} = f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) + f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) - f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) \end{array}
$$

结论:

$$
\left. \frac {d y}{d x} \right| _ {x = 0} = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) \left. \frac {d ^ {2} y}{d x ^ {2}} \right| _ {x = 0} = f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) + f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (1, 1) - f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (1, 1)
$$
