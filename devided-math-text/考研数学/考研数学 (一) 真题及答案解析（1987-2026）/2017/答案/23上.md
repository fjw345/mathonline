【答案】

$(I)f_{Z_i}(z) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{z^2}{2\sigma^2}}, & z > 0,\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$

(Ⅱ)矩估计 $\hat{\sigma} = \frac{1}{n}\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}|X_i - \mu |;$

(III)最大似然估计: $\hat{\sigma} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}(X_i - \mu)^2}$

【解析】(1) $F_{z}(z)=P(Z_{i}\leq z)\Rightarrow P(|X_{i}-\mu|\leq z)^{n-1}$

当 $z < 0, F_{z_i}(z) = 0 \in$

当 $z \geq 0, F_{z_i}(z) = P(-z \leq X_i - \mu \leq z) = P(\mu - z \leq X_i \leq \mu + z) = F_X(\mu + z) - F(\mu - z)$

当 $z \geq 0$ 时，$\leftarrow$

$$
\therefore f _ {z _ {i}} (z) = \left(F _ {z _ {i}} (z)\right) ^ {\prime} = f _ {x} (\mu + z) + f _ {x} (\mu - z) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi} \sigma} e ^ {- \frac {z ^ {2}}{2 \sigma^ {2}}} + \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi} \sigma} e ^ {- \frac {z ^ {2}}{2 \sigma^ {2}}} = \frac {2}{\sqrt {2 \pi} \sigma} e ^ {- \frac {z ^ {2}}{2 \sigma^ {2}}} \Leftrightarrow
$$

综上 $f_{z_i}(z) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l}\frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{z^2}{2\sigma^2}}, z > 0 \\ 0, z \leq 0 \end{array} \right.$

$(\Pi)E(Z_i) = \int_0^{+\infty}z\frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{z^2}{2\sigma^2}}dz = \int_0^{+\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} e^{-\frac{z^2}{2\sigma^2}}dz^2$

$$
= \frac {- 2 \sigma^ {2}}{\sqrt {2 \pi} \sigma} \Big | _ {0} ^ {+ \infty} e ^ {- \frac {z ^ {2}}{2 \sigma^ {2}}} d (- \frac {z ^ {2}}{2 \sigma^ {2}}) = \frac {2 \sigma}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} = \sqrt {\frac {2}{\pi}} \sigma
$$

$$
\text {令} E \left(Z _ {i}\right) = \bar {Z} \quad \bar {Z} = \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} Z _ {i} = \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left| X _ {i} - \mu \right| ^ {\infty}
$$

由此可得 $\sigma$ 的矩估计量 $\hat{\sigma} = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{n}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}|X_i - \mu |$

对总体 X 的 n 个样本 $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n}$ ，则相交的绝对误差的样本 $Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \cdots, Z_{n}, Z_{i} = |x_{i} - u|, i = 1, 2, \ldots, n$ ，令其样本值为 $Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \cdots, Z_{n}, Z_{i} = |x_{i} - u|$

则对应的似然函数 $L(\sigma)=\left\{\begin{aligned}\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}\right)^{n}e^{-\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}}, & Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \cdots Z_{n}>0 \\ 0, & \text{其他}\end{aligned}\right.$
