两边取对数，当 $Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \cdots, Z_{n} > 0$ 时 $^{4}$

$$
\ln L (\sigma) = n \ln \frac {2}{\sqrt {2 \pi} \sigma} - \frac {1}{2 \sigma^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} Z _ {i} ^ {2} \leftrightarrow
$$

令$\frac {d \ln L (\sigma)}{d \sigma} = - \frac {n}{u} + \frac {1}{\sigma^ {3}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} Z _ {i} ^ {2} = 0$

所以， $\bar{\sigma}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}Z_{i}^{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_{i}-u)^{2}}$ 为所求的最大似然估计。
