【解析】设 $C=\begin{pmatrix}x_{1}&x_{2}\\x_{3}&x_{4}\end{pmatrix}$ ，由于AC-CA=B，故
 $\begin{pmatrix}1&a\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}x_{1}&x_{2}\\x_{3}&x_{4}\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}x_{1}&x_{2}\\x_{3}&x_{4}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&a\\1&0\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&b\end{pmatrix},$
即 $\left(\begin{aligned}x_{1}+ax_{3}&x_{2}+ax_{4}\\ x_{1}&x_{2}\end{aligned}\right)-\left(\begin{aligned}x_{1}+x_{2}&ax_{1}\\ x_{3}+x_{4}&ax_{3}\end{aligned}\right)=\left(\begin{aligned}0&1\\ 1&b\end{aligned}\right).$
