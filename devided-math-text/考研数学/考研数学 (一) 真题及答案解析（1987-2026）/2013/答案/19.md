【解析】

(I) $\overrightarrow{AB} = (-1,1,1)$，所以直线L方程 $\frac{x - 1}{-1} = \frac{y}{1} = \frac{z}{1}$

设 $\Sigma$ 上任一点 $y$ 由直线L上的点 $F(y)$ 绕 $z$ 轴旋转一周得到，则$\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x^{2} + y^{2} = x_{0}^{2} + y_{0}^{2}\\ z = z_{0} \end{array} \right.$ 又 $\frac{x_0 - 1}{-1} = \frac{y_0}{1} = \frac{z_0}{1}$ ，所以 $\Sigma$ 方程为 $x^{2} + y^{2} = (1 - z)^{2} + z^{2} = 2z^{2} - 2z + 1$ (II) $x^{2} + y^{2} - 2(z - \frac{1}{2})^{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 设形心坐标 $(\overline{x},\overline{y},\overline{z})$ ，几何体关于 $xoz,yoz$ 对称， $\overline{x} = \overline{y} = 0$

$$
\overline {{z}} = \frac {\iiint_ {\Omega} z d v}{\iiint_ {\Omega} d v} = \frac {\int_ {0} ^ {2} z d z \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 2 z ^ {2} - 2 z + 1} d x d y}{\int_ {0} ^ {2} d z \iint_ {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 2 z ^ {2} - 2 z + 1} d x d y} = \frac {\pi \int_ {0} ^ {2} (2 z ^ {3} - z ^ {2} + z) d z}{\pi \int_ {0} ^ {2} (2 z ^ {2} - 2 z + 1) d z} = \frac {7}{5}.
$$
