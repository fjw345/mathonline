【答案】 $\sqrt{2}$

【解析】 $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{dt}\cdot \frac{1}{\frac{dx}{dt}} = \frac{\sin t + t\cos t - \sin t}{\cos t} = t,$ $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{d\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)}{dt}\cdot \frac{dt}{dx} = \frac{1}{\frac{dx}{dt}} = \frac{1}{\cos t},\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\bigg|_{t = \frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{1}{\cos\frac{\pi}{4}} = \sqrt{2}$
