【答案】 $1 - \frac{1}{e}$

【解析】 $f(y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}e^{-y}, & y > 0,\\ 0, & y\leq 0, \end{array} \right.$

$$
P \Big \{Y \leq a + 1 \big | Y > a \Big \} = \frac {P \big \{Y > a , Y \leq a + 1 \big \}}{P \big \{Y > a \big \}} = \frac {\int_ {a} ^ {a + 1} f (y) d y}{\int_ {a} ^ {+ \infty} f (y) d y} = \frac {e ^ {- a} - e ^ {- (a + 1)}}{e ^ {- a}} = 1 - \frac {1}{e}
$$
