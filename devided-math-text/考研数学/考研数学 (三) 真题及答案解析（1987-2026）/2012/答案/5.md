【解析】

$$
\left| \alpha_ {1}, \alpha_ {3}, \alpha_ {4} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & 1 & - 1 \\ 0 & - 1 & 1 \\ c _ {1} & c _ {3} & c _ {4} \end{array} \right| = c _ {1} \times \left| \begin{array}{c c} 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & 1 \end{array} \right| = 0,
$$

故 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{3},\alpha_{4}$ 必定线性相关，从而应选C.
