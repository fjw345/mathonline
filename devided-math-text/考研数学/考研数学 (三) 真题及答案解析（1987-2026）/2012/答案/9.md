【答案】 $e^{-\sqrt{2}}$

$$
\lim _ {x \to \frac {\pi}{4}} (\tan x) ^ {\frac {1}{\cos x - \sin x}} = \lim _ {x \to \frac {\pi}{4}} e ^ {\frac {\ln \tan x}{\cos x - \sin x}} = \lim _ {x \to \frac {\pi}{4}} e ^ {\frac {\tan x - 1}{\cos x - \sin x}} = \lim _ {x \to \frac {\pi}{4}} e ^ {\frac {\frac {\sin x - \cos x}{\cos x}}{\cos x - \sin x}} = \lim _ {x \to \frac {\pi}{4}} e ^ {- \frac {1}{\cos x}} = e ^ {- \sqrt {x}}.
$$
