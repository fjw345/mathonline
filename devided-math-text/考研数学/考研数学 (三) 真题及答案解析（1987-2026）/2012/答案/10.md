$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {【解析】} y & = f [ f (x) ] = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \ln \sqrt {f (x)}, & f (x) \geq 1 \\ 2 f (x) - 1, & f (x) < 1 \end{array} \right. = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \ln \sqrt {\ln \sqrt {x}}, & x \geq e ^ {2} \\ 2 \ln \sqrt {x} - 1, & 1 \leq x < e ^ {2} \\ 2 (2 x - 1) - 1, & x < 1 \end{array} \right. \\ & = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {1}{2} \ln \left(\frac {1}{2} \ln x\right), & x \geq e ^ {2} \\ \ln x - 1, & 1 \leq x < e ^ {2}, \\ 4 x - 3, & x > 1 \end{array} \right. \end{array}
$$

所以$\left. \frac {d y}{d x} \right| _ {x = e} = (\ln x - 1) \Big | _ {x = e} = \frac {1}{x} \Bigg | _ {x = e} = \frac {1}{e}.$
