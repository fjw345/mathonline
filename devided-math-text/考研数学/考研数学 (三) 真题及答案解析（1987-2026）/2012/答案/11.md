【答案】 $2dx - dy$
【解析】由于 $\lim_{\substack{x\to 0\\ y\to 1}}\frac{f(x,y) - 2x + y - 2}{\sqrt{x^2 + (y - 1)^2}} = 0$ ，则 $\lim_{\substack{x\to 0\\ y\to 1}}\left(f(x,y) - 2x + y - 2\right) = 0$
由 $f(x,y)$ 连续，则 $f(0,1) - 0 + 1 - 2 = 0,f(0,1) = 1$
则 $\lim_{\substack{x\to 0\\ y\to 1}}\frac{f(x,y) - f(0,1) - 2x + (y - 1)}{\sqrt{x^2 + (y - 1)^2}} = 0$ ，观察可知 $f(x,y)$ 在(0,1)处可微，且$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\bigg|_{(0,1)} = 2,\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\bigg|_{(0,1)} = -1.$ 故 $dz = 2dx - dy$
