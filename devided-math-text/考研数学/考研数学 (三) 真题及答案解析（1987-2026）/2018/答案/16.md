【解】原式 $= \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} dx \int_{\sqrt{3}x}^{\sqrt{3(1-x^{2})}} x^{2} dy$
$= \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} x^{2} y \left| \sqrt{\frac{3(1-x^{2})}{\sqrt{3}x}} \right. dx$
$= \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} x^{2} \left[ \sqrt{3(1-x^{2})} - \sqrt{3} x \right] dx$
$= \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} x^{2} \sqrt{3(1-x^{2})} dx - \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \sqrt{3} x^{3} dx$
$= I_{1} - I_{2},$
其中 $I_{1} = \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} x^{2} \sqrt{3(1-x^{2})} dx^{x=\sin t} = \sqrt{3} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sin^{2} t \cos^{2} t dt = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{1-\cos 4t}{2} dt = \frac{\sqrt{3}\pi}{32},$
$I_{2} = \int_{0}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \sqrt{3} x^{3} dx = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{16}.$
故原式 $I = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{32} (\pi - 2)$.
