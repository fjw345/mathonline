(9) $\mathrm{e}^{-1}$

解故应填 $\mathrm{e}^{-1}$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left| = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \left(1 - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) ^ {n} \right| \\ = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \left(\frac {n}{n + 1}\right) ^ {n} = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \left[ 1 + \left(- \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) \right] ^ {n} \\ = \mathrm{e} ^ {\lim _ {n \to \infty} n \left(- \frac {1}{n + 1}\right)} = \mathrm{e} ^ {- 1}. \end{array}
$$
