(11) $\frac{1}{18}(1 - 2\sqrt{2})$ 解 由分部积分法:$\int_{0}^{1} x^{2} f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{3} \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \mathrm{d}x^{3} = \frac{1}{3} x^{3} f(x) \bigg|_{0}^{1} - \frac{1}{3} \int_{0}^{1} x^{3} f'(x) \mathrm{d}x$，①又 $f(1) = \int_{1}^{1} \sqrt{1 + t^{4}} \mathrm{d}t = 0, f'(x) = \sqrt{1 + x^{4}}$，代入①式:$\frac{1}{3} f(1) - \frac{1}{3} \int_{0}^{1} x^{3} \sqrt{1 + x^{4}} \mathrm{d}x = -\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{4} \int_{0}^{1} \sqrt{1 + x^{4}} \mathrm{d}x^{4}$ $= -\frac{1}{12} \cdot \frac{2}{3} (1 + x^{4})^{\frac{3}{2}} \bigg|_{0}^{1} = \frac{1}{18}(1 - 2\sqrt{2}).$
