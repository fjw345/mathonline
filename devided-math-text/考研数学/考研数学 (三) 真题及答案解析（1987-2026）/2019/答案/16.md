(16) 解 因为

$$
\frac {\partial g}{\partial x} = y - f _ {u} (x + y, x - y) - f _ {v} (x + y, x - y),
$$

$$
\frac {\partial g}{\partial y} = x - f _ {u} (x + y, x - y) + f _ {v} (x + y, x - y),
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial x ^ {2}} = - f _ {u u} (x + y, x - y) - 2 f _ {u v} (x + y, x - y) - f _ {v v} (x + y, x - y),
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial x \partial y} = 1 - f _ {u u} (x + y, x - y) + f _ {v v} (x + y, x - y),
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial y ^ {2}} = - f _ {u u} (x + y, x - y) + 2 f _ {u v} (x + y, x - y) - f _ {v v} (x + y, x - y),
$$

所以

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial x ^ {2}} + \frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial x \partial y} + \frac {\partial^ {2} g}{\partial y ^ {2}} = 1 - 3 f _ {u u} (x + y, x - y) - f _ {v v} (x + y, x - y).
$$
