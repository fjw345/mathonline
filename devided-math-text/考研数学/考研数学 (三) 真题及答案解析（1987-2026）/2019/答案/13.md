解 由题意得

$$
\overline {{\boldsymbol {A}}} = \left(\begin{array}{c c c c c}1&0&- 1&\vdots&0\\1&1&- 1&\vdots&1\\0&1&a ^ {2} - 1&\vdots&a\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c c}1&0&- 1&\vdots&0\\0&1&0&\vdots&1\\0&1&a ^ {2} - 1&\vdots&a\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c c}1&0&- 1&\vdots&0\\0&1&0&\vdots&1\\0&0&a ^ {2} - 1&\vdots&a - 1\end{array}\right).
$$

要使 Ax=b 有无穷多解，则应使 $\mathrm{r}(\mathbf{A})=\mathrm{r}(\overline{\mathbf{A}})<3$ ，

当 $a^2 - 1 = a - 1 = 0$，即 $a = 1$ 时，$\mathrm{r}(\mathbf{A}) = \mathrm{r}(\overline{\mathbf{A}}) = 2 < 3$.

故应填1.
