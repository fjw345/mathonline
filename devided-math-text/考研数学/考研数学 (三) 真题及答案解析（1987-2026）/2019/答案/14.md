$$
\frac {2}{3}
$$

解 由随机变量 $X$ 的概率密度 $f(x) = \begin{cases} \frac{x}{2}, & 0 < x < 2, \\ 0, & \text{其他}, \end{cases}$ 可知 $X$ 的分布函数 $F(X) = \begin{cases} 0, & x < 0, \\ \frac{x^2}{4}, & 0 \leqslant x < 2, \\ 1, & x \geqslant 2, \end{cases}$, $EX = \int_0^2 x f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^2 x \cdot \frac{x}{2} \mathrm{d}x = \frac{4}{3}$, $P\{F(X) > EX - 1\} = P\left\{F(X) > \frac{4}{3} - 1\right\} = P\left\{\frac{X^2}{4} > \frac{1}{3}\right\}$ $= P\left\{X > \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right\} = \int_{\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}}^2 \frac{x}{2} \mathrm{d}x = \frac{2}{3}$. 故应填 $\frac{2}{3}$.
