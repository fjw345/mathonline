(3)【答案】(B)↓

【考查分析】本题考查直角坐标和极坐标的转换.↓

【详解】在极坐标系下该二重积分要分成两个积分区域

$$
D _ {1} = \left\{(r, \theta) \left| 0 \leq \theta \leq \frac {\pi}{4}, 0 \leq r \leq 2 \sin \theta \right. \right\} D _ {2} = \left\{(r, \theta) \left| \frac {\pi}{4} \leq \theta \leq \frac {\pi}{2}, 0 \leq r \leq 2 \cos \theta \right. \right\} \xleftarrow {}
$$

所以 $\iint_{D} f(x, y) dx dy = \int_{0}^{\frac{x}{4}} d\theta \int_{0}^{2\sin \theta} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) r dr + \int_{\frac{x}{4}}^{\frac{x}{2}} d\theta \int_{0}^{2\cos \theta} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) r dr$，选(B).
