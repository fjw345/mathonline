(23)【答案】(1) $\hat{\theta}=2\overline{X}-1.$

(II) $\hat{\theta}=\min\{X_{1},X_{2},\cdots,X_{n}\}.$

【考查分析】本题考查矩估计和最大似然估计.↓

【详解】(1) $E(X) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xf(x;\theta)dx = \int_{\theta}^{1}x\cdot \frac{1}{1 - \theta} dx = \frac{1 + \theta}{2}$

令 $E(X) = \overline{x}$，即 $\frac{1 + \theta}{2} = \overline{x}$，解得 $\theta = 2\overline{x} - 1.$

$\widetilde{\theta} = 2\overline{X} -1$ 为 $\theta$ 的矩估计量，其中 $\overline{X} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}X_{i};$

(II) 似然函数

$$
L (\theta) = \prod_ {i = 1} ^ {n} f (x _ {i}; \theta) = \left\{ \begin{array}{c} {{\frac {1}{(1 - \theta) ^ {n}}, \theta \leq x _ {i} \leq 1,}} \\ {{0, \text {其他}.}} \end{array} \right.
$$

当 $\theta \leq x_{i} \leq 1$ 时， $L(\theta) = \prod_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{1 - \theta} = \left(\frac{1}{1 - \theta}\right)^{n}$，↓
