(II) $E(Y)=16$ .↓

【考查分析】本题考查离散型随机变量的概率分布和数学期望.

【详解】(1) 记 $p$ 为观测值大于 $3$ 的概率，则 $p = P\{X > 3\} = \int_{3}^{+\infty} 2^{-x} \ln 2dx = \frac{1}{8}$. ∵ $Y$ 的概率分布为↓

$$
P \{Y = n \} = C _ {n - 1} ^ {1} p (1 - p) ^ {n - 2} p = (n - 1) (\frac {1}{8}) ^ {2} (\frac {7}{8}) ^ {n - 2}, \quad n = 2, 3, \dots
$$

$$
E (Y) = \sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} n \cdot P \{Y = n \} = \sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} n \cdot (n - 1) (\frac {1}{8}) ^ {2} (\frac {7}{8}) ^ {n - 2} = (\frac {1}{8}) ^ {2} \sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} n \cdot (n - 1) (\frac {7}{8}) ^ {n - 2}
$$

记 $S(x) = \sum_{n=2}^{\infty} n \cdot (n-1)x^{n-2} - 1 < x < 1$，则↓

$$
S (x) = \sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} n \cdot (n - 1) x ^ {n - 2} = (\sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} n \cdot x ^ {n - 1}) ^ {r} = (\sum_ {n = 2} ^ {\infty} x ^ {n}) ^ {n} = \frac {2}{(1 - x) ^ {3}},
$$

从而 $E(Y) = \frac{1}{8^2} S\left(\frac{7}{8}\right) = 16$
