$$
\left| \frac {1}{2} \right. _ {\downarrow}
$$

【考查分析】本题考查二维正态分布的性质.↓

【详解】由题设知， $X \sim N(1,1), Y \sim N(0,1)$ ，且 $X,Y$ 相互独立，从而↓

$P(XY - Y < 0) = P((X - 1)Y < 0) = P(X - 1 > 0, Y < 0) + P(X - 1 < 0, Y > 0) \downarrow$

$= P\{X > 1\}P\{Y < 0\} + P\{X < 1\}P\{Y > 0\} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$
