(15)【答案】 $a = -1, b = -\frac{1}{2}, k = -\frac{1}{3}$.

【考查分析】本题考查利用等价无穷小的定义求参数。

【详解】方法一:利用泰勒公式.

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {1 = \underset {x \to 0} {\lim} \frac {f (x)}{g (x)} = \underset {x \to 0} {\lim} \frac {x + a \ln (1 + x) + b x \sin x}{k x ^ {3}}} \\ & {= \underset {x \to 0} {\lim} \frac {x + a \left(x - \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} + \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} + o (x ^ {3})\right) + b x \left(x - \frac {x ^ {3}}{6} + o (x ^ {3})\right)}{k x ^ {3}}} \\ & {= \underset {x \to 0} {\lim} \frac {(1 + a) x + \left(b - \frac {a}{2}\right) x ^ {2} + \frac {a}{3} x ^ {3} - \frac {b}{6} x ^ {4} + o (x ^ {3})}{k x ^ {3}}} \\ & {\text {即} 1 + a = 0, b - \frac {a}{2} = 0, \frac {a}{3 k} = 1} \\ & {\therefore a = - 1, b = - \frac {1}{2}, k = - \frac {1}{3}} \end{array}
$$

方法二:利用洛必达法则.

$$
\begin{array}{l} 1 = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f (x)}{g (x)} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {x + a \ln (1 + x) + b x \sin x}{k x ^ {3}} \\ = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {1 + \frac {a}{1 + x} + b \sin x + b x \cos x}{3 k x ^ {2}} \end{array}
$$

因为分母的极限为 $0$ ，则分子的极限为 $0$ ，即 $a = -1$
