(10)【答案】 $2\downarrow$

【考查分析】本题考查变上限积分函数求导.

【详解】因为 $f(x)$ 连续，所以 $\varphi (x)$ 可导，所以 $\varphi^{\prime}(x) = \int_{0}^{x^{2}}f(t)dt + 2x^{2}f(x^{2})$；↓因为 $\varphi (1) = 1$，所以 $\varphi (1) = \int_0^1 f(t)dt = 1\downarrow$

又因为 $\varphi^{\prime}(1) = 5$ ，所以 $\varphi^{\prime}(1) = \int_{0}^{1}f(t)dt + 2f(1) = 5\downarrow$ 故 $f(1) = 2\leftarrow$
