(16)【答案】

$$
\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {2}{5} \downarrow
$$

【考查分析】本题考查利用简化性质计算二重积分.

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\text {【详解】} \iint_ {D} x (x + y) d x d y = \iint_ {D} x ^ {2} d x d y} \\ & {= 2 \int_ {0} ^ {1} d x \int_ {x ^ {2}} ^ {\sqrt {2 - x ^ {2}}} x ^ {2} d y} \\ & {= 2 \int_ {0} ^ {1} x ^ {2} (\sqrt {2 - x ^ {2}} - x ^ {2}) d x} \\ & {= 2 \int_ {0} ^ {1} x ^ {2} \sqrt {2 - x ^ {2}} d x - \frac {2}{5} \stackrel {x - \sqrt {2} \sin t} {=} 2 \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{4}} 2 \sin^ {2} t 2 \cos^ {2} t d t - \frac {2}{5} \downarrow} \\ & {= 2 \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{4}} \sin^ {2} 2 t d t - \frac {2}{5} = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \sin^ {2} u d u - \frac {2}{5} = \frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {2}{5}.} \end{array}
$$
