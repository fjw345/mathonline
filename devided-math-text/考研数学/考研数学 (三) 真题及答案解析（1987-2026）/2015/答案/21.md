【考查分析】本题考查相似矩阵和矩阵的相似对角化.

【详解】(Ⅰ) $A \sim B$ 则 $tr(A) = tr(B)$ 即 $3 + a = 1 + b + 1$.↓

$\left|A\right| = \left|B\right|$ 即 $2a - 3 = b$ ↓

整理得到 $\begin{cases} a - b = -1 \\ 2a - b = 3 \end{cases} \Rightarrow \begin{cases} a = 4 \\ b = 5 \end{cases}$

$A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 & -3 \\ -1 & 3 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 & -3 \\ -1 & 2 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} = E + C$

(Ⅱ)

$C = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 2 & -3 \\ -1 & 2 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} (1 - 2 - 3)$

$C$ 的特征值 $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = 0, \lambda_3 = 4$.↓

当 $\lambda = 0$ 时，$(0E - C)x = 0$ 的基础解系为

$\xi_1 = (2,1,0)^T; \xi_2 = (-3,0,1)^T$ ↓

当 $\lambda = 5$ 时，$(4E - C)x = 0$ 的基础解系为 $\xi_3 = (-1, -1,1)^T$ ↓

$A = E + C$ ，则 $A$ 的特征值为 $1,1,5$.↓

$P = (\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3) = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -3 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$

令 则↓

$P^{-1}AP = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & & \\ & 1 & \\ & & 5 \end{pmatrix}$.
