【答案】C

【解析】因为 $X$，$Y$ 是二维正态分布，所以 $\overline{X}$ 与 $\overline{Y}$ 也服从二维正态分布，则 $\overline{X} - \overline{Y}$ 也服从二维正态分布，即 $E(\hat{\theta}) = E(\overline{X} - \overline{Y}) = E(\overline{X}) - E(\overline{Y}) = \mu_1 - \mu_2 = \theta$，$D(\hat{\theta}) = D(\overline{X} - \overline{Y}) = D(\overline{X}) + D(\overline{Y}) - cov(\overline{X}, \overline{Y}) = \frac{\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 - 2\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2}{n}$，故选 C.
