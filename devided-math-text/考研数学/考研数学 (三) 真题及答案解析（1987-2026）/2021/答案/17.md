【答案】 $\alpha = \frac{1}{\pi}\left(\frac{1}{e} -e\right)$

【解析】要想极限存在，则左右极限相等.

又由于 $\lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\left[\alpha \arctan{\frac{1}{x}} + (1 + |x|)^{\frac{1}{x}}\right] = \frac{\pi}{2}\alpha + e.$ $\lim_{x\to 0^{-}}\left[\alpha \arctan{\frac{1}{x}} + (1 + |x|)^{\frac{1}{x}}\right] = -\frac{\pi}{2}\alpha + \frac{1}{e}.$ 从而 $\frac{\pi}{2}\alpha + e = -\frac{\pi}{2}\alpha + \frac{1}{e}$，即 $\alpha = \frac{1}{\pi} (\frac{1}{e} - e)$.
