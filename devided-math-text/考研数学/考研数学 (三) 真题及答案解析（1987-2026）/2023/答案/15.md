解析:

由 $\begin{vmatrix} a & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & a & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & a \end{vmatrix} = 4 \neq 0$，和方程组有解知:

$r\left( \begin{array}{ccc}a & 0 & 1\\ 1 & a & 1\\ 1 & 2 & a\\ a & b & 0 \end{array} \right) = r\left( \begin{array}{cccc}a & 0 & 1 & 1\\ 1 & a & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 & a & 0\\ a & b & 0 & 2 \end{array} \right) = 3$ ，从而 $\left| \begin{array}{ccc}a & 0 & 1 & 1\\ 1 & a & 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 & a & 0\\ a & b & 0 & 2 \end{array} \right| = 0$

按第四列展开有: $1\left| \begin{array}{ccc}1 & a & 1\\ 1 & 2 & a\\ a & b & 0 \end{array} \right| - 2\left| \begin{array}{ccc}a & 0 & 1\\ 1 & a & 1\\ 1 & 2 & a \end{array} \right| = 0$ ，所以 $\left| \begin{array}{ccc}1 & a & 1\\ 1 & 2 & a\\ a & b & 0 \end{array} \right| = 8.$
