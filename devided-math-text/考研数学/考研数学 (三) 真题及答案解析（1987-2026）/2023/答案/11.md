答案: $\frac{2}{3}$
解析:$\lim_{x\to \infty}x^{2}\left(2 - x\sin \frac{1}{x} -\cos \frac{1}{x}\right) = \lim_{x\to \infty}\frac{2 - x\sin\frac{1}{x} - \cos\frac{1}{x}}{\frac{1}{x^2}} = \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{1}{t^2}\left(2 - \frac{1}{t}\sin t - \cos t\right)$ $= \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{t - \sin t + t(1 - \cos t)}{t^3}\\ = \lim_{t\to 0}\frac{t - \sin t}{t^3} +\lim_{t\to 0}\frac{1 - \cos t}{t^2}\\ = \frac{2}{3}$
