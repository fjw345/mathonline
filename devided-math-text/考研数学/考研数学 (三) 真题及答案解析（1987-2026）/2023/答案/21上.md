解析:（1）因为 $A\left( \begin{array}{c}x_{1}\\ x_{2}\\ x_{3} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{cc}x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3}\\ 2x_{1} - x_{2} + x_{3}\\ x_{2} - x_{3} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 1 & 1\\ 2 & -1 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{array} \right)\left( \begin{array}{c}x_{1}\\ x_{2}\\ x_{3} \end{array} \right)$ 对任意的 $x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}$ 均成立，所以 $A = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 1 & 1\\ 2 & -1 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & -1 \end{array} \right)$ （2） $\left|\lambda E - A\right| = \left| \begin{array}{ccc}\lambda -1 & -1 & -1\\ -2 & \lambda +1 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & \lambda +1 \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{ccc}\lambda -1 & -1 & 0\\ -2 & \lambda & -2 - \lambda \\ 0 & -1 & 2 + \lambda \end{array} \right| = \left| \begin{array}{ccc}\lambda -1 & -1 & 0\\ -2 & \lambda & 0\\ 0 & -1 & 2 + \lambda \end{array} \right|$ $= (2 + \lambda)(\lambda^2 -\lambda -2) = (\lambda +2)(\lambda -2)(\lambda -1) = 0$ 所以 $A$ 的特征值为 $\lambda_1 = -2$ ， $\lambda_{2} = 2$ ， $\lambda_3 = -1$ 。$\lambda_{1} = -2$ 时， $\lambda_1E - A = \left( \begin{array}{ccc} - 3 & -1 & -1\\ -2 & -1 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & -1 \end{array} \right)\to \left( \begin{array}{ccc}1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 1 & 1\\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right),$ 得特征向量的基础解系为 $\alpha_{1} = (0, - 1,1)^{T}$
