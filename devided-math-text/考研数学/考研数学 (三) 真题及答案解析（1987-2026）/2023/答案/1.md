答案:（A）

解析:由偏导数定义:

$$
\left. \frac {\partial f}{\partial x} \right| _ {(0, 1)} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {f (x , 1) - f (0 , 1)}{x - 0} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\ln (1 + | x \sin 1 |) - 0}{x - 0} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {| x \sin 1 |}{x} = \sin 1 \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {| x |}{x},
$$

不存在.

$\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\Big|_{(0,1)} = \lim_{y\to 1}\frac{f(0,y) - f(0,1)}{y - 1} = \lim_{y\to 1}\frac{\ln y - 0}{y - 1} = \lim_{y\to 1}\frac{y - 1}{y - 1} = 1$ ，存在，选（A）.
