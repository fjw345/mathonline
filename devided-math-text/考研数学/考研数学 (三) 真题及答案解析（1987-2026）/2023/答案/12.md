答案: $\frac{\pi}{3}$

解析:由题意: $df(x,y) = \frac{xdy - ydx}{x^2 + y^2},f(1,1) = \frac{\pi}{4}$ 知:

$$
\frac {\partial f}{\partial x} = \frac {- y}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}, \frac {\partial f}{\partial y} = \frac {x}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}
$$

对 $\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = \frac{x}{x^2 + y^2}$ 两端对 $y$ 积分可得:

$f(x,y) = \arctan \frac{y}{x} +\varphi (x)$ ，则有

$$
\frac {\partial f}{\partial x} = \frac {- \frac {y}{x ^ {2}}}{1 + \frac {y ^ {2}}{x ^ {2}}} + \varphi^ {\prime} (x) = \frac {- y}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} + \varphi^ {\prime} (x) = \frac {- y}{x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}
$$

$$
\varphi^ {\prime} (x) = 0, \quad \varphi (x) = C
$$

7

$f(x,y) = \arctan \frac{y}{x} +C$

$f(1,1) = \frac{\pi}{4}$

所以， $f(x,y) = \arctan{\frac{y}{x}} + C$ 又 $f(1,1) = \frac{\pi}{4}$，解得 $C = 0$，从而 $f(x,y) = \arctan{\frac{y}{x}}$，$f(\sqrt{3},3) = \arctan{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\pi}{3}$.

$f\left(\sqrt{3},3\right) = \arctan \sqrt{3} = \frac{\pi}{3}$
