解析:由 $X \sim P(1)$ ，知: $P(X = k) = \frac{\lambda^{k}}{k!} e^{-\lambda} = \frac{e^{-1}}{k!} (k = 0, 1, 2, \cdots)$ ，EX = 1 则

$$
\begin{array}{r l} E \left(\left| X - E X \right|\right) & = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {+ \infty} \left| x _ {k} - E X \right| P _ {k} = \left| 0 - 1 \right| \cdot e ^ {- 1} + \left| 1 - 1 \right| \cdot e ^ {- 1} + \left| 2 - 1 \right| \cdot \frac {e ^ {- 1}}{2 !} + \dots + (k - 1) \frac {1}{k !} e ^ {- 1} + \dots \\ & = e ^ {- 1} + e ^ {- 1} \sum_ {k = 2} ^ {\infty} (k - 1) \cdot \frac {1}{k !} \\ & = e ^ {- 1} + e ^ {- 1} \sum_ {k = 2} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{(k - 1) !} - e ^ {- 1} \sum_ {k = 2} ^ {\infty} \frac {1}{k !} \\ & = e ^ {- 1} + e ^ {- 1} (e - 1) - e ^ {- 1} (e - 1 - 1) \\ & = \frac {2}{e} \end{array}
$$

选（C）.
