解析:将平面区域划分成 $D_{1} = \{(x,y)\mid x^{2} + y^{2}\leq 1,(x - 1)^{2} + y^{2}\leq 1\}$

$$
D _ {2} = \left\{(x, y) \mid x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} > 1, (x - 1) ^ {2} + y ^ {2} \leq 1 \right\}
$$

其中

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\iint_ {D _ {1}} \left(1 - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}\right) d x d y = 2 \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{3}} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {1} (1 - r) r d r + 2 \int_ {\frac {\pi}{3}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {2 \cos \theta} (1 - r) r d r} \\ & {= \frac {1}{9} \pi + 2 \int_ {\frac {\pi}{3}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} (2 \cos^ {2} \theta - \frac {8}{3} \cos^ {3} \theta) d \theta} \\ & {= \frac {4}{9} \pi + \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {3 2}{9}} \\ & {\iint_ {D _ {2}} \left(\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} - 1\right) d x d y = \iint_ {D} \left(\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} - 1\right) d x d y - \iint_ {D _ {1}} \left(\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} - 1\right) d x d y} \\ & {\qquad = \int_ {- \frac {\pi}{2}} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} d \theta \int_ {0} ^ {2 \cos \theta} (r - 1) r d r + \iint_ {D _ {1}} \left(1 - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}\right) d x d y} \\ & {\qquad = - \frac {5}{9} \pi + \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2}} \\ & {\text {原式} = \iint_ {D _ {1}} \left(1 - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}\right) d x d y + \iint_ {D _ {2}} \left(\sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}} - 1\right) d x d y} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {4}{9} \pi + \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {3 2}{9} - \frac {5}{9} \pi + \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2} = 3 \sqrt {3} - \frac {\pi}{9} - \frac {3 2}{9}.} \end{array}
