解析:由拉普拉斯行列式值: $\begin{vmatrix} A & E \\ O & B \end{vmatrix} = |A| \cdot |B|$ .

故

$$
\left( \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right) ^ {*} = \left( \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right) ^ {- 1} \left| \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right|
$$

$$
\left[\begin{array}{c c c c}A&E&E&O\\O&B&O&E\end{array}\right]\rightarrow \left[\begin{array}{c c c c}A&E&E&O\\O&E&O&B ^ {- 1}\end{array}\right]\rightarrow \left[\begin{array}{c c c c}A&O&E&- B ^ {- 1}\\O&E&O&B ^ {- 1}\end{array}\right]\rightarrow \left[\begin{array}{c c c c}E&O&A ^ {- 1}&- A ^ {- 1} B ^ {- 1}\\O&E&O&B ^ {- 1}\end{array}\right]
$$

即

$$
\left( \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right) ^ {- 1} = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} A ^ {- 1} & - A ^ {- 1} B ^ {- 1} \\ O & B ^ {- 1} \end{array} \right]
$$

则

$$
\left( \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right) ^ {*} = \left( \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right) ^ {- 1} \left| \begin{array}{c c} A & E \\ O & B \end{array} \right| = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} A ^ {- 1} & - A ^ {- 1} B ^ {- 1} \\ O & B ^ {- 1} \end{array} \right] \cdot | A | \cdot | B | = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} | B | A ^ {*} & - A ^ {*} B ^ {*} \\ O & | A | B ^ {*} \end{array} \right]
$$
