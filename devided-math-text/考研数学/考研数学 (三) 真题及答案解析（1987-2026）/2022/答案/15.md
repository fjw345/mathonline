【解析】由题可得 $E_{23}AE_{12}(-1) = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 1 & -1\\ 1 & -1 & 0\\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$
方法1: $A = E_{23}\left( \begin{array}{cc} - 2 & 1\\ 1 & -1\\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right)E_{12}(1) = \left( \begin{array}{ccc} - 1 & 1 & -1\\ -1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & -1 & 0 \end{array} \right).$
$\left|\lambda E - A\right| = \left| \begin{array}{ccc} \lambda +1 & -1 & 1\\ 1 & \lambda & 0\\ 0 & 1 & \lambda \end{array} \right| = (\lambda +1)(\lambda^2 +1) = 0$ ，得 $\lambda_1 = -1$ ， $\lambda_{2} = i$ ， $\lambda_3 = -i$
所以 $A^{-1}$ 的特征值为 $\lambda_1 = -1$ ， $\lambda_{2} = -i$ ， $\lambda_3 = i$ ，从而 $tr(A^{-1}) = -1$
方法2: $[E_{23}AE_{12}(-1)]^{-1} = \left( \begin{array}{ccc} - 2 & 1 & -1\\ 1 & -1 & 0\\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right)^{-1} = E_{12}^{-1}(-1)A^{-1}E_{23}^{-1},$
则 $A^{-1} = E_{12}(-1)\left( \begin{array}{ccc} - 2 & 1 & -1\\ 1 & -1 & 0\\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right)^{-1}E_{23} = E_{12}(-1)\left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & 0 & -1\\ 0 & -1 & -1\\ -1 & -1 & 1 \end{array} \right)E_{23} = \left( \begin{array}{ccc}0 & -1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & -1\\ -1 & 1 & -1 \end{array} \right),$
所以 $tr(A^{-1}) = -1$
