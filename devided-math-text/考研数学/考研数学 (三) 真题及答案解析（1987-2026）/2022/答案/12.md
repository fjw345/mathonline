【答案】 $\ln 3 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\pi$

【解析】

$$
\begin{array}{l} \int_ {0} ^ {2} \frac {2 x - 4}{x ^ {2} + 2 x + 4} d x = \int_ {0} ^ {2} \frac {2 x + 2}{x ^ {2} + 2 x + 4} d x - 6 \int_ {0} ^ {2} \frac {1}{x ^ {2} + 2 x + 4} d x = \int_ {0} ^ {2} \frac {d (x ^ {2} + 2 x + 4)}{x ^ {2} + 2 x + 4} - 6 \int_ {0} ^ {2} \frac {1}{3 + (x + 1) ^ {2}} d x \\ = \ln (x ^ {2} + 2 x + 4) \Big | _ {0} ^ {2} - 2 \sqrt {3} \int_ {0} ^ {2} \frac {1}{1 + (\frac {x + 1}{\sqrt {3}}) ^ {2}} d (\frac {x + 1}{\sqrt {3}}) \\ = \ln 3 - 2 \sqrt {3} (\arctan \frac {3}{\sqrt {3}} - \arctan \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}) \\ = \ln 3 - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \pi . \end{array}
$$
