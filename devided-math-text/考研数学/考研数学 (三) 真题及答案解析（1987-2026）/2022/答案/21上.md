【解析】(1) 令 $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 4 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

$$
\left| \lambda E - A \right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda - 3 & 0 & - 1 \\ 0 & \lambda - 4 & 0 \\ - 1 & 0 & \lambda - 3 \end{array} \right| = (\lambda - 4) ^ {2} (\lambda - 2) = 0,
$$

得 A 的特征值为 $\lambda_{1} = \lambda_{2} = 4$ ， $\lambda_{3} = 2$ .

当 $\lambda = 4$ 时，$(4E - A)x = 0$ 的基础解析为 $\alpha_{1} = (0,1,0)^{T}$，$\alpha_{2} = (1,0,1)^{T}$；
