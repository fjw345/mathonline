【解析】 $I = \iint_{D} \frac{(x - y)^{2}}{x^{2} + y^{2}} dx dy$

$= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} d\theta \int_{0}^{2} \frac{(r \cos \theta - r \sin \theta)^{2} r}{r^{2}} dr + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} d\theta \int_{0}^{\frac{2}{\sin \theta - \cos \theta}} \frac{(r \cos \theta - r \sin \theta)^{2} r}{r^{2}} dr$

$= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 2(\cos \theta - \sin \theta)^{2} d\theta + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} 2d\theta$

$= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 2 - 4 \sin \theta \cos \theta d\theta + \pi = 2\pi - 2$
