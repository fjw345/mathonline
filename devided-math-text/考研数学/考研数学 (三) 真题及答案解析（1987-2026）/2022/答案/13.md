【解析】方法1: $f'(x)=e^{\sin x}\cos x-\cos xe^{-\sin x}$ ,

$$
f ^ {\prime \prime} (x) = e ^ {\sin x} \left(\cos^ {2} x - \sin x\right) + e ^ {- \sin x} \left(\cos^ {2} x + \sin x\right).
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} f ^ {\prime \prime \prime} (x) & = e ^ {\sin x} (- 2 \cos x \sin x - \cos x) + \cos x e ^ {\sin x} (\cos^ {2} x - \sin x) \\ & - \cos x e ^ {- \sin x} (\cos^ {2} x + \sin x) + e ^ {- \sin x} (- 2 \cos x \sin x + \cos x). \end{array}
$$

则 $f^{\prime \prime}(2\pi) = 1 - 1 - 1 + 1 = 0$

方法2:令 $f(x) = e^{\sin x} + e^{-\sin x}$，则 $f(-x) = e^{-\sin x} + e^{\sin x} = f(x)$，可知 $f(x)$ 为偶函数且 $T = 2\pi$。

于是 $f^{\prime}(x)$ 为奇函数，$f''(x)$ 为偶函数，$f'''(x)$ 为奇函数，从而 $f'''(0) = 0$ ，故 $f'''(2\pi) = f'''(0) = 0.$
