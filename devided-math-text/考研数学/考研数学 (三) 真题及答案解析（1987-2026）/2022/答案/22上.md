【解析】由题知， $X$ 的概率密度为 $f_{X}(x;\theta) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{1}{\theta} e^{-\frac{x}{\theta}}, & x > 0,\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$
$Y$ 的概率密度为 $f_{Y}(y;\theta) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{1}{2\theta} e^{-\frac{y}{2\theta}}, & y > 0,\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{array} \right.$
令 $L = \prod_{i = 1}^{n}f_{X}(x_{i};\theta)\prod_{j = 1}^{m}f_{Y}(y_{j};\theta) = \prod_{i = 1}^{n}\frac{1}{\theta} e^{-\frac{x_i}{\theta}}\prod_{j = 1}^{m}\frac{1}{2\theta} e^{-\frac{y_j}{2\theta}}(x_i > 0,y_j > 0),$ 则
$\ln L = \sum_{i = 1}^{n}(-\ln \theta -\frac{x_i}{\theta}) + \sum_{j = 1}^{m}(\ln \frac{1}{2} -\ln \theta -\frac{y_j}{2\theta})$
