【解析】由 $(\alpha_{1}+k\alpha_{3},\alpha_{2}+l\alpha_{3})=(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3})\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\k&l\end{pmatrix}$ 知，
 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 线性无关时，因为 $\begin{vmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{vmatrix}\neq0$
所以 $\alpha_{1} + k\alpha_{3}, \alpha_{2} + l\alpha_{3}$ 线性无关反之不成立.如当 $\alpha_{3}=0$ ，且 $\alpha_{1}$ 与 $\alpha_{2}$ 线性无关时， $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 线性相关
【答案】A
