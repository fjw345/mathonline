【解析】 $S = \frac{X_1 - X_2}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{X_3^2}}$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \left| X _ {1} - X _ {2} \sim N \big (0, 2 \sigma^ {2} \big) \right| \\ \frac {X _ {1} - X _ {2}}{\sqrt {2} \sigma} \sim N (0, 1) \\ X _ {3} \sim N (0, \sigma^ {2}) \end{array}
$$

$$
\therefore \frac {X _ {3}}{\sigma} \sim N (0, 1) \quad \therefore \left(\frac {X _ {3}}{\sigma}\right) ^ {2} \sim \chi^ {2} (1)
$$

$\frac{X_1 - X_2}{\sqrt{2}\sigma}$ 与 $\frac{X_3}{\sigma}$ 相互独立.

$$
\frac {\frac {X _ {1} - X _ {2}}{\sqrt {2} \sigma}}{\sqrt {\frac {X _ {3} ^ {2}}{\sigma^ {2}}}} = \frac {X _ {1} - X _ {2}}{\sqrt {2} \sqrt {X _ {3} ^ {2}}} \sim t (1)
$$
