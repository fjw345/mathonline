【解】

$$
X \sim \left( \begin{array}{c c} 0 & 1 \\ \frac {1}{3} & \frac {2}{3} \end{array} \right), Y \sim \left( \begin{array}{c c} 0 & 1 \\ \frac {1}{3} & \frac {2}{3} \end{array} \right)
$$

(1) $\rho_{XY} = \frac{EXY - EX \cdot EY}{\sqrt{DX} \sqrt{DY}} = \frac{EXY - \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{2}{3}}{\frac{2}{9}} = \frac{1}{2}$

$\Rightarrow EXY = \frac{5}{9}$. 而 $EXY = 1 \times p\{X = 1, Y = 1\}$

| X\\Y | 0 | 1 | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 2/9 | 1/9 | $\frac{1}{3}$ |
| 1 | 1/9 | 5/9 | $\frac{2}{3}$ |
| | 1/3 | 2/3 | |

(Ⅱ) $P\{X + Y\leq 1\} = 1 - P\{X + Y > 1\}$ $= 1 - P\{X = 1,Y = 1\}$ $= 1 - 5 / 9 = 4 / 9$
