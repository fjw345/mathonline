【解】

$$
F _ {Y} (y) = P \{Y \leq y \} = P \{X = 1 \} P \{Y \leq y \mid X = 1 \} + P \{X = 2 \} P \{Y \leq y \mid X = 2 \}
$$

$$
= \frac {1}{2} P \{Y \leq y \mid X = 1 \} + \frac {1}{2} P \{Y \leq y \mid X = 2 \},
$$

$y < 0$ 时， $F_{Y}(y) = 0$

当 $0 \leq y < 1$ 时，$F_{Y}(y) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{y}{1} + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{y}{2} = \frac{3y}{4}$;

当 $1 \leq y < 2$ 时，

$$
F _ {Y} (y) = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \frac {y}{2} = \frac {y}{4} + \frac {1}{2};
$$

当 $y \geq 2$ 时， $F_{y}(y) = 1$ ，

故 $F_{y}(y)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&0,y<0\\&\frac{3y}{4},0\leq y<1\\&\frac{y}{4}+\frac{1}{2},1\leq y<2\\&1,y\geq2\end{aligned}\right.$ 。

$$
(\text {II}) \quad f _ {Y} (y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {3}{4}, 0 < y < 1 \\ \frac {1}{4}, 1 < y < 2, \\ 0, \text {其他} \end{array} \right.
$$
