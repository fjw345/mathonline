【解】

由对称性得 $I = \iint_{D} \frac{x \sin(\pi \sqrt{x^2 + y^2})}{x + y} \, dx \, dy = \iint_{D} \frac{y \sin(\pi \sqrt{x^2 + y^2})}{x + y} \, dx \, dy$，于是 $I = \frac{1}{2} \left[ \iint_{D} \frac{x \sin(\pi \sqrt{x^2 + y^2})}{x + y} \, dx \, dy + \iint_{D} \frac{y \sin(\pi \sqrt{x^2 + y^2})}{x + y} \, dx \, dy \right]$

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \frac {1}{2} \iint_ {D} \sin (\pi \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}) d x d y = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} d \theta \int_ {1} ^ {2} r \sin \pi r d r = \frac {1}{4 \pi} \int_ {1} ^ {2} \pi r \sin \pi r d (\pi r) \\ = \frac {1}{4 \pi} \int_ {\pi} ^ {2 \pi} t \sin t d t = - \frac {1}{4 \pi} \int_ {\pi} ^ {2 \pi} t d (\cos t) = - \frac {1}{4 \pi} t \cos t | _ {\pi} ^ {2 \pi} + \frac {1}{4 \pi} \int_ {\pi} ^ {2 \pi} \cos t d t = - \frac {3}{4}. \end{array}
$$
