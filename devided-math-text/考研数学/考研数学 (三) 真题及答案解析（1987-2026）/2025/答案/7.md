$f(x,y) = -a\left(x^{2} - y^{2}\right) - \left(2xy - 4x^{2}\right) = (4 - a)x^{2} + ay^{2} - 2xy = (x,y)\left( \begin{array}{cc}4 - a & -1\\ -1 & a \end{array} \right)\binom{x}{y}$
