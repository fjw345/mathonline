【答案】 $\frac{1}{3}$
【解析】$P(A\cup B\cup C) = P(A) + P(B) + P(C) - P(AB) - P(BC)$ $= p(A) + p(B) + p(c) - p(A)p(B) - p(D)p(c) = \frac{1}{4} +\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{4} -\frac{1}{8} -\frac{1}{8} = \frac{3}{4}$ $P(AB\overline{C}) + p(A\overline{B} C) + P(\tilde{A} BC) = P(AB) + p(BC)$ $= P(A)P(B) + P(B)P(C) = \frac{1}{4}$ $P = \frac{\frac{1}{4}}{\frac{3}{4}} = \frac{1}{3}$
