【解析】

$F_{A}(x)$ 为样本中 $\{x_i\leqslant x\}$ 发的概率。

$$
I _ {i} (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1 & x _ {i} \leq x \\ 0 & x _ {i} > x \end{array} . Z _ {i} (x) \sim B [ 1. F (x) ] \right.
$$

$$
F _ {n} (x) = \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} I _ {i} (x) D \left[ F _ {n} (x) \right] = D \left[ \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} Z _ {i} (x) \right] = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} n D Z _ {i} (x) \cdot \frac {1}{n} F (x) [ 1 - F (x) ]
$$
