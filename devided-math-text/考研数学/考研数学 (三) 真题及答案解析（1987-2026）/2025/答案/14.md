【答案】 $\frac{1}{8}\mathrm{e}^{-2}$ 【解析】 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}|_{(1,1,1)} = \frac{\int_y^x\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t + xe^{-x^2}}{1 + \frac{1}{z}} |_{(1,1,1)} = \frac{\mathrm{e}^{-1}}{2},$ $\frac{\partial^2z}{\partial^2x}|_{(1,1,1)} = \frac{\left[2\mathrm{e}^{-x^2} + xe^{-x^2}(-2x)\right](1 + \frac{1}{z}) + \frac{1}{z^2}(\int_y^x\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t + xe^{-x^2})\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}|_{(1,1,1)}}{(1 + \frac{1}{z})^2}.$ $= \frac{\mathrm{e}^{-1}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{e}^{-1}}{2}}{4} = \frac{\mathrm{e}^{-2}}{8}.$
