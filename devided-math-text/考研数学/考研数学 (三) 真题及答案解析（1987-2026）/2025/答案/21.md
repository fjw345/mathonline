21. 解:

（1）因为 $r(A) = 2, A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 3 & 0 & -1 \\ -1 & 0 & -2 & -a & -1 \\ 1 & 1 & a & 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 3 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & -a & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & a - 1 & -2a + 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$，故 $a = 1$ 时.
（2）由（1）可知，$A \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 3 & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & -1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$，令 $A = (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4, \alpha_5)$，则 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ 为 $A$ 的列向量组的一个极大线性无关组，$\alpha = \alpha_1, \beta = \alpha_2$ 且 $\alpha_3 = 2\alpha_1 - \alpha_2, \alpha_4 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_2$，$\alpha_5 = \alpha_1 + 2\alpha_2$，由 $A = GH$，解得 $H = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$.
