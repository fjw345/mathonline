8.【答案】D

【解析】

$$
\operatorname{Cov} (X, X + 2 Y) = \operatorname{Cov} (X, X) + 2 \operatorname{Cov} (X, Y) = 0
$$

$$
= D X + 2 \operatorname{Cov} (X, Y) = 0
$$

$$
\mathrm{Cov} (X, Y) = - \frac {1}{2} D X = - \frac {1}{2}
$$

$$
\mathrm{Cov} (X, X - Y) = \mathrm{Cov} (X, X) - \mathrm{Cov} (X, Y) = D X - \mathrm{Cov} (X, Y) = 1 + \frac {1}{2} = \frac {3}{2}
$$

$$
D (X - Y) = D X + D Y - 2 \operatorname{Cov} (X, Y) = 1 + 2 - 2 \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) = 4
$$

$$
D X = 1 \quad \therefore \rho_ {X, X - Y} = \frac {\frac {3}{2}}{2 \times 1} = \frac {3}{4}
$$
