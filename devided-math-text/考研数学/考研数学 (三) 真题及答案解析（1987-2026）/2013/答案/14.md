答案: $2\mathrm{e}^2$
解析:
标准正态分布的概率密度 $\mathrm{f}(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}$
$E\left(Xe^{2X}\right) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xe^{2x}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}dx = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}xe^{-\frac{1}{2} (x - 2)^2 -2}dx = e^2\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2} (x - 2)^2}dx = 2e^2$
