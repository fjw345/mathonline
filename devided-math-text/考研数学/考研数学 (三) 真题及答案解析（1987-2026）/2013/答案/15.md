解析:

$$
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {1 - \cos x \cos 2 x \cos 3 x}{a x ^ {n}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {1 - \frac {\cos 6 x + \cos 4 x + \cos 2 x + 1}{4}}{a x ^ {n}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {3 - \cos 6 x - \cos 4 x - \cos 2 x}{4 a x ^ {n}} \\ = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {6 \sin 6 x + 4 \sin 4 x + 2 \sin 2 x}{4 a n x ^ {n - 1}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {3 6 \cos 6 x + 1 6 \cos 4 x + 4 \cos 2 x}{4 a n (n - 1) x ^ {n - 2}} \\ \text {所以} n = 2 \\ \text {当} n = 2 \text {时，由题意} \frac {3 6 + 1 6 + 4}{4 a \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = 1 \Rightarrow a = 7 \\ \text {所以} n = 2, a = 7 \end{array}
$$
