答案:(B)

解析: $\lim_{x\to 1}\frac{|x|^x - 1}{x(x + 1)\ln|x} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{e^{x\ln|x}}{x(x + 1)\ln|x} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x\ln|x|}{x(x + 1)\ln|x}| = 1.$

$$
\varliminf_ {x \to 1} f (x) = \varliminf_ {x \to 1} \frac {x \ln | x |}{x (x + 1) \ln | x |} = \frac {1}{2}
$$

$$
\lim _ {x \to - 1} f (x) = \lim _ {x \to - 1} \frac {x \ln | x |}{x (x + 1) \ln | x |} = \infty
$$

而 $f(0), f(1)$ 无定义，故 $x = 0, x = 1$ 为可去间断点.
