且 $R(A) = R(B)$

$$
A \rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c}1&a&1\\0&b - a ^ {2}&0\\0&0&0\end{array}\right) \quad B = \left(\begin{array}{c c c}2&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{array}\right)
$$
