解析:令 $C = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \\ x_3 & x_4 \end{pmatrix}$，则

$$
A C = \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 & a \\ 1 & 0 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c} x _ {1} & x _ {2} \\ x _ {3} & x _ {4} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c} x _ {1} + a x _ {3} & x _ {2} + a x _ {4} \\ x _ {1} & x _ {2} \end{array} \right)
$$

$$
C A = \left( \begin{array}{c c} x _ {1} & x _ {2} \\ x _ {3} & x _ {4} \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 & a \\ 1 & 0 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c} x _ {1} + x _ {2} & a x _ {1} \\ x _ {3} + x _ {4} & a x _ {3} \end{array} \right)
$$

$$
A C - C A = \left( \begin{array}{c c} - x _ {2} + a x _ {3} & - a x _ {1} + x _ {2} + a x _ {4} \\ x _ {1} - x _ {3} - x _ {4} & x _ {2} - a x _ {3} \end{array} \right),
$$

则由 $AC - CA = B$ 得

$\left\{ \begin{array}{l}-x_{2} + ax_{3} = 0\\ -ax_{1} + x_{2} + ax_{4} = 1\\ x_{1} - x_{3} - x_{4} = 1\\ x_{2} - ax_{3} = b \end{array} \right.$ ，此为4元非齐次线性方程组，欲使 $C$ 存在，此线性方程组必须有解，于是

$$
\overline {{A}} = \left(\begin{array}{c c c c c}0&- 1&a&0&0\\- a&1&0&a&1\\1&0&- 1&- 1&1\\0&1&- a&0&b\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c c}0&- 1&a&0&0\\0&1&- a&0&1 + a\\1&0&- 1&- 1&1\\0&1&- a&0&b\end{array}\right)\rightarrow \left(\begin{array}{c c c c c}1&0&- 1&- 1&1\\0&1&- a&0&0\\0&1&- a&0&1 + a\\0&1&- a&0&b\end{array}\right)
$$
