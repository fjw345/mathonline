解析:由题意可得:

$$
V _ {x} = \pi \int_ {0} ^ {a} (x ^ {\frac {1}{2}}) ^ {2} d x = \frac {3}{5} \pi a ^ {\frac {5}{3}}
$$

$$
V _ {y} = 2 \pi \int_ {0} ^ {a} x \cdot x ^ {\frac {1}{3}} d x = \frac {6 \pi}{7} a ^ {\frac {7}{3}}
$$

因为: $V_{y} = 10V_{x}$ 所以 $\frac{6\pi}{7} a^{\frac{7}{3}} = 10\cdot \frac{3}{5}\pi a^{\frac{5}{3}}\Rightarrow a = 7\sqrt{7}$
