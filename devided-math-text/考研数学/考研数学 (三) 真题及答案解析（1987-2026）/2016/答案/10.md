【答案】: $-\cos 1 + \sin 1$

$$
\lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \left(\sin \frac {1}{n} + 2 \sin \frac {2}{n} + \dots + n \sin \frac {n}{n}\right) = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} i \sin \frac {i}{n} = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \frac {i}{n} \sin \frac {i}{n}
$$

$$
= \int_ {0} ^ {1} x \sin x d x = - \int_ {0} ^ {1} x d \cos x = - x \cos x \left| \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 0 \end{array} + \int_ {0} ^ {1} \cos x d x = - \cos 1 + \sin 1 \right.
$$
