【解析】由重要极限得，原式为

$$
e ^ {\lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\cos 2 x + 2 x \sin x - 1}{x ^ {4}}} = e ^ {\lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {1 - \frac {1}{2} (2 x) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2 4} (2 x) ^ {4} + 2 x \left(x - \frac {1}{6} x ^ {3}\right) - 1 + o (x ^ {4})}{x ^ {4}}} = e ^ {\lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\frac {1}{3} x ^ {4} + o (x ^ {4})}{x ^ {4}}} = e ^ {\frac {1}{3}}
$$
