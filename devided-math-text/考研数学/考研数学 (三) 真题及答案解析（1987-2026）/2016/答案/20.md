(2) $A^{T}A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & 2\\ 2 & 2 & 2\\ 2 & 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix},\quad A^{T}\beta = \begin{pmatrix} -1\\ -2\\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$
$\left(A^TA\vdots A^TA\beta\right) = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 & 2\vdots -1\\ 2 & 2 & 2\vdots -2\\ 2 & 2 & 2\vdots -2 \end{pmatrix}\rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0\vdots 1\\ 0 & 1 & 1\vdots -2\\ 0 & 0 & 0\vdots 0 \end{pmatrix}$ ，则通解为 $k\left( \begin{array}{c}0\\ -1\\ 1 \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}{c}1\\ -2\\ 0 \end{array} \right),k\in R$
