$\frac {\partial_ {z} ^ {2}}{\partial x \partial y} \Big | _ {(1, 1)} = f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} (2, 2) + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (2, 2) f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) \tag {$ 【答案】 $}$

【解析】解:

$\because f(1,1) = 2$ 是 $f(u,v)$ 的极值，故 $f_{1}^{\prime}(1,1) = 0,f_{2}^{\prime}(1,1) = 0.$

$$
\frac {\partial z}{\partial x} = f _ {1} ^ {\prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ] + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ] f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (x, y)
$$

$$
\frac {\partial^ {2} z}{\partial x \partial y} = f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ] + f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ] f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (x, y) + [ f _ {2 1} ^ {\prime \prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ]
$$

$$
+ f _ {2 2} ^ {\prime \prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ] f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (x, y) ] f _ {1} ^ {\prime} (x, y) + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} [ (x + y), f (x, y) ] f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y)
$$

$$
\therefore \frac {\partial_ {z} ^ {2}}{\partial x \partial y} \bigg | _ {(1, 1)} = f _ {1 1} ^ {\prime \prime} (2, 2) + f _ {2} ^ {\prime} (2, 2) f _ {1 2} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1)
$$
