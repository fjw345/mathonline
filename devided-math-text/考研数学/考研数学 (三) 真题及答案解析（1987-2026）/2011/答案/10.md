【答案】 $2(\ln 2 + \frac{1}{2})dx - 2(\ln 2 + \frac{1}{2})dy$ 【解析】 $\left.\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,1)} = \frac{d(1 + x)^x}{dx}\bigg|_{x = 1} = (e^{x\ln(1 + x)})^t\bigg|_{x = 1} = (e^{x\ln(1 + x)})\bigg|_{x = 1} + (\ln (1 + x) + \frac{x}{1 + x})\bigg|_{x = 1}$ $= 2(\ln 2 + \frac{1}{2})$

$$
\left. \frac {\partial z}{\partial y} \right| _ {(1, 1)} = \frac {d \left(1 + \frac {1}{y}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{y}}}{d y} \Big | _ {y = 1} = \left[ e ^ {\frac {\ln \left(1 + \frac {1}{y}\right)}{y}} \right] ^ {\prime} \Big | _ {y = 1} = e ^ {\frac {\ln \left(1 + \frac {1}{y}\right)}{y}} \left[ \begin{array}{c} y \cdot \frac {- 1}{y ^ {2}} \\ \frac {1 + \frac {1}{y}}{y} - \ln (1 + \frac {1}{y}) \\ \hline y ^ {2} \end{array} \right] _ {y = 1} = 2 \cdot (\frac {- 1}{2} - \ln 2)
$$
