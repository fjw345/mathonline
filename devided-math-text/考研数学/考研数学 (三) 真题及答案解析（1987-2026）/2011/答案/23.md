① $f_{x}(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned} & x,0<x\leq\\ & 2-x,1<x\leq2\\ & 0,\text{其它}\end{aligned}\right.$

② $f_{x|y}(x|y)=\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{2-2y},&y<x<2-y\\ 0,&\text{其它}\end{aligned}\right.$

【解析】解:

$f(x,y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l}\frac{1}{A},(x,y)\in G\\ 0,\text{其它} \end{array} \right. = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}1,(x,y)\in G\\ 0,\text{其它} \end{array} \right.,\text{其中} A\text{为} G\text{的面积}$

$$
f _ {X} (x) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} f (x, y) d y = \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \int_ {0} ^ {x} 1 d y = x, 0 < x \leq 1 \\ \int_ {0} ^ {2 - x} 1 d y = 2 - x, 1 < x \leq 2 \\ 0, \text {其它} \end{array} \right.
$$

② $f_{Y}(y) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x,y)dx = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\int_{-y}^{2 - y}1dx, & y < x < 2 - y\\ 0, & \text{其它} \end{array} \right.$

当 $0 < y < 1$ 时，$f_{r}(y) \neq 0$

$$
f _ {x \mathrm{P}} (x | y) = \frac {f (x , y)}{f _ {r} (y)} = \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \frac {1}{2 - 2 y}, y < x < 2 - y \\ 0, \text {其它} \end{array} \right.
$$
