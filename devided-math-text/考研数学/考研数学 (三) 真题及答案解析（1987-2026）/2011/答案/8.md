【解析】 $E(X) = \lambda ,D(X) = \lambda$

$$
E (T _ {1}) = E (\frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i}) = \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} = \lambda
$$

$$
E (T _ {2}) = E (\frac {1}{n - 1} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} + \frac {1}{n} X _ {n}) = \frac {1}{n - 1} (n - 1) \lambda + \frac {1}{n} \lambda = \lambda + \frac {1}{n} \lambda
$$

$$
E T _ {1} < E T _ {2}
$$

$$
D (T _ {1}) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} D (X _ {i}) = \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \cdot n \cdot \lambda = \frac {\lambda}{n}
$$

$$
D (T _ {2}) = \frac {1}{(n - 1) ^ {2}} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n - 1} D (X _ {i}) + \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} D (X _ {n})
$$

$$
= \frac {1}{(n - 1) ^ {2}} \cdot (n - 1) \cdot \lambda + \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} \lambda = \frac {\lambda}{n - 1} + \frac {\lambda}{n ^ {2}}
$$

$D T _ {2} > D T _ {1} \quad$选$\mathsf {D}$
