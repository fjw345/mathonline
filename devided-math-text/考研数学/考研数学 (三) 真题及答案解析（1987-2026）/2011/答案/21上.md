$$

$$

$$
\alpha_ {1} = \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \\ - 1 \end{array} \right), \alpha_ {2} = \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{array} \right)
$$
