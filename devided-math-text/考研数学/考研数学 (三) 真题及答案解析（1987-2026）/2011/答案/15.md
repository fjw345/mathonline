【答案】 $-\frac{1}{2}$

【解析】解:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\text {原式} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\sqrt {1 + 2 \sin x} - x - 1}{x ^ {2}} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\frac {2 \cos x}{2 \sqrt {1 + 2 \sin x}} - 1}{2 x} = \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\cos x - \sqrt {1 + 2 \sin x}}{2 x \sqrt {1 + 2 \sin x}}} \\ & {= \frac {1}{2} \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\cos x - 1 - [ \sqrt {1 + 2 \sin x} - 1 ]}{x} = \frac {1}{2} [ \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {- \frac {x ^ {2}}{2}}{x} - \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {\frac {1}{2} 2 \sin x}{x} ] = - \frac {1}{2}} \end{array}
$$
