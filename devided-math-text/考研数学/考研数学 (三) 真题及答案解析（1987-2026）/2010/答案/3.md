(3)【分析】利用二阶可导函数 $F(x)$ 在点 $x = x_0$ 处取得极大值的一个充分条件是 $F'(x_0) = 0$ 且 $F''(x_0) < 0$ 来解决本题.

设 $F(x) = f(g(x))$ ，由题设知 $F(x)$ 二阶可导，且

$$
\begin{array}{r l} F ^ {\prime} (x _ {0}) & = [ f (g (x)) ] ^ {\prime} | _ {x = x _ {0}} = f ^ {\prime} (g (x _ {0})) g ^ {\prime} (x _ {0}) = f ^ {\prime} (a) g ^ {\prime} (x _ {0}) = 0, \\ F ^ {\prime \prime} (x _ {0}) & = [ f (g (x)) ] ^ {\prime \prime} | _ {x = x _ {0}} = f ^ {\prime \prime} (g (x _ {0})) [ g ^ {\prime} (x _ {0}) ] ^ {2} + f ^ {\prime} (g (x _ {0})) g ^ {\prime \prime} (x _ {0}) \\ & = f ^ {\prime} (a) g ^ {\prime \prime} (x _ {0}), \end{array}
$$

因为 $g''(x_0) < 0$，即 $F''(x_0)$ 与 $f'(a)$ 反号，从而当 $f'(a) > 0$ 时就有 $F'(x_0) = 0$ 且 $F''(x_0) < 0$，即 $x = x_0$ 是 $F(x) = f(g(x))$ 的一个极大值点，故应选(B).
