(8)【分析】 易见 $f(x) \geqslant 0$，由于 $f_{1}(x)$ 是标准正态分布的概率密度，$f_{2}(x)$ 是 $[-1,3]$ 上均匀分布的概率密度，因此有

$$
f _ {1} (x) = \varphi (x) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {x ^ {2}}{2}}; f _ {2} (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {1}{4}, & - 1 \leqslant x \leqslant 3, \\ 0, & \text {其他}. \end{array} \right.
$$

因为 $f(x)$ 是概率密度函数，所以 $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x = 1$，即

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} f (x) \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {0} a f _ {1} (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} b f _ {2} (x) \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {0} \frac {a}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {x ^ {2}}{2}} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {3} \frac {b}{4} \mathrm{d} x \\ & = \frac {a}{2} + \frac {3}{4} b = 1, \end{array}
$$

$\Rightarrow 2a + 3b = 4$ .故应选(A).
