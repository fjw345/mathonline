(14)【分析】根据简单随机样本的性质, $X_{1},X_{2},\cdots,X_{n}$ 相互独立且与总体同分布,即 $X_{i}\sim N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$ ,于是 $EX_{i}=\mu,DX_{i}=\sigma^{2},EX_{i}^{2}=DX_{i}+(EX_{i})^{2}=\sigma^{2}+\mu^{2}$ .因此

$ET=E\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}^{2}\right)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}EX_{i}^{2}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(\sigma^{2}+\mu^{2}\right)=\sigma^{2}+\mu^{2}.$
