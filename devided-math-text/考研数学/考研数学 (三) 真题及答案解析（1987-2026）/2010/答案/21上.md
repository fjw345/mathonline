(21)【分析】因为 $Q$ 是正交矩阵 $\Leftrightarrow Q^{\mathrm{T}} = Q^{-1}$, 所以 $Q^{\mathrm{T}}AQ = A$, 即 $Q^{-1}AQ = A$. $A$ 的对角线上的元素是 $A$ 的特征值, $Q$ 是 $A$ 的特征向量.

【解】按已知条件，$(1,2,1)^{\mathrm{T}}$ 是矩阵 $A$ 的特征向量，设特征值是 $\lambda_{1}$，那么

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 0 & - 1 & 4 \\ - 1 & 3 & a \\ 4 & a & 0 \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{array} \right] = \lambda_ {1} \left[ \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{array} \right] \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 0 + (- 2) + 4 = \lambda_ {1}, \\ - 1 + 6 + a = 2 \lambda_ {1}, \\ 4 + 2 a + 0 = \lambda_ {1} \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \lambda_ {1} = 2, \\ a = - 1. \end{array} \right.
$$

由

$$
\mid \lambda E - A \mid = \left| \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda & 1 & - 4 \\ 1 & \lambda - 3 & 1 \\ - 4 & 1 & \lambda \end{array} \right| = (\lambda - 2) (\lambda - 5) (\lambda + 4),
$$

知矩阵 A 的特征值是: 2, 5, -4.

对 $\lambda = 5$ ，由 $(5E - A)x = 0$

$$
\left[\begin{array}{c c c}5&1&- 4\\1&2&1\\- 4&1&5\end{array}\right]\rightarrow \left[\begin{array}{c c c}1&2&1\\0&1&1\\0&0&0\end{array}\right],
$$

得基础解系 $\alpha_{2} = (1, -1, 1)^{\mathrm{T}}$.

对 $\lambda = -4$ ，由 $(-4E - A)x = 0,$

$$
\left[\begin{array}{r r r}{- 4}&{1}&{- 4}\\{1}&{- 7}&{1}\\{- 4}&{1}&{- 4}\end{array}\right]\rightarrow \left[\begin{array}{r r r}{1}&{- 7}&{1}\\{0}&{1}&{0}\\{0}&{0}&{0}\end{array}\right],
$$

得基础解系 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{3} = (-1,0,1)^{\mathrm{T}}$

因为 $A$ 是实对称矩阵，特征值不同特征向量相互正交，故只需把 $\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ 单位化，有

$$
\gamma_ {2} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {3}} (1, - 1, 1) ^ {\mathrm{T}}, \quad \gamma_ {3} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2}} (- 1, 0, 1).
$$
