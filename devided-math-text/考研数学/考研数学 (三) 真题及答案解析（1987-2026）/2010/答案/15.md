(15)【解】设 $f(x)=(x^{\frac{1}{x}}-1)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}$，则 $\ln f(x)=\frac{\ln(x^{\frac{1}{x}}-1)}{\ln x}$，且由洛必达法则可得

$J=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\ln(x^{\frac{1}{x}}-1)}{\ln x}=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{(x^{\frac{1}{x}})'}{\frac{1}{x}(x^{\frac{1}{x}}-1)}=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{x(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}})'}{\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}-1}$

$=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{x\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}}-\frac{\ln x}{x^{2}}\right)}{\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}-1}=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{1-\ln x}{x(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}-1)}$.

由于 $\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\ln x}{x}=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{1}{x}=0$，于是当 $x\to+\infty$ 时有 $\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}-1\sim\frac{\ln x}{x}$. 代入上式即得

$J=\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{1-\ln x}{\ln x}=-1$.

求所求极限 $\lim_{x\to+\infty}\left(x^{\frac{1}{x}}-1\right)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}=\mathrm{e}^{J}=\mathrm{e}^{-1}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}}$.
