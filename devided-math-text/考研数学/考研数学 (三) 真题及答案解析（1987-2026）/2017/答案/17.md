## 【详解】由定积分的定义

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \lim _ {n \to \infty} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \frac {k}{n ^ {2}} \ln \left(1 + \frac {k}{n}\right) & = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {1}{n} \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \frac {k}{n} \ln \left(1 + \frac {k}{n}\right) = \int_ {0} ^ {1} x \ln (1 + x) d x \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {1} \ln (1 + x) d x ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \end{array}
$$
