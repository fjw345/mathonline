【详解】 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=y(3-x-y)-xy=3y-2xy-y^{2},\quad\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=3x-x^{2}-2xy,\quad\leftarrow$
$\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x^{2}}=-2y,\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}=-2x,\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}=\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y\partial x}=3-2x\leftarrow$
解方程组 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}&=3y-2xy-y^{2}=0\\ \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}&=3x-x^{2}-2xy=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，得四个驻点.对每个驻点验证 $AC-B^{2}$ ，发现只有在点(1,1)处满足

$AC - B^{2} = 3 > 0$ ，且 $A = C = -2 < 0$ ，所以(1,1)为函数的极大值点，所以应该选（D）
