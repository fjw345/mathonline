【详解】令 $x - t = u$ ，则 $t = x - u, dt = -du$ ， $\int_0^x\sqrt{x - te^t} dt = \int_0^x\sqrt{ue^{x - u}} du\leftarrow$ $\lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\frac{\int_{0}^{x}\sqrt{x - te^{t}}dt}{\sqrt{x^{3}}} = \lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\frac{e^{x}\int_{0}^{x}\sqrt{ue^{-u}}du}{\sqrt{x^{3}}} = \lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\frac{\int_{0}^{x}\sqrt{ue^{-u}}du}{\sqrt{x^{3}}} = \lim_{x\to 0^{+}}\frac{\sqrt{x}e^{-x}}{\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{x}} = \frac{2}{3}\leftarrow$
