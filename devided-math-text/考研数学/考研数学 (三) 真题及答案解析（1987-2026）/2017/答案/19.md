【详解】（1）由条件 $a_{n + 1} = \frac{1}{n + 1}(na_n + a_{n - 1})\Rightarrow (n + 1)a_{n + 1} = na_n + a_{n - 1}\leftrightarrow$

也就得到 $(n+1)(a_{n+1}-a_{n})=-(a_{n}-a_{n-1})$ ，也就得到 $\frac{a_{n+1}-a_{n}}{a_{n}-a_{n-1}}=-\frac{1}{n+1},n=1,2,\cdots\leftarrow$

$$
\frac {a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n}}{a _ {1} - a _ {0}} = \frac {a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n}}{a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1}} \times \frac {a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1}}{a _ {n - 1} - a _ {n - 2}} \times \dots \times \frac {a _ {2} - a _ {1}}{a _ {1} - a _ {0}} = (- 1) ^ {n} \frac {1}{(n + 1) !}\left. \right) ^ {n}
$$

也就得到 $a_{n + 1} - a_n = (-1)^{n + 1}\frac{1}{(n + 1)!}, n = 1,2,\dots \leftrightarrow$

$$
a _ {n + 1} = (a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n}) + (a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1}) + \dots + (a _ {2} - a _ {1}) + a _ {1} = \sum_ {k = 2} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {k + 1} \frac {1}{k !} \leqslant
$$

$\rho = \lim_{n\to \infty}\sqrt[n]{\left|a_n\right|}\leq \lim_{n\to \infty}\sqrt[n]{\frac{1}{2!} +\frac{1}{3!} +\dots +\frac{1}{n!}}\leq \lim_{n\to \infty}\sqrt[n]{e} = 1$ ，所以收敛半径 $R\geq 1$

（2）所以对于幂级数 $\sum_{n = 0}^{\infty}a_nx^n$ ，由和函数的性质，可得 $S^{\prime}(x) = \sum_{n = 1}^{\infty}na_{n}x^{n - 1}$ ，所以

$$
\begin{array}{r l} (1 - x) S ^ {\prime} (x) & = (1 - x) \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} n a _ {n} x ^ {n - 1} = \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} n a _ {n} x ^ {n - 1} - \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} n a _ {n} x ^ {n} \\ & = \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} (n + 1) a _ {n + 1} x ^ {n} - \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} n a _ {n} x ^ {n} \\ & = a _ {1} + \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} ((n + 1) a _ {n + 1} - n a _ {n}) x ^ {n} \\ & = \sum_ {n = 1} ^ {\infty} a _ {n - 1} x ^ {n} = \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} a _ {n} x ^ {n + 1} = x \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} a _ {n} x ^ {n} = x S (x) \end{array}
$$

也就是有 $(1-x)S'(x)-xS(x)=0(x\in(-1,1))$ .

解微分方程 $(1-x)S'(x)-xS(x)=0$ ，得 $S(x)=\frac{Ce^{-x}}{1-x}$ ，由于 $S(0)=a_{0}=1$ ，得 $C=1\leftarrow$
所以 $S(x)=\frac{e^{-x}}{1-x}.\leftarrow$
