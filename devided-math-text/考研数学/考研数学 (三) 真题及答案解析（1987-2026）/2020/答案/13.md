【答案】 $a^2 (a^2 -4)$

【解析】

$$
\text {原式} = a \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & a & 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & 1 & a & 0 \\ 1 & - 1 & 0 & a \end{array} \right| = a \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & a & 1 & - 1 \\ 0 & 2 & a + 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & a & a \end{array} \right| = a \left| \begin{array}{c c c} a & 1 & - 1 \\ 2 & a + 1 & 1 \\ 0 & a & a \end{array} \right| = a ^ {2} \left| \begin{array}{c c c} a & 2 & - 1 \\ 2 & a & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array} \right| = a ^ {2} (a ^ {2} - 4).
$$
