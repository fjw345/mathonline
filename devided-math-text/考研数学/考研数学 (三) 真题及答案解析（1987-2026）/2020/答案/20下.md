$\pmb{B}$ 的属于特征值 $\lambda_{2} = 0$ 的特征向量为 $\pmb{\beta}_{2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\binom{1}{-2}$. 记 $Q_{1} = (\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 2\\ -2 & 1 \end{array} \right),\quad Q_{2} = (\beta_{1},\beta_{2}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\left( \begin{array}{cc}2 & 1\\ 1 & -2 \end{array} \right)$ ，就有$Q_{1}^{T}AQ_{1} = Q_{2}^{T}BQ_{2} = \left( \begin{array}{cc}5 & 0\\ 0 & 0 \end{array} \right),$

因此 $B = Q_{2}Q_{1}^{T}AQ_{1}Q_{2}^{T}$ ，只需令

$$
\boldsymbol {Q} = \boldsymbol {Q} _ {1} \boldsymbol {Q} _ {2} ^ {T} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}} \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 & 2 \\ - 2 & 1 \end{array} \right) \cdot \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}} \left( \begin{array}{c c} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & - 2 \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c} \frac {4}{5} & - \frac {3}{5} \\ - \frac {3}{5} & - \frac {4}{5} \end{array} \right),
$$

则 $B = Q^{T}AQ$ ，二次型 $f(x_{1}, x_{2})$ 经正交变换 x = Qy 化为 $g(y_{1}, y_{2})$ 。
