【解析】

$$
\lim _ {x \rightarrow a} \frac {\sin f (x) - \sin a}{x - a} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow a} \frac {\sin f (x) - \sin a}{f (x) - a} \cdot \frac {f (x) - a}{x - a} = \cos \left. f (x) \right| _ {x = a} \cdot b = b \cos f (a)
$$

设 $f(x) = u$ ，则 $\lim_{x\to a}\frac{\sin f(x) - \sin a}{f(x) - a} = \lim_{u\to f(a)}\frac{\sin u - \sin a}{u - a} = \cos u\Big|_{u = f(a)} = \cos f(a)$

则$\lim _ {x \rightarrow a} \frac {\sin f (x) - \sin a}{x - a} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow a} \frac {\sin f (x) - \sin a}{f (x) - a} \cdot \frac {f (x) - a}{x - a} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow a} \frac {\sin f (x) - \sin a}{f (x) - a} \cdot \lim _ {x \rightarrow a} \frac {f (x) - a}{x - a} = b \cos a$
