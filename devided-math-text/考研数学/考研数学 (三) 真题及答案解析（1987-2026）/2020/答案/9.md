【答案】 $dz\big|_{(0,\pi)}=(\pi-1)\mathrm{d}x-\mathrm{d}y$

【解析】 $\frac{\mathrm{dz}}{\mathrm{dx}} = \frac{y + \cos(x + y)}{1 + (xy + \sin(x + y))^2},\frac{\mathrm{dz}}{\mathrm{dy}} = \frac{x + \cos(x + y)}{1 + (xy + \sin(x + y))^2}$ ，将 $x = 0,y = \pi$ 带入可知，

$$
d z \big | _ {(0, \pi)} = (\pi - 1) \mathrm{d} x - \mathrm{d} y
$$
