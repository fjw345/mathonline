$\iint_{D}f(x,y)dxdy = A$

【解析】设 $\iint_{D}f(x,y)dxdy = A$ ，则 $f(x,y) = y\sqrt{1 - x^2} +Ax$

$f(x,y) = y\sqrt{1 - x^2} +Ax$

两边同取积分得 $A = \iint_{D} f(x, y) dx dy = \iint_{D} y\sqrt{1 - x^2} dx dy + \iint_{D} Axdxdy$
$= \iint_{D} y\sqrt{1 - x^2} dx dy = 2\iint_{D_1} y\sqrt{1 - x^2} dx dy = 2\int_0^1 \sqrt{1 - x^2} dx \int_0^{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} y dy$
$= \int_0^1 (1 - x^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} dx^{x = \sin t} = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^4 t dt = \frac{3}{4} \frac{1}{2} \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{3\pi}{16}$.
